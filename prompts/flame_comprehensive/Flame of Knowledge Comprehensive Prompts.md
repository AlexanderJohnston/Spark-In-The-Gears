## Ship Exterior

### Canon notes

- Played canon identifies the Flame as Xal Aran's sister ship and shows it as an artifact-class dreadnought parked beside the Skull of Xal, still and continent-sized beside the party's battleship (`Sessions/Session 8/Session 8 Log.md:207`; `Sessions/Session 13/Session 13 Log.md:1123`; `Sessions/Session 14/Session 14 Log.md:21`).

These prompts preserve both presentations without deciding its current location.

## The Web of Divinity

### Canon notes

- The Web is a divine Artifact connected to the bridge's temporal matrix. It examines branching possibilities and has affected time on a ten-thousand-year scale; its exact appearance and full operation remain unspecified (`Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:57`; `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:61`; `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:65`; `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:70`; `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:219`; `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:223`).
- Played canon says its user observed collapsing possible futures and isolated a rare survival branch. Nara proposes asking what would have happened if Kaelith activated the freighter device, while warning that direct exposure inevitably consumes sanity (`Sessions/Session 24/Session 24 Log.md:573`; `Sessions/Session 24/Session 24 Log.md:575`; `Sessions/Session 24/Session 24 Log.md:752`; `Sessions/Session 24/Session 24 Log.md:757`).
