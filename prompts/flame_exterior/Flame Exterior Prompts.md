# Flame of Knowledge — Exterior Prompts

## Canon and visualization notes

- The Flame of Knowledge is a derelict, fire-aspected Great Ship at least ten thousand years old; it served as an archive and strategic-observation vessel rather than a simple frontline warship (`Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:12`, `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:15`, `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:16`, `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:47`).
- The location guide places it amid the Vagrant Fleet wreckage near Kemet (`Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:14`, `Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:51`). Played campaign canon instead shows it parked beside the Skull of Xal, opposite the Word of Xal (`Sessions/Session 13/Session 13 Log.md:1123`, `Sessions/Session 14/Session 14 Log.md:21`). The prompts preserve both presentations without deciding whether the ship moved.
- Played canon establishes a scale like a floating continent and makes the party's battleship look borrowed beside it (`Sessions/Session 13/Session 13 Log.md:1123`, `Sessions/Session 14/Session 14 Log.md:21`).
- Canon does not specify exact exterior geometry (`Places/The Flame of Knowledge.md:65`). For visual continuity, all three prompts use the same **GM visualization choice**: a long cathedral-like central spine, scorched black-red armor, broken observation rings, and ember-bright fissures. These features are not asserted as published canon.
