## Ships  
A "click" is the abstract unit for ship movement; in atmosphere, ship speeds are effectively tripled compared to ground vehicles.

- Hit Points: 50  
- Armor: 6  
- Resistance: 3  
- Damage: 12 + Spell (or by ammo)

- Limit: 4  
- Ammo: Spell, Missiles

- Special: Contains 1 Spellmatrix.
