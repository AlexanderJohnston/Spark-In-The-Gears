## Armor and Shields  
  
### NTM Cavalier Armor Line  
All Cavalier armors are atmosphere‑sealed for **up to 2 hours** and grant immunity to gas attacks.  
  
- **NTM Cavalier Light Body Armor**  
  - Armor: 1  
  - Resistance: 0  
  - Limit: 0  
  - Cost: 300 credits  
  
- **NTM Cavalier Body Armor**  
  - Armor: 2  
  - Resistance: 0  
  - Limit: 0  
  - Requirement: Strength 2+  
  - Cost: 500 credits  
  
- **NTM Cavalier Heavy Body Armor**  
  - Armor: 3  
  - Resistance: 0  
  - Limit: 0  
  - Requirement: Strength 3+  
  - Cost: 1,200 credits  
  
### Spellshields  
- **Light Spellshield**  
  - Armor: 2  
  - Resistance: 1  
  - Limit: 2  
  - Special: Can be used to parry spells; can be wrist‑mounted so a weapon can still be held.  
  - Cost: 2,000 credits  
  
- **Medium Spellshield**  
  - Armor: 3  
  - Resistance: 1  
  - Limit: 3  
  - Requirement: Strength 2+; must be hand‑wielded.  
  - Special: Can be used to parry spells.  
  - Cost: 3,000 credits  
  
- **Heavy Spellshield**  
  - Armor: 4  
  - Resistance: 2  
  - Limit: 4  
  - Requirement: Strength 3+; must be hand‑wielded.  
  - Special: Can be used to parry spells.  
  - Cost: 4,000 credits  
  
### Inuran Standard Spellarmor  
All suits are atmosphere‑sealed, grant gas immunity, and include Strength bonuses.  
  
- **Mark III Standard Spellarmor**  
  - Armor: 4  
  - Resistance: 1  
  - Limit: 2  
  - Effects: +1 Strength; 4 Armor; 1 Magic Resistance.  
  - Speed: 45 kph  
  - Cost: 12,000 credits  
  
- **Mark V Standard Spellarmor**  
  - Armor: 5  
  - Resistance: 3  
  - Limit: 3  
  - Effects: +2 Strength; 5 Armor; 3 Magic Resistance; 1 potion loader.  
  - Speed: 60 kph  
  - Cost: 22,000 credits  
  
- **Mark VII Standard Spellarmor**  
  - Armor: 6  
  - Resistance: 4  
  - Limit: 4  
  - Effects: +3 Strength; 6 Armor; 4 Magic Resistance; 3 potion loaders.  
  - Speed: 90 kph  
  - Cost: 171,000 credits  
  
- **Mark IX Standard Spellarmor**  
  - Armor: 7  
  - Resistance: 5  
  - Limit: 5  
  - Effects: +4 Strength; 7 Armor; 5 Magic Resistance; 5 potion loaders.  
  - Speed: 90 kph  
  - Cost: 971,000 credits
