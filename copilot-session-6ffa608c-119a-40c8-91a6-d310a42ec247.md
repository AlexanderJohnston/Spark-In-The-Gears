4) Each ship’s total **actions this round = total crewed consoles + spell matrices**.

### 2) Pilot step (do this for each ship, in initiative order if you use one)
6) **Velocity choice (optional):** Pilot may spend **1 action** to **+1 or -1 Velocity** (within ship limits).  
7) **Movement (mandatory):** Pilot must fly the **entire movement** each round:  
   - **Distance moved = base move × current Velocity**.  
   - Once you accelerate, you keep that speed **every move** until you decelerate.  
8) **Update defense:** Effective **Dodge = Dodge + Velocity** while targeted.  
9) **Maintain elevation:** If elevation changes, update the **die**.

---

10) **Fire a weapon (costs 1 action):**  
   - Roll attack vs target’s **effective Dodge (Dodge + Velocity)**.  
   - On hit: **Damage − Shield Strength**.  
   - Apply remaining damage to **Shield HP first**, then to **ship HP** if shields drop.  
11) **Depletion rule:** After firing, that weapon becomes **Depleted** and **cannot fire** until someone spends **1 action to Recharge that weapon**.  
12) **Guided weapons note:** Missiles/torpedoes are more accurate but can be **shot down (active defenses)** or **deflected/disrupted (passive defenses)**.

13) Each round, the **pilot gets 1 free Recharge action** (choose one):
