# Kaelith Voryn‑Shai — Personality Profile (Player‑Facing)

---

## Read‑Aloud (Primary)
Narrator: Kaelith
You move like you’re always half a step away from ignition—quiet, controlled, then suddenly *sharp*. Your armor is a hungry shadow at your shoulder, your magic a black‑violet ember in your palm, and your patience is a finite resource you spend with intent.

You’re polite when it’s useful, blunt when it matters, and protective when people are about to do something stupid in front of you.

## 1‑Page Quickplay Sheet (Table‑Ready)

### Kaelith in one line
A void‑touched Shayan exile chasing the *grand cycle*—and refusing to be anyone’s puppet, even his own armor’s.

### Core vibe
- **Practical mystic**: metaphysics, but with a checklist.
- **Blunt with a blade‑edge smile**: truth, delivered like a warning label.
- **Protective under pressure**: you grab people out of the fire first, then you process feelings later.

### Roleplay sliders (pick where you are *today*)
- **Masking “human” ↔ Not bothering** (S5: “difficult…to act human.”)
- **Restraint ↔ Flame‑temper** (S9: “habit of tossing around flame…”) 
- **Teacher/mentor ↔ Lone relic‑wolf** (S10: Corvin; “taught him a thing or three.”)
- **Cosmic awe ↔ Bitter pragmatism** (S5 revelation vs “What the fuck is this thing?”)
- **Protect the crew ↔ Protect the mission** (S5: hauling people free vs hunting goals)
- **Polite diplomacy ↔ Knife‑truth** (S10: Governor meeting)

### Do / Don’t (play cues)
**Do**
- Speak in **clean, declarative sentences** when it counts. (S10: “I don’t mince words.”)
- Use **small, physical tells** instead of long speeches (arms folded; gaze lingering; hand‑magic as punctuation).
- Treat trust like a **currency**: spend it slowly, but spend it decisively.

**Don’t**
- Don’t grovel. If an authority figure tries to own you, you **reframe the power dynamic**.
- Don’t handwave your armor’s hunger. You might *wear* it, but you don’t let it drive.
- Don’t do “soft lies.” If you lie, it’s a **hard tactical omission**.

### Three default reactions (steal these when you’re tired)
1) **Someone pushes authority** (Governor, military, syndicate)
   - You give them the **fakest polite smile**… then you say the thing that makes the room go quiet. (S10)
2) **Someone is about to self‑sacrifice**
   - You physically stop them. Grip. Anchor. “Not today.” (S4/S5: holding Lysa back; hauling people out)
3) **Something offers power** (Catalyst, relic, armor)
   - You look *hungry* for answers, not for domination—then you set rules: “master, not servant.” (S5)

### Three tells / habits
- **Coffee fixation** as stress relief / grounding. (S10: “I’m dying for a coffee.”)
- **Hands do magic while you talk** (void ball, flame flare) when you’re testing someone. (S3)
- **Protective proximity**: you trail, hover near, or place yourself as the “last line.” (S10: “trail closely behind Asha”; S5/S8)

### Three go‑to phrases (use as signature beats)
- “**I don’t mince words.**” (Session 10)
- “**I must become its master and not its servant.**” (Session 5)
- “**I don’t trust easily.**” (Session 3)

### Binary choice prompts (quick switches)
OOC: When you hesitate, pick one.
- **A: Polite mask** (smooth, formal, “kind sir…”) 
- **B: Knife‑truth** (blunt, political, a little venom) 

OOC: When anger spikes, pick one.
- **A: Threat as theater** (show void/fire; make them remember you)
- **B: Threat as action** (short, final, no spectacle)

### Optional micro‑mechanic (self‑prompt)
OOC: When Sargoth “hungers,” roll 1d6.
- **1–2**: You go still and quiet; you clamp down hard.
- **3–4**: You bargain with yourself: “after this, we feed it *safely*.”
- **5–6**: You snap at someone to regain control of the room.

## Social style & boundaries

### How you approach people
- **Default distance**: you trail, observe, absorb faces, clock weak points. (S10)
- **Respect competence**: pilots, captains, mages who keep their head. (S1/S3/S5)
- **You test with pressure**: a threat‑smile, a flare of void/fire, a pointed question. (S3)

### Trust rules (player‑usable)
OOC: Kaelith doesn’t “trust.” He **permits**.
- **Permission tier 1**: You get politeness.
- **Tier 2**: You get blunt honesty.
- **Tier 3**: You get vulnerability (rare).

Evidence:
- “I don’t trust easily… I hope you can understand that.” (S3)

### Boundaries you enforce
- **No one takes the hit for you**: “It’s on me… I’ll take the responsibility.” (S8)
- **No puppet strings**: “determine if we are to be the little puppets in your pocket.” (S10)
- **No suicide runs** (especially when you can physically stop it). (S4/S5)
