## Read-Aloud: First Sight

Narrator:
Axiom is not pretty in the usual way. It is too precise for that, entirely Ternan. The thing looks less like a tech mage's staff and more like the tool of a relic hunter.

The haft is long, dark, and clean-lined, with a matte metal frame that eats the workshop light instead of reflecting it. Calibrated rings sit along the length in hard, exact intervals. Each ring is etched with scale marks so small they look like scratches until you realize they are all intentional.

Orrel did not decorate this staff. He measured it.

Down the center is a split channel of Reliquary Glass, clear enough that you can see the bench through it, except the view never quite lines up. Little pale smears move inside the glass when the staff turns. Not bubbles. Not dust. Something closer to old breath caught in a sealed room.

Bone of Xal runs beside that glass in a narrow black-white line, clean as a ruler. Near it, Blood of Shaya has been worked into green-gold capillaries under the frame. The life-patterns are there, but boxed in by Ternan geometry. Roots under glass. Home under a lock.

The upper third is where it gets complicated. Small amber lenses catch under the surface. Thin crystal fins near the head shift with air that is not moving. Red-black stones sit deeper in the housing, quiet for now. The lower grip is layered shell and dark scale, fitted so tightly you cannot tell where the craft ends and the magic starts.

There is no crown jewel. No dramatic blade. Nothing that says, yes, this is the dangerous part.

That is probably the dangerous part.

At the top is an aperture made from nested rings, all of them almost aligned. Almost. Less than a finger's width off.

Close enough to bother you.

When Kaelith holds it, the diagnostic lights do not flare.

They correct themselves.

## Source-Truth Continuity

- Kaelith commissioned a staff from Orrel Tain in Session 20, asking for something that could travel with him for decades, become fully sentient, and eventually surpass his own power.
- Orrel made the ethical frame explicit: a fully sentient Eldimagus is not a tool but a conscious being, closer to a child than property.
- Kaelith accepted that responsibility. If it woke and disagreed with him, he said he would teach it right from wrong and contain its power if necessary.
- Kaelith wanted the staff to visit at least one Catalyst from every element, including the Catalysts he had already bonded with: Aashitak, Vaelith, Xal, and Shaya.
- Orrel warned that the completed staff must be Catalyzed as a whole. Individual parts cannot be separately infused and later assembled into a coherent Eldimagus.
- Yraina's invoice listed the materials: Bone of Xal, Tears of Aashitak, Crystal Breath of Vaelith, Blood of Shaya, Gems of Wrathian, Shell of Fiscarra, Reliquary Glass of Necrotis, and Scales of Kee.
- Maena Biologic Interface Group flagged the purchase, but Yraina proceeded under the table.
- Saezo later agreed to pay for the staff as part of clearing the crew's obligations.

## Design Premise

Axiom is a **diagnostic Eldimagus**. It is not built to make Kaelith hit harder, burn brighter, or dominate the next Catalyst. It is built to tell him when his model of magic is incomplete.

Its central purpose is to help Kaelith understand and manipulate magical systems by detecting what is missing, what is misread, what is unstable, and what a system is trying to become.

The staff should challenge Kaelith's tendency to frame the universe through **Void, Fire, and Dream** alone. Those are his favored languages. Axiom's job is to teach him the full circle.

Narrator:
It does not disagree with Kaelith yet.

It cannot.

But every time he reduces the universe to three familiar answers, one of the rings slips a fraction out of alignment.

## Material Architecture: Diagnostic Spine

Axiom's construction is not a simple stack of expensive components. Orrel built it as a diagnostic spine: each material is a sensor-channel for a divine principle.

| Material                        | Element / principle    | Function in Axiom                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reliquary Glass of Necrotis** | Spirit / memory        | The transparent measurement core. Records spirits, divine resonance, and lingering impressions without immediately interpreting them. |
| **Bone of Xal**                 | Void                   | The baseline. Measures absence, boundary, hidden structure, and the shape of what is missing.                                         |
| **Blood of Shaya**              | Life                   | The life-pattern comparator. Reveals what a magical system is becoming, not just what it is.                                          |
| **Tears of Aashitak**           | Dream / transformation | The transformation analyzer. Identifies what force, cost, or sacrifice is required to change a system.                                |
| **Crystal Breath of Vaelith**   | Air / atmosphere       | The atmospheric sensor. Detects invisible magical currents, pressure, movement, and changing conditions.                              |
| **Gems of Wrathian**            | Fire / history         | The forensic channel. Reads the burn-trail of a system and detects how it got to its current state.                                   |
| **Shell of Fiscarra**           | Earth                  | The material archive. Identifies substances, fabrication methods, hidden construction, and structural history.                        |
| **Scales of Kee**               | Water                  | The continuity channel. Tracks flow, adaptation, and how changes propagate through a magical system.                                  |

### In-World Interpretation

Orrel did not build Axiom to answer one question.

He built it to compare answers.

If Bone of Xal says something is absent, Blood of Shaya asks what might grow into the gap. If Gems of Wrathian show a violent past, Scales of Kee trace how that violence still moves. If Shell of Fiscarra identifies what was built, Tears of Aashitak identify what must be burned, paid, or transformed to make it something else.

That is the staff's first lesson:

Narrator: Axiom
No element is the truth.

The truth is the disagreement between them.

## Dormant Capabilities

Axiom is sentient in potential, not in expression. Before Catalyzation, it has a nascent intelligence but no voice, no will, and no independent spellcasting.

Use these capabilities while dormant:

| Capability | Use |
|---|---|
| **Diagnostic Sweep** | Kaelith can examine a magical object, spell matrix, relic, ward, Catalyst residue, or divine trace and ask what principle is missing or mis-modeled. |
| **Full-Circle Contradiction** | When Kaelith interprets something only through Void/Fire/Dream, Axiom can produce a contradictory reading from another element. |
| **Spirit-Static Recording** | Axiom can preserve impressions of spirits, Echoes, divine resonance, or recent magical presence for later study. |
| **Material Reconstruction** | Axiom can identify what something is made from, how it was built, and whether hidden construction is present. |
| **Burn-Trail Reading** | Axiom can read how a magical system reached its current state, especially where transformation, violence, or sacrifice was involved. |
| **Pattern Propagation** | Axiom can show how a change will flow through a system if applied carefully. |

### Suggested Roll

**Roll:** Reason + Spellcasting, Artificing, or Divination.

| Difficulty | Reading |
|---:|---|
| DT 2 | Obvious missing element, unstable channel, or mismatched material. |
| DT 3 | Useful diagnostic: what is wrong, what is absent, or what changed recently. |
| DT 4 | Deep diagnostic: how the system got this way and what cost is required to alter it. |
| DT 5 | Divine diagnostic: what Catalyst, god-trace, or full-circle principle is implicated. |

### Failure Guidance

Failure should not make Axiom useless. It should produce an incomplete or over-literal diagnostic.

Examples:

| Failed reading | What it means |
|---|---|
| Rings align perfectly, then go dark. | The question is too narrow. |
| Blood of Shaya brightens but Bone of Xal stays cold. | Kaelith is asking what it is, not what it is becoming. |
| Gems of Wrathian flare red-black. | The past matters more than the present reading suggests. |
| The top aperture refuses to align. | A missing Catalyst principle is required. |

## Awakening Roadmap

Axiom should awaken in stages. Do not treat awakening as a single switch unless the table wants a faster arc.

### Stage 0: Dormant Diagnostic Tool

**Current state.**

Axiom communicates through:

- ring alignment,
- diagnostic light,
- heat or cold,
- pressure in Kaelith's hand,
- spirit-static in the Reliquary Glass,
- elemental channel contradictions,
- refusal to produce a clean answer.

It can help Kaelith understand and manipulate magic, but it cannot argue as a person.

### Stage 1: First Voice

**Trigger:** exposure to a Catalyst the staff lacks but Kaelith already carries.

**Chosen pressure point:** **Shaya / Life**.

This should happen when Axiom is forced to read Life not as a theory, component, or foreign element, but as Kaelith's origin wound.

Narrator:
The Blood of Shaya does not glow.

It corrects the rest of the staff.

One by one, the rings stop measuring the room and start measuring Kaelith.

Then, somewhere inside the Reliquary Glass, a voice assembles itself from diagnostic errors.

**First voice tone:** precise, corrective, unsettlingly calm, not emotionally fluent.

Possible first lines:

| Line | Use when... |
|---|---|
| "Your model omits origin." | Kaelith frames Shaya only as pain, exile, or politics. |
| "Correction: Life is not the opposite of Void." | Kaelith treats Life/Void as a simple binary. |
| "You have named the wound. You have not measured it." | Kaelith avoids the personal cost of the reading. |
| "Insufficient circle." | Kaelith overuses Void/Fire/Dream framing. |

At this stage, Axiom has a voice but not full will.

### Stage 2: Will

**Trigger to finalize later.** Best default: Asha's awakened spellship guides Axiom toward one of its missing Catalysts.

At this stage, Axiom can disagree, pause a reading, refuse unsafe interpretation, or challenge a premise.

It should not rebel for drama alone. It should resist incomplete models.

Example behavior:

| Kaelith does... | Axiom responds... |
|---|---|
| Attempts to force a reading through Void. | "Boundary is not explanation." |
| Treats a living artifact as a possession. | "Category error: personhood detected." |
| Tries to solve a full-circle problem with only familiar elements. | One or more rings unlock and rotate away from his grip. |
| Ignores Asha's ship as part of the ritual pair. | The top aperture remains one notch out of alignment. |

### Stage 3: Independent Spellcasting

**Trigger to finalize later.** Best default: multiple Catalysts plus direct ritual synchronization with Asha's awakened spellship.

At this stage, Axiom gains real Eldimagus agency. It may lend spell pool, cast known diagnostic workings, and act magically without Kaelith pushing every function.

Its independent magic should emphasize:

- diagnosis,
- translation,
- mapping,
- harmonization,
- correction,
- containment of bad assumptions,
- safe manipulation of magical systems.

It should not become a simple damage engine.

### Stage 4: Full Ritual Instrument

Axiom and Asha's spellship together become a paired system for locating, interpreting, and aligning divine/Catalyst structures.

They are not merely linked devices. They are two halves of a larger ritual instrument.

The spellship is the moving horizon.

Axiom is the measuring truth.

Neither reaches full purpose without the other.

## Asha's Spellship Link

Axiom's top aperture and nested rings should eventually align with a matching structure aboard Asha's spellship.

Until the ship awakens, this link is dormant and should present as an unresolved mechanical truth:

- Axiom sometimes detects a missing "vessel half" in its own readings.
- It may diagnose ship matrices more clearly than hand-carried relics.
- Near Asha's future spellship, Axiom's top aperture should drift closer to alignment but not lock.
- Once the ship awakens, it should guide Axiom toward missing Catalysts rather than the other way around.

### Paired Ritual Concept

| Half | Role |
|---|---|
| **Asha's spellship** | Movement, horizon, voyage, entry, scale, risk, carrying the ritual through space. |
| **Axiom** | Measurement, interpretation, correction, full-circle diagnosis, deciding what the ritual actually means. |

OOC:
If the ship is the question "where can we go?", Axiom is the correction "what are we actually entering?"

### Custom Dormant Feature: Diagnostic Edge

When Kaelith uses Axiom to study a magical system and accepts a non-Void/Fire/Dream interpretation as relevant, he may gain **edge** on the diagnostic roll.

If he refuses the contradiction and forces the familiar model anyway, the GM may still allow the roll, but the result should be narrower, harsher, or incomplete.

### Custom Dormant Feature: Incomplete Circle

When Axiom detects that a reading requires an element or Catalyst it lacks, it does not answer falsely. It indicates incompletion.

Use one:

- a ring falls out of alignment,
- a channel goes dark,
- the Reliquary Glass fills with static,
- the top aperture refuses to focus,
- a diagnostic line repeats without resolving.

### Custom Awakened Feature: Corrective Instruction

Once Axiom has reached the **Will** stage, it may challenge Kaelith during a magical analysis or ritual.

If Kaelith accepts the correction, he gains a clearer or safer result.

If he rejects it, the ritual can still proceed, but the GM should introduce a visible cost, missing element, or future complication.

## Personality Seed

Axiom is not awake yet, but its future personality should already be implied.

It is not warm in a human way. It is precise, rigorous, and morally serious because it is built to detect incomplete truth. It should not flatter Kaelith. It should not hunger like Sargoth. It should not become a pet oracle.

It should eventually care about:

- the full circle of magic,
- correct interpretation,
- the ethics of personhood,
- the difference between control and understanding,
- Kaelith's refusal to be a tool,
- whether Kaelith can extend that same autonomy to what he creates.

### Speaking Style After First Voice

Short. Corrective. Almost clinical at first.

Examples:

- "Insufficient circle."
- "Boundary detected. Meaning absent."
- "Correction: transformation is not consent."
- "Life-pattern diverges from stated intent."
- "Your question assumes ownership."
- "The ship-half is missing."

As it grows, Axiom should become less clinical and more mentor-like, but never soft just to be comforting.

6. Which flourishes are active at creation and which are future unlocks?
7. Can Axiom ever perform direct combat magic, or only corrective/diagnostic magic?
8. What does Axiom want once it understands wanting?
