# Yraina Maena

**Inuran · Maena exile · concierge, negotiator, and infiltration partner**  
**Mechanical proposal:** Scoundrel, adapted Larcenist path. Experienced supporting NPC; not a starting-character build.

> [!important] Canon and proposed mechanics
> Biography, demonstrated actions, the restoration case, and the Session 30 position below are established. Attributes, skill ratings, class/path, catalyzation, weapon model, and armor model are **GM-ready proposals**, not previously revealed facts. Use this sheet as the encounter profile if adopted; it does not retroactively change prior rolls or give her unseen active effects. The supplied rules have no Inuran racial package: retain her Inuran identity and use a **neutral mechanical baseline with no racial bonuses**, not Human traits.

## Abilities, magic, and limits

- **Silver Tongue — proposed retained Larcenist feature:** Edge on Negotiation checks; one additional die specifically when conning or bluffing. Already included above.
- **Adapted path — explicit GM variation:** retain Scoundrel bases and Larcenist skills/masteries/Silver Tongue. **Do not grant Aura of Disruption or Disarm Device.** The printed path has automatic interference with recording and magical lock-opening tools; neither is established for Yraina. Her presence does not scramble cameras, erase records, or defeat security magic. The adaptation intentionally removes these features without replacement.
- **Voice of Shi — proposed passive catalyzation:** +1 Charisma, +3 Spell Pool, Dream Resistance 3. Already included. It grants neither a general resistance bonus nor extra successes on attacks.
- **Spells:** no combat spell repertoire is assigned to this profile, and none is active. The specific Voice of Shi entry lists its passive bonuses; the general creation text also discusses aspect Bolt/Manifest spells, but their full effect entries are absent from the supplied extracts. Do not invent a Dream Bolt damage profile or import Chameleon, Vanish, charm, healing, or counterspell here. This encounter profile functions through skills and the physical pistol. If spells are added later, use the campaign's actual spell cards and casting-access rules; her proposed expression would be Charisma. No hard-casting feature or Spellcasting mastery is granted.
- **Maena authority invocation — established, unreliable:** in Session 27 she traced spirit sigils that illuminated her wrist and a matching terminal symbol. She described a soul fragment held in the core repository and believed the authorization non-revokable. It unexpectedly failed on two attempts. This is not proof of a general Spirit spell list or a working access bypass. Cause unresolved; resolve any retry from the current fiction.
- **Ambush:** the combat excerpt awards Scoundrels Edge against opponents unaware of their presence. Apply **+2 successes only when that condition actually holds**, with no stacking. She does not gain Spellsniper's +2 Accuracy, +2 damage, or One with Shadow. The normal getting-the-drop rule requires actual unawareness, not merely the word “concealed.”

## Session 30 — Exchange Nine

**Established live position:** concealed in the **sample-custody doorway**, pistol ready, supporting Asha's ambush and keeping **Senn** in view. She has not fired. **Vesk** has arrived with two elite Maena Tech Mage escorts. Asha and Kaelith are concealed with Chameleon; **Yraina has no established Chameleon effect**. Her restoration case remains with her; its precise placement within the doorway has not been narrated.

She distracted Senn with shipping-manager concerns; after the PCs interrupted his report, she drew on **Meret Daal**, made Daal surrender the communicator, sent receiving staff toward **Survey Records**, and arranged Daal as bait at the comparison bench. The surrendered communicator's present holder is not specified here. These actions are past facts, not extra free actions when initiative begins.

1. **At the opening:** keep the pistol and case secure, watch Senn, and let the agreed ambush develop. Concealment does not create an already-resolved hit, an automatic interrupt, or an extra turn. Apply a surprise round only against those actually unaware under the table's ruling.

4. **If exposed or pressured:** use an action to reach real cover or withdraw toward the PCs; ask for help with restraints. Preserve a route out and a life afterward. Hatred of Maena makes her willing to take risks, not eager to die pointlessly.

## Character and knowledge

Pale-gold-haired, strikingly attractive, athletic in appearance, and impeccably composed when she can manage it. Her polished hospitality is a practiced professional manner. With Asha and Kaelith she can laugh, swear, show fear, and be blunt; the older concierge portrayal is not a rule against those later developments.

Daughter of **Kyrsa Maena**, estranged for over half her life. She spent **fifteen years destitute** waiting for the Trade Moon project; Saezo's employment was her first decent job. She hates her family and would gladly see its operation burn. She also wants security, money, freedom, and a future beyond the operation. Asha and Kaelith have become trusted collaborators. In the supplied live continuity, Kaelith's offer of a future with them pleasantly surprised her; she is more than an employee assigned to watch clients.

She understands Maena's administrative habits, household referrals, status performances, memory-work procedures, and the limits of her own access. She devised the wipe-and-restore entry plan and helped restore both PCs. **She does not know Kyrsa's hidden binding, the Custodian's private plans, or unrevealed outcomes.** Family membership is not omniscience. Keep the failed authorization unexplained unless new play supplies the cause.
