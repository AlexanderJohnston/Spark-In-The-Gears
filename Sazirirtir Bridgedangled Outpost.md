# Month 6

The land is not harsh but it is bare. Tobul spots the caravan of Udil Birdcrystal, a merchant and citizen of The Arrow of Respecting. The Outpost Liaison, Rimtar Atolzuglar from Feb Rirnol (the Mountainhomes), arrives moments later. Their timing is good, as we have just begun construction of the first drawbridge to secure our trade depot if we ever come under siege by the locals. Tobul notes that a basic military would be even better, but it will be a while before we have even the space let alone gear to train with.

Tobul requested future shipments of cave spider silk and logs from the Liaison while our broker, Throb, bartered with Udil for nothing because Udil couldn't unload his goods fast enough before the month was over.

A second entrance was built due to mistakes in construction of the first bridge. It will temporarily provide relief for the stranded dwarves outside and later can be set with traps for invaders.

During construction of the second entrance the Miner, Dishmab Zegathel, fell through a poorly planned mining shaft and into a large pile of galena ore, dying upon impact.

The following constructions are underway:
- Four new temples
- A crypt for the dead
- A Tavern with dormitory attached to the dining hall
- More bedrooms
- A temporary stone workshop for turning all of the non-economic stone into blocks



# Month 18

Construction of the Grand Works has commenced. The beating heart of our future industry.

The Planter, Zir Growsalot, and the Broker, Thob Thobbiest, were both put on vacation to avoid mental burnout. Donk Pocket takes his place as a temporary replacement.

A caravan arrived and a great many items were acquired by Donk Pocket. Mostly thread, food, cloth, barrels and beverages. A few anvils as well. He requested additional cloth, wood, seeds, meat, and cheese.

The vacation didn't seem to help the mood much, so Growsalot and Thob were both put back to work.

Our historian forgot to continue writing but there was also the following:
- Magma flow power system established
- There is a magma screw pump carved out for pumping the lava back into the volcano, but it's not finished.
- Workshop centers established
- Stockpiles established
- Kitchen area built out and food storage
- Log storage and garbage on the top floor
- Beautification initiatives for smoothing and engraving
- Future office spaces built out
- A dwarven child died but we never found the body so he probably jumped in the volcano. A slab was engraved in his honor.

Note that there are a couple missing walls at the top layer where the first magma forges are placed. I think magma crabs are getting in through those holes, from the volcano.


# Cont

Trade caravan arrived

Liaison from mountainhome arrived. In a fever, we requested additional enormous corkscrews, giant axe blades, spiked balls, and large, serrated discs. Additional anvils, cloth too. And surprisingly sand.

We purchased all of their clothing, cloth, bags and bins of seeds and leather and silk. We gave away our remaining gazots and cut gems.

Additional work orders have been established for glass production. We desperately need a source of sand! Perhaps delving deeper?

Billon bars, electrum bars, galena smelting, and other smelting was established.

The Farmer's guild hall, Farming Affini ty, is finally rich enough to be considered satisfactory.

Catten Kilrudstelid became obsessed with a project and took over the leatherworks. She created Etvuthkib, the legendary wolverine leather backpack, dedicated to The Whirling Hammer. Work is now underway on a grand artifact storage room. A reliquary?

While watching a demonstration on Dodging, militia commander, Mafol Stabbiest, became quite attached to their shield. Mafol later became rather attached to their spear as well.

To help improve morale around the fortress, the great shower was added to the main hall.

Finally... in the month of Granite the Elves finally arrived with the Spring weather. They remain lukewarm at best, but that is the most we can hope for from these local elves. Perhaps if we continue to shovel gifts, gazots, and gems at them, then they will start to see us more favorably.

Ber Tadmeng was elected mayor, ousting the incumbent, whose name we have already forgotten. Was it Thob Thobbiest? If so, then good riddance. He works hard but he is constantly filled with internal rage over the early mining accidents.

That said... Ber Tadmeng has poor empathy, a meager ability with social relationships, no sense of spatial awareness, poor memory, and sucks at talking to people (heyyooo!) but for some reason everyone has elected her. I guess she's just kind of awesome when you get to know her, who knows.

From the elves, we attempted to purchase all of the clothing, seeds, sand, clay, and the strange... grown-wood instruments. Why not just grow the tree, chop and shape it? You can always grow more trees. We purchased some random animals from them at Ber's demand, but her intentions are suspicious. Maybe she just likes to see the natural world caged up. She is deeply moved by nature and beauty, but despises nature. Such a strange dwarf.

As a note, stop trying to offer Gazots to the elves. They really don't like our use of wood. 

Rimtar Meskogan became withdrawn and behaved strangely, with dwarves reporting that he seemed to be muttering about cuts and gems. He has disappeared into the Gemcutter's workshop... claiming he will emerge as our legendary Gem Setter. He will change the course of our history! Days later he rejoined civilization, showing off Zoluthsut, a perfectly cut brown zircon. It immediately went into the Reliquary.

A necromancer visited the fort. Somewhat unnerving.

Onget Nileskol followed in Rimtar's footsteps, but instead became a legendary Stonecutter. May his engravings and crafts carry us forward into the future.

Lastly, Zir Growsalot became a Hammer Lord!
