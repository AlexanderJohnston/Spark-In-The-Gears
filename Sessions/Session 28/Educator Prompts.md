# Educator Prompts

Short cues for continuing from the end of Session 27.

## Yraina acts

**Narrator:** Yraina moves at once, reaching for the woman and trying to wrench her away from the display. The specialist is still waiting for an answer.

**GM:** Resolve the woman's resistance and Yraina's attempt to disable or kill her before advancing. The method and result are open. Leave Asha and Kaelith's actions to their players; don't assume an alarm or escape.

## The specialist waits

After the immediate action resolves, keep the contracts open on the display. The specialist looks between the woman and the visitors. “How many times have I asked?” He waits for an answer.

Let the players decide whether to answer him, secure the displayed record, or do something else. Don't supply the number of refusals or decide whether he wants restoration.
