# Session 28 Design

**Current operation guide:** [[Maena Raid GM Guide]] develops the approved raid scene by scene for Yraina's servant-and-wipe route. Subsequent GM direction makes discovery of Maena's neural-lattice god-making project the primary goal, with obvious Terra/Sol leads. This document retains the Educator setup; the guide governs the later operation.

For the continuation after leaving the Educator and the main Maena operation, see [[Maena Raid Draft]]. It is a first high-level proposal, with new material clearly separated from established events.

The GM has confirmed that Kyrsa is elsewhere during the main raid. Its scope is a decisive strike against her operation that can expose the hidden operator, with the larger program still to pursue.

**The crew has the equipment within reach and has encountered someone whose present refusal Maena will not recognize. What they do with that situation determines the operation's scale.**

Start from [[Session 28 Scratchpad]]. The established starting facts below come from the log. **Every new motive, resource, response, discovery, and offscreen result proposed here is GM preparation, not canon.** There is no prescribed route or sequence, and no requirement to complete either a raid or the later memory-wipe infiltration.

## Starting situation

Asha, Kaelith, and Yraina are in the specialist's room. Asha's hand is on his shoulder, her equipment case is nearby, and she has just signaled toward the woman. Yraina looks ready to kill. The woman's procedure has not resumed; the specialist's question about repeated refusals is still unanswered. No alarm is established.

**Subsequent GM clarification, 2026-09-22:** Yraina takes Asha's hint and will disable or kill the woman. That response is now established direction, not an optional design proposal. Begin with the impending intervention and resolve its method and outcome in play; do not skip ahead to a presumed death, successful restraint, or building-wide alarm. What the party does with the specialist, equipment, witnesses, and remaining facility stays open.

The players have already made consequential choices: they trusted Yraina's route, improvised past its failure, took the cases, followed the woman, and stayed while the contracts were exposed. Those choices have gained equipment and information while placing them in apparent service to the coercive procedure. The specialist has reason to fear them. His response can change with what they actually do.

## Resistance and consequences — proposed

Prepare resistance around objectives, so the map can support player invention. Retain the logged entrance, vehicle access, descending corridor, preparation room, and specialist room. If more space is needed, add record custody and a service connection as plainly labeled additions. Do not pre-place the soul repository here.

Local defenders, if called, should first seek control of exits, people, and valuable records. Establish their arrival and capabilities through observable activity. The PCs' armor and magic can decisively defeat local resistance; that success should matter. Maena can investigate a loss later without making every corridor an unwinnable fight now.

## The failed authority stays unresolved

The earlier design suggested an obsolete procedure, but play supplied a stronger and more specific claim: Yraina said access was non-revokable without rebuilding the system. A generic expired credential would evade the actual discrepancy.

Use her uncertainty as it stands. She may acknowledge the failure, distinguish what she remembers from what she can verify, and help preserve evidence. Her successful navigation and bluff provide reasons to value her even while questioning her explanation. Do not secretly settle the answer through an NPC aside or turn an arbitrary technical failure into proof of betrayal.

Investigation can establish bounded facts before the cause is known: what the local terminal recorded, whether anyone actually recognized her, whether the failed invocation was reported. The meaning of those facts must remain consistent with the eventual GM ruling. No new culprit or soul mechanic is required for this session.

## Kyrsa and the concealed binding — confirmed GM truth

**GM confirmation, 2026-09-22:** Kyrsa Maena, Yraina's mother, is the secretly bound Maena Matriarch. The earlier identity question is settled. The ancient Necrotis follower's bespoke Nefarius/Necrotis binding acts on Kyrsa; her own institutional interests and responsibility remain real.

This gives a future investigation a personal connection to Yraina. If evidence of unusual decisions becomes available, Yraina's family knowledge may help interpret it, within the limits of her long estrangement. Such evidence remains proposed until encountered. Neither Yraina nor the PCs automatically knows about the binding, and this confirmation supplies no explanation for the failed authorization. Kyrsa's location and any response to the Educator operation still depend on established information and events.

## Distributed work — all results below are proposals

These are independent assignments with their own pace. No team has to finish a predetermined share of one puzzle. Give each a proportionate accomplishment when travel and work time permit; a few minutes in the Educator do not automatically produce completed research on another ship or planet.

### Lysa aboard the Maker's Wrath

**Established:** approved visit and translocation, with Aran, Voria, and Frit required to accompany her. She promised Kaelith she would bring something back; permission to take an artifact has not been granted.

**Proposed intentions:** Lysa wants a useful way to recognize coercion and understand Maena's possible inheritance. Her escorts want the research contained and supervised. Their exact individual attitudes can develop in play; do not manufacture a disagreement just to obstruct her.

**Useful proposed gain:** a supervised comparison procedure for recording ordinary Spirit bindings and distinguishing their effects from lost memory access. Notes or an approved diagnostic record can be the “something” she brings back. This gives later evidence a better test without identifying the unseen Matriarch's blood mixture from afar.

**Pressure and consequence:** deeper access or removal of a dangerous sample may need an expanded agreement. Successful study can remain a straightforward gain. She does not have to unleash something, gain a Catalyst, or return with a universal cure.

### Seraphine and Tamsin investigating Ternus

**Established:** corvette departure with a small marine squad; arrival and active contacts are not yet shown.

**Proposed intentions:** Seraphine wants a procurement trail she can act on and sources who will still speak to her afterward. Tamsin protects the team and checks where an inquiry exposes them. A contact can want an irregular request investigated while still believing ordinary procurement serves Ternus.

**Useful proposed gain:** authenticate one shipment's supplier, receiving office, and release authority. A Maena-linked intermediary, if supported by those records, provides a specific next lead. Do not infer the whole Umbral production process from a shipping entry.

**Pressure and consequence:** observing preserves access; copying records risks a source; stopping a shipment changes supply and may concern Saezo if he learns of the loss. Seraphine can investigate competently without asking Asha to approve every conversation. A major disruption warrants a report if the team can communicate; silence is not authorization for unlimited sabotage.

### Rho and Smoke approaching Saezo

**Established:** arrangements for a platoon protection offer. Saezo has not accepted it in the log.

**Proposed intentions:** Rho wants credible access and an intervention option; Smoke and the platoon provide actual protection. Saezo wants useful professionals who visibly strengthen his command against Maena.

**Useful proposed gain:** a limited protective assignment lets them learn who can order a reserve transfer and observe one physical dependency in the feed toward the deep engine. Acceptance should be a new reported development. If access is narrower, identifying the responsible operator or obtaining a future inspection opportunity still counts as progress.

**Pressure and consequence:** safeguarding operations gains trust but also protects dangerous infrastructure. Suspicious inspection can narrow access. Before any immediate dump crisis, establish observable preparations such as centralizing command, summoning operators, and changing feed configuration, plus time and opportunities to interfere. Those details are proposed, not facts already known to Rho.

Keeping Saezo reassured is temporary protection against a dangerous response. His desired Catalyst, Maena's intended birth, and the tainted reserve remain distinct. Disabling a dependency need not mean igniting Spark or giving Maena control.

### Local support

**Proposed default:** Nyrix and available ship crew remain reachable from the nearby Lone Star. Confirm personnel availability before assigning a rescue force; the two detached marine groups cannot simultaneously be aboard. The logged one-week promise is a fallback, not an obligatory delay if the PCs ask for help sooner.

The ship offers extraction, communications, a secure place to examine kits, and the revival brew already delivered in Session 26. Do not place that brew in the room or use it to repair memories without a basis. Bell can support care if available; her participation has not been assigned in the log.

## Equipment acquisition and the next infiltration are separate decisions

Getting usable kits out is a real success. It can support restoration for others, study, leverage, or the previously proposed infiltration. It does not commit the players to being wiped.

If they return to that proposal, resolve only the practical questions it depends on: the target and purpose of entry; each volunteer's consent; what the wipe removes and preserves; tested restoration requirements; custody of backups; how Yraina can actually restore them during holding; gear access; and a recovery arrangement if she cannot. Carry forward her claims as claims until tested.

The living armor and Axiom are separate intelligences or relationships whose treatment needs clarity before use of the method. Axiom's logged Xal/Void development does not grant automatic Spirit diagnosis, a complete memory archive, or remote explanation of the failed authorization. Kaelith's suggested Void retrieval remains a potential resource to assess, not a completed solution to every entry restriction.

If the crew chooses another way into Maena, use what it has earned: equipment, documents, a possible ally, knowledge of ordinary custody, and perhaps an authenticated destination. Avoid restarting the campaign as though rejecting the wipe wastes their progress.

## Useful session outcomes

A playable gain may be as small as getting the kits out with a preserved refusal record, or as large as removing the specialist and breaking this facility's control over his expertise. Other gains include a concrete destination, clearer limits on Yraina's knowledge, or an independent team's first useful access.

Failures should also leave an actionable state: lost cover but retained equipment; a rescued person with records still held by Maena; escaped staff who can identify the group; a bargain with an obligation. None requires a predetermined ending, a revelation of the concealed binding, or the main infiltration to happen this session.

Both requested GM clarifications are resolved: Yraina will act to disable or kill the woman, and Kyrsa is the bound Matriarch. The attack's result remains unplayed; the binding remains GM knowledge. No new narration is included.
