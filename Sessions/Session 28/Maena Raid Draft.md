# Maena Raid Draft

**Developed version:** [[Maena Raid GM Guide]] is the scene-by-scene operation for the selected servant-and-wipe route. Subsequent GM direction establishes discovery of Maena's purpose as the primary objective: it manufactures neural-lattice hardware and intends Spark to animate an unprecedented artificial god. The operation also supplies clear evidence that Maena knows about extraordinary power associated with Terra/Sol. The research and takeover targets below remain valuable optional interventions. Alternative entry routes in this retained outline are superseded for the current operation.

## Overview

**Proposed direction: use the equipment acquired at the Educator to make a meaningful strike against Maena's Trade Moon headquarters, where household authority supports the effort to take Saezo's infrastructure and overcome the Hand's taint.**

The Educator established how Maena controls useful people. The main raid should let the crew attack what that control makes possible. Give them a consequential target: the people, records, and current research that support Maena's next move. Taking or destroying those assets changes the enemy's actual capabilities.

The work after leaving the Educator is compact: secure what survived, determine what Maena knows, validate the means of entry and recovery, and choose what the raid is meant to accomplish. This can happen through practical preparation and short reports. It does not require another extended council or a second acquisition adventure.

The raid itself is an open operation against several connected functions. The party can infiltrate, negotiate, seize particular assets, confront leadership, or fight its way through. Those approaches produce different opportunities and resistance; none is an obligatory sequence.

**Draft status:** all new locations, staff roles, dependencies, evidence, and enemy responses below are proposals. Established facts are identified separately. No Educator escape, wipe, raid, or offscreen mission result has happened yet. This draft extends [[Session 28 Design]] and [[Session 28 Scratchpad]]. It may carry beyond Session 28 rather than fitting a predetermined session length.

**GM-confirmed scope:** Kyrsa is elsewhere; this raid targets her operation. It is a decisive strike that can expose the hidden operator, with the larger program still to pursue. The particular headquarters and its contents below remain proposals within that scope.

## After the Educator: turn the gain into a usable operation

These are preparation needs, not scenes that must happen in order. The nearby Lone Star is an available base if they choose it.

### Carry forward the actual exit

| Exit outcome | What the crew has earned | What changes before the raid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Equipment secured, identities concealed** | A restoration resource and local procedural knowledge. | Preserve the possibility of ordinary entry. Missing equipment alone need not reach senior leadership immediately. |
| **Witnesses can identify Yraina or the PCs** | The same equipment, with a known attribution risk. | Establish whether a report reaches the proposed target. Extra scrutiny must follow that report, rather than appear everywhere at once. |
| **Specialist or records brought out** | A person with his own preferences and/or evidence of denied consent. | Offer protection and control over his records. Any help he gives is voluntary; he is not automatically an expert on the headquarters or the hidden program. |
| **Equipment lost or damaged** | Whatever information, relationships, or other assets survive. | Direct contact, commercial cover, or assault remains viable. Do not make repeating the theft the only way forward. |

Yraina has reason to report the limit of her knowledge after the authorization failure. Keep that conversation practical: what she can still substantiate, what requires checking, and what access her family relationship could obtain. Do not resolve the discrepancy as an expired credential or establish betrayal by implication.

### Give the raid a target before choosing the entry method

**Proposed target:** Maena's Trade Moon headquarters, combining commercial management, household administration, and a restricted research operation. It coordinates local activity; it does not contain the entire family, the Umbral mines, Saezo's reservoir, or the moon's deep Catalyst engine.

**Lead:** Yraina identifies the headquarters and its ordinary function from her local experience and supplies the household intake procedure herself. She does not know what the restricted research is doing. Educator records or a later procurement report can corroborate the connection if the players obtain them; access to the next adventure is not gated behind rescuing one particular NPC.

**Recommended operational purpose:** seize enough of Maena's local command and research to obstruct its next move and identify the concealed program's leadership. Present the attainable assets clearly enough that the players can choose a narrower priority.

### Make the wipe credible if they still want it

Yraina's logged proposal remains available: enter as her personal servants, be sent to temporary holding, restore their memories, then retrieve equipment through Kaelith's proposed Void rift. It is not an automatic ticket to leadership.

**Administrative basis:** intake refers a returning family member's disputed personal-servant claim to household staff. Recognition and ordinary referral can place the group in holding even when the discretion sigil is unavailable. Yraina knows this procedure; it works as described for the operation. This is a separate access process, not an explanation for the Educator failure.

**Proposed kit access:** a declared restoration kit can remain with the household sponsor during that review, subject to normal inspection. Validate this detail through current information; do not hide a confiscation rule until the PCs are already helpless. The actual imprints, kit, operator, and time alone are distinct dependencies.

Use equipment inspection and an informed voluntary test, if requested, to establish what restoration does. Agree on what memory access disappears, what skills remain, and what happens to memories made during the interval. The earlier memory-rule table remains a proposal, not settled mechanics. Backups kept aboard and available support give the plan resilience without guaranteeing extraction.

For gear retrieval, assess Kaelith's actual Void capability and the intended location before commitment. Any local ward must have an observable extent and a way to interact with it. Avoid inventing facility-wide anti-Void protection solely to defeat his plan. Axiom and living armor require separate treatment; no unannounced erasure or automatic restoration power.

### Let the divided crew improve the odds

- **Rho and Smoke:** if sufficient time has passed and Saezo accepts the protection offer, they can help keep him informed and identify a warning that he is preparing the reserve-to-core transfer. They cannot promise remote control of his systems before gaining access. Their platoon remains committed there unless deliberately reassigned.
- **Seraphine and Tamsin:** a verified supplier, official, or authorization chain can help authenticate seized records or support commercial cover. Their corvette departure is established; finished research is not. The raid can proceed before a report arrives.
- **Lysa:** supervised work aboard the Maker's Wrath can produce a comparison method for Spirit coercion. It becomes useful when the crew brings back evidence or obtains access to a subject. She does not diagnose Kyrsa remotely or arrive automatically with a cure.
- **Nyrix and the Lone Star:** available local support can hold backups, gear, and an extraction plan. Specify a practical check-in if the players want one; the existing one-week rescue promise need not be the only trigger. Confirm remaining personnel before assigning additional marines.

## The raid: five functions worth reaching

**Proposed structure.** These functions connect through staff, work, transport, and authority. They are not five mandatory encounters or a linear floor plan.

| Function | What it does | Useful gain | Natural conflict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reception and household intake** | Admits business visitors, workers, and family dependents; handles disputed custody. | A credible presence, staff cooperation, or control of a local entrance and exit. | Staff want a responsible sponsor; security wants accountability; the crew wants movement without surrendering initiative. |
| **Specialist allocation and records** | Assigns expertise and tracks which identities and memories a job requires. | Current assignments, an insider relationship, and the ability to interrupt or redirect labor. | Preserving an imprint can help its subject while also preserving something Maena values. Workers have different wishes about restoration and escape. |
| **Restricted interface research** | Studies why the stored Spark will not support the intended work and develops possible workarounds. | Seize the active research, recruit willing expertise, or destroy a concrete experimental dependency. | Researchers may know the technical obstacle without knowing the intended birth. Their work is harmful even when they describe it as solving an industrial problem. |
| **Trade and executive control** | Coordinates contracts, operational appointments, and preparations to take Saezo's infrastructure. | Identify the succession mechanism, disrupt its implementation, obtain the relevant trade terms, or expose its sponsors. | Rival executives can preserve their positions by cooperating, concealing, or attempting to replace Kyrsa. A stolen signature is not universal control. |
| **Local director's office and restricted correspondence** | Holds delegated authority and instructions connecting Kyrsa to the concealed program. | Identify who directs protected work, obtain evidence beyond ordinary corporate abuse, and seize a current reporting channel or destination. | The director answers to Kyrsa but receives protected instructions that bypass ordinary management. Kyrsa herself is elsewhere. |

### Give the research a real vulnerability

**Proposed dependency:** the local workaround effort relies on a calibrated comparison rig and accumulated experimental results interpreting how tainted Spark behaves. Its current progress is concentrated here; relevant input material may come from elsewhere. This does not establish that later Spark shipments are clean or that the scientists already know how to reverse the Hand's weapon.

Removing the rig and records, destroying them, or persuading key specialists to leave produces a genuine setback. State its limits: it halts or substantially delays this local line of work, not every use of Spark across Ternus. Do not reveal convenient perfect backups after the raid solely to cancel the success. If another copy or team exists, make it part of the evidence and target assessment.

### Give the takeover a concrete administrative form

**Proposed mechanism:** a prepared emergency operating agreement would replace Saezo's management with Maena-backed administrators after a manufactured finding that he cannot safeguard the reserve. The raid can uncover the sponsors, required ratification, and people designated to take physical control.

This supplies leverage against the political removal already established in GM notes. It does not mean an unsigned document can instantly command all the pumps. Seizing or exposing the agreement can block this route while creating evidence Rho or Seraphine can use. If Maena tries another route afterward, that requires new work and creates new signs.

Proposed personal priority if Yraina's presence is reported: bring her back within an arrangement Kyrsa can supervise. | Reliable reports, demonstrated losses, or a credible offer can prompt new instructions. No live audience or immediate awareness is guaranteed. |
| **Local director** | Preserve commercial operations, retain Kyrsa's confidence, and avoid inheriting blame for the protected research. | Evidence that another authority is spending the director's people and resources without accepting responsibility; a way to survive without protecting every secret. Can supply access, negotiate, resist, or try to transfer blame upward. |
| **Security commander** | Protect leadership, prevent unauthorized movement, and retain key assets. | A credible household order, a fractured command structure, superior force, or a defensible withdrawal. Can be defeated, persuaded, isolated, or bypassed. |
| **Research lead and controlled specialists** | Preserve work, personal safety, or access to their own expertise. Knowledge of the end use varies. | Evidence of what the work enables, access to their imprints, or a credible offer of protection. They need not all join the crew or share one moral position. |
| **Ancient Necrotis follower** | Preserve progress toward the birth and conceal or retain control over Kyrsa. | Losing the real research dependency, exposure of the binding, or losing access to the household. Responds through actual channels and agents, not omniscient awareness of the PCs. |

The working proposal places the hidden operator offsite, with its identity, physical form, and exact location still unselected. Choose finite local resources when bulking out the raid. Its ancient nature must not become permission to negate any successful plan.

## Entry, force, and resistance

| Approach | Advantage | Price or exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yraina's wiped-servant cover** | Reaches internal custody under an apparently ordinary procedure; restoration can return initiative inside. | Dependence on recovery arrangements, limited initial movement, and possible recognition. A real wipe does not conceal famous faces. |
| **Family approach through Kyrsa's local director** | Can reach a consequential delegate quickly by invoking a real relationship. | Makes Yraina's return visible and gives the director time to report it. Kyrsa is not brought onsite to complete this approach. |
| **Commercial or specialist access** | Exploits a working manufacturer with deliveries, contractors, and technical demands. | Requires a credible purpose and only grants access appropriate to it. Education records or crew intelligence can help without being mandatory keys. |
| **Targeted assault** | Uses the party's considerable power to seize a selected asset or entrance. | Gives defenders clear information and makes reinforcement or asset evacuation more likely. It can succeed outright. |

**Proposed defensive character:** ordinary staff and controlled workers occupy most business functions; professional armored defenders and Spirit specialists protect sensitive work. Dangerous spirits are credible from the freighter evidence, but employees need not all be binders or fanatics. Salt can buy limited protection against spirits if brought; it does not stop armed personnel or remove a binding.

Security reacts to a detected intrusion by containing the affected area, protecting leadership and research, and requesting help. Those objectives compete for people. Attackers who redirect orders, split defenders, secure transport, or sever communications can change the response. There is no automatic final corridor full of enemies regardless of their method.

Wider Maena retaliation should be consequential but legible. Local containment, identification of the attackers, and a request for outside force are separate developments. Yraina's old warning about orbital power supports a serious escalation risk, not an unannounced strike that ends play.

## Discovering control without collapsing the mechanisms

The raid can provide independent routes into the same question: who can make Kyrsa accept decisions against her own interests?

- **Administrative evidence — proposed:** protected research continues despite a documented instruction to reduce a costly activity; the countermand identifies an authority outside the ordinary executive chain.
- **Human evidence — proposed:** an employee knows which person or channel consistently gets such decisions reversed. Yraina can compare what she sees with the mother she remembers, without possessing current omniscience.
- **Magical evidence — proposed:** a preserved instruction or research object may carry a trace of another practitioner's work, giving the crew something concrete to compare with Lysa's findings. That establishes a magical lead, not a diagnosis of the absent Kyrsa. Direct investigation of her concealed binding belongs to a later opportunity to examine her or evidence genuinely linked to that procedure.

These avenues can converge or substitute for one another. Do not require three predetermined clues before allowing a sound inference. Evidence can earn a specific suspected intermediary or location even if the party cannot yet break the binding.

The Educator's memory technology, Yraina's soul-linked access, the Hand's Spark taint, and Kyrsa's bespoke binding remain different things. No universal control terminal should resolve all four. The authorization discrepancy remains open; reaching the headquarters does not automatically locate its sealed core or supply a retroactive explanation.

## Leadership and the extent of victory

**Kyrsa is elsewhere.** Executive instructions, her appointed director, and current correspondence make her authority tangible. Contacting her is possible if the PCs obtain a viable channel; a remote audience is not guaranteed or needed to complete the raid. Yraina's claim that important access would ultimately put her before her mother can remain a longer-term prospect rather than an automatic transfer during this operation.

**Proposed operator presence:** keep the ancient follower offsite too, exerting influence through a protected research liaison and a reporting channel. This supports the chosen scope without introducing a villain who must escape. The liaison is a subordinate, not a second secretly bound Matriarch or a guaranteed expert on the whole scheme. Its name and exact relationship to the follower can be developed later.

**Recommended principal gain:** seize or disable the comparison rig and its accumulated results. **Additional attainable gain:** break the prepared takeover arrangement. Either materially damages Maena; achieving both is a major victory, not the minimum required for success.

**Recommended investigative payoff:** establish that protected work answers to an authority outside Kyrsa's ordinary chain, and obtain an actionable destination, named intermediary, or authenticated reporting channel for that authority. Evidence may support suspicion of coercion, but proving the exact binding on an absent Kyrsa requires more than reading unusual orders. The crew can expose the hidden operator's role without immediately knowing its ancient identity or blood formula.

The larger program survives because its Umbral work, leadership, and wider supply relationships are elsewhere—not because the GM restores the local assets the crew successfully removed. The next target should follow from what they learn here. Killing the local director does not automatically transfer Kyrsa's binding, trigger the moon's engine, or wipe every record.

## What the raid can leave changed

| Achieved result | Lasting gain | Remaining pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Local research seized or disabled** | Maena loses meaningful progress toward overcoming the taint; the crew may gain interpretable evidence. | Protect captured material and willing specialists; prevent recovery or a visibly new research effort. |
| **Takeover machinery exposed or broken** | Maena loses its prepared route into Saezo's infrastructure. | Saezo may become more confident; Rho's work still matters. |
| **Local director or research liaison captured or killed** | Local command or the protected reporting relationship loses a real participant. | Surviving staff retain only their actual authority. Kyrsa and the offsite operator must react through the resources still available to them. |
| **Hidden operator's role exposed** | An identifiable intermediary, destination, or reporting channel replaces a broad suspicion. | Follow the lead and investigate Kyrsa's coercion; her exact binding need not be diagnosed during this raid. |
| **Workers and their histories removed from Maena control** | Maena loses expertise and the people gain choices. | Shelter, consent, and their future affiliations remain practical concerns. |
| **Partial raid with an extraction** | Retained equipment, evidence, or people remain genuine gains even if another target is lost. | Maena can act on what witnesses and surviving records actually reveal. |

Nothing here requires the players to choose a wipe, clear every function, or destroy the whole headquarters. Extraction can follow an accomplished objective instead of a final boss.
