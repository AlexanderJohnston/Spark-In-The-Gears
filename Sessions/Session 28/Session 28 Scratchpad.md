## Established events

### Agreement and deployment

- Kaelith committed to accompanying Asha after asking how restoration would work. Yraina proposed declaring both as personal servants at Maena entry, using the resulting temporary holding period to restore them. That remains a proposed later operation.
- Kaelith asked for intervention if they had not returned in a week. Nyrix agreed to break the doors. The exact start of that interval and a nearer check-in schedule were not established.
- Kaelith proposed retrieving their gear through a Void rift after restoration. Yraina said she would not smuggle it for them. No gear cache or successful rift retrieval was shown.
- Yraina announced she would take kitchen salt as a temporary barrier against spirits. Lysa confirmed the principle; collection and current carriage were not shown.
- Aran conveyed Pantheon approval for **one mage** aboard the Maker's Wrath, accompanied by **Aran, Lady Voria, and Lady Frit**. Lysa sent her readiness missive and was immediately translocated away.
- Seraphine and Tamsin departed aboard a corvette with a small squad of Asha's marines. Rho and Smoke made arrangements for Saezo. The Lone Star traveled to the Trade Moon and landed at a minor port.

### Entry and equipment

- Asha, Kaelith, and Yraina flew in their suits to the Educator, about half a kilometer from the port. Its concealed surface construction includes pedestrian and vehicle entrances. No exterior guards were visible; something behind the glass tracked their arrival.
- Yraina attempted her authority invocation twice. On the first attempt, her wrist and the attendant's display showed matching symbols before going dark. The door did not admit them. On the second, nothing answered.
- Yraina claimed she had been attacked by a worker and freshly wiped, and had come to restore a backup. **This was a bluff, not her actual condition.** Asha and Kaelith supported it with successful Performance checks.
- The attendant became afraid and manually admitted them. Staff in the corridor moved aside. Social authority worked after magical authorization failed.
- They passed a man asking for his daughter after a frame was removed. A technician told him he had no dependents; he struggled to remember why he had asked, and she reached for the frame again. Neither the daughter's existence nor his true family circumstances were resolved.
- In a preparation room, Yraina removed **three complete cases** from a cabinet and handed them to the PCs. Each had fitted spaces containing a frame, handheld unit, and blank wafers. Nearby were numbered memory wafers, some labeled with names. No personal backups were made or named wafers explicitly taken.
- Yraina looked terrified and wanted to leave. An older Inuran woman entered, treated them as available assistance, and requested a witness and someone to hold a specialist still.

### The specialist and the cutoff

The group followed her two doors down. The specialist had three contracts covering the same dates and facilities he did not understand. He asked Kaelith to let him finish reading. Asha put the equipment case on a nearby surface, stood behind him, and placed a hand on his shoulder without pressure but with implied intent.

His reading distinguished three permissions: payment for work, retention of memories by another facility, and future restoration of expertise while omitting the intervening period. A recording showed him previously asking to cancel. Its disposition was: **“Cancellation deferred pending restoration of the consenting identity.”**

The woman defended this as restoring the context of an informed decision. The specialist asked how many times he had requested release. Yraina looked ready to kill and sought a signal from Asha. Asha's player described her attempt to suppress empathy failing, followed by a slight head jerk toward the woman.

**Nothing after that signal has happened.** No attack, restraint, alarm, rescue, restoration, extraction, or death is recorded. The specialist's question remains unanswered. The GM's OOC expectation of a “big raid” and action next week establishes preparation intent, not an attack already made or a required player route. The session ended early and awarded 2 EXP.

**Subsequent GM clarification, 2026-09-22:** “Yraina will take the hint and disable or kill her.” This settles her intended response to Asha's signal. Carry it into Session 28 as an impending action, not a completed attack or a confirmed death. Its method and outcome remain open.

## Subsequent GM confirmations

**Confirmed 2026-09-22:** Kyrsa Maena, Yraina's mother, is the secretly bound Maena Matriarch. This supersedes the identity uncertainty in earlier preparation. It makes the concealed coercion directly relevant to Yraina's family without establishing that she or the PCs know about it. It does not explain Yraina's failed authority or establish Kyrsa's current location.

The GM has also settled Yraina's response to Asha's signal: she will disable or kill the woman, with method and outcome still open.

**Confirmed scope for the upcoming main raid:** Kyrsa is elsewhere; the party will be raiding her operation. Design a decisive strike that can expose the hidden operator, with the larger program still to pursue. [[Maena Raid Draft]] proposes the target and its assets within those bounds. This is preparation scope, not a logged party decision or completed event.

**Further GM corrections for the operation:**

- The servant-and-wipe route is selected. The operation guide supplies concrete preparation, intake, restoration, and internal scenes for that route.
- The main objective is to discover why Maena is pursuing this work. Maena manufactures neural-lattice magitech hardware and intends to use Spark to breathe life into it, creating its own magitech god.
- This would be unprecedented: all existing gods began as living mortal mages who became too powerful. An intelligent artifact is not already a demonstrated manufactured god.
- Maena knows there is great power associated with Terra, humanity's original homeworld, and specifically Sol. The raid should contain obvious hints of that knowledge.
- **GM-only:** that power is the most powerful Catalyst in the universe. This is not yet a character discovery. The exact relationship between Sol's recorded apparent ending, the closed route, and the present power is not explained by this ruling.

These corrections make research disruption and the Saezo takeover packet optional gains alongside the primary investigation. They do not identify Saezo's intended Catalyst with Maena's manufactured god, connect every memory mechanism, or resolve Yraina's failed access. The precise way Terra/Sol power could serve Maena's project remains to be established.

## Unresolved questions

Live questions can be answered through play or ordinary preparation:

- How does Yraina attempt to disable or kill the woman, what is the result, and what do the PCs choose next?
- Why did authorization fail despite Yraina's explanation? What evidence could actually distinguish causes?
- What does this specialist want beyond finishing his reading and having his refusal heard? His specialty, name, and desired custody of his memories are not fixed.
- What restoration operations can the acquired equipment reliably perform? Inspection and operating instructions can settle this before voluntary use.
- Which witnesses or records survive a disturbance, and who receives any report?
- How will the party use the discoveries obtained through the now-selected servant-and-wipe infiltration?
