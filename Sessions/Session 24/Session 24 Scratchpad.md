# Session 24 Scratchpad

## Session thesis

Session 24 should convert revelation into leverage.

The party now has:

- proof that **Terra was real**
- proof that **Soma Arata became the Hand of Eterra**
- a physical record they chose to preserve for the **Flame of Knowledge**
- a missing companion record labeled **SOL**
- a patron rich enough to change the scale of the campaign overnight

Do not make Session 24 another buried-lore crawl. Make it about what the crew does **after** learning something history was built to hide.

The high-level feel should be:

- strategic instead of exploratory
- pressured instead of leisurely
- choice-rich instead of linear
- full of dangerous opportunity

## Start state from Session 23

- The observatory record stated that **Sol collapsed** and that the road back to Terra had to be closed.
- The record named **Captain Soma Arata** as the one who accepted the **Hand of Eterra**.
- The Hand is framed as a duty of refusal and containment, not prestige.
- The crew recovered **SMD-456cd-e99c2-Ëterra_hist.cc2**.
- The box also had a missing serial: **SMD-156cd-c13c1-SOL.cc2**.
- Asha and Kaelith got a sharp Aashitak memory-fragment:  
  **"Eterra chose to die so we COULD fight."**
- The party decided to **keep the disk** and ultimately try to deliver it to the **Flame of Knowledge**.
- Back on the Lone Star, Lysa framed three major paths:
  - understand the freighter spell
  - understand Spark
  - pursue Terra
- Nyrix immediately keyed on the Flame of Knowledge and implied there is more to learn there.
- The crew then returned to Saezo Ekron, gave him the **Maena** answer and the spiritual-control angle, and got paid in a much bigger way than expected.
- Saezo cleared their obligations and promised:
  - **Lone Star** refit / uplift
  - **Mark IX spell armor** for the marines
  - matching weapons
  - high-grade battle supplies
  - payment of **Kaelith's staff invoice**

## What Session 24 needs to accomplish

Pick **two or three** of these and let the rest stay live:

1. Turn the Hand record into a living political problem, not just a solved mystery.
2. Put real pressure on the choice to keep the disk.
3. Give Saezo's wealth and patronage a cost, a timeline, or a new expectation.
4. Advance the **Maena** front through pressure, surveillance, contact, or cleanup.
5. Make the missing **SOL** record matter.
6. Let the party choose what campaign lane they want to push next.
7. Reintroduce **Governor Rourke** as a Ternan political actor who wants a controlled version of the truth.

## Core truths to preserve

- **The Hand of Eterra is an obligation, not a throne.**
- **Terra should remain emotionally enormous and practically distant.**
- **Maena is still the best modern culprit, but not the full ancient answer.**
- **Spark is now too large to ignore as an industrial and moral problem.**
- **Saezo is useful, but that does not make him safe.**
- **The missing SOL record is a better immediate lead than trying to solve Terra outright.**

## Best overall framing

Treat Session 24 as a **strategy session under pressure** with one live external complication.

That means the session works best if it contains:

- one major discussion with teeth
- one concrete new problem
- one meaningful choice about direction
- one forward-moving consequence by the end

## Use modular beats, not fixed scenes

These are ingredients. Use the ones the players pull on.

## Module A - Custody of the Hand record

### Purpose

Force the group to define what the disk means to them.

### Questions this module should put on the table

- Do they still intend to give it to the Flame of Knowledge?
- Do they trust Xal and Nara enough for that?
- Do they copy it, split it, hide it, or keep it whole?
- Who gets to decide what knowledge is too dangerous to circulate?

### Good outcomes

- The party agrees on a provisional chain of custody.
- The disk becomes a moral and strategic object, not backpack loot.
- Someone voices the fear that preserving truth and weaponizing truth may become the same thing.

### Strong tension points

- Asha worries about future consequences.
- Kaelith sees the value of protecting knowledge from the wrong hands.
- Tamsin pushes for the practical option.
- Seraphine may distrust state archives but still distrust divine custody.
- Nyrix may react to the topic in a way that implies Kumoshi is adjacent to the same secret.

## Module B - Saezo's patronage becomes real

### Purpose

Make the reward feel huge, useful, and slightly dangerous.

### Do not run this as a shopping scene.

Run it as a **power shift**.

### What this module should establish

- Saezo can materially change what the crew is capable of.
- That level of support invites attention.
- If he is investing this hard, he expects future usefulness.
- The crew now risks becoming a deniable anti-Maena asset whether they say yes or not.

### Good hooks

- A refit liaison wants specs, timelines, or operational priorities.
- Saezo asks what they plan to hit next before he commits the full package.
- Someone on the ship realizes this kind of money changes how other factions read the Lone Star.
- Kaelith's staff arriving soon makes him a power spike and a target.

### Best use

This module works well near the front of the session because it raises the stakes on every later choice.

## Module C - The missing SOL record

### Purpose

Turn the observatory from a closed answer into an open wound.

### Key idea

The recovered Ëterra record explained **why the way home was closed**.

The missing **SOL** record may explain one of these:

- what actually happened to Sol
- what "bled at the edge of the map"
- why Terra itself became dangerous
- who removed the record later and why

### Best interpretation

The missing record is not just absent. It was **taken**.

That means someone else already understood this place mattered.

### What this should imply

- Maena may not be first on this trail.
- Ternus may have stripped some truths but missed others.
- Xal / Nara may have known part of this and left the crew only enough to move.
- Soma Arata's later actions may make more sense once the SOL record is found.

### Do not answer yet

Do not reveal the full SOL truth in Session 24.

Only establish that:

- the record matters
- the removal was likely intentional
- finding it is now a viable major objective

## Module D - Choose the next operational lane

### Purpose

Let the players set direction without needing you to lock the session into one plot track.

Present the available routes clearly, then let one become primary.

| Route | What it answers | What it costs / risks | Good NPCs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flame of Knowledge** | custody of the Hand disk, Xal/Nara's foreknowledge, dragon-scale breadcrumbs | divine politics, trust questions, giving up exclusive control of the record | Nyrix, Lysa, Asha |
| **Freighter / Joren / aux core** | what the sabotage actually did, how Eterra tech met modern systems, who touched what | spirit risk, forensic complexity, possible direct Maena proof | Lysa, Seraphine, Kaelith |
| **Spark route** | what Spark really is, why Ternus industrialized it, why the freighter may have been headed toward the trade moon | major faction attention, moral horror, economic power structures | Asha, Yraina, Saezo |
| **War Archive / bill of sale** | formal proof chain, buyer / manufacturer history, state cover-up depth | Ternus scrutiny, bureaucracy, surveillance | Seraphine, Kaelith, Tamsin |
| **Maena counterplay** | what Kyrsa wants now, why Yraina matters, how active the family is on this trail | personal exposure, retaliation, false hospitality | Yraina, Saezo, Asha |
| **Terra / Soma / Kumoshi** | ancient context, Dream-memory link, deeper meaning of the Hand | high abstraction, hard answers, risk of overexposure too soon | Nyrix, Lysa, Kaelith |

Recommended approach: let the party choose **one primary route** and let **one secondary route** advance offscreen.

## Module E - Governor Rourke contacts the crew

### Purpose

Make the Hand record immediately matter to **state secrecy, morale, and narrative control** instead of just archaeology.

### Why this works now

- Kaelith already invoked the governor while requesting the derelict freighter bill of sale.
- Ternus has strong habits around archive control, memory policy, and bureaucratic redaction.
- Rourke already knows the crew as useful, inconvenient folk heroes.
- The party now has exactly the kind of truth a governor would want managed before it spreads.

### Best way Rourke knows something is wrong

Use **partial awareness**, not full knowledge.

He does not need to know they recovered the Hand disk specifically.
He only needs enough to justify contact:

- Kaelith's request hit a level that genuinely required executive review.
- The observatory site or related archive trail flagged as an old sealed category once someone touched it.
- The Lone Star crew is already watched more closely than average because Rourke's office has used them for morale optics before.
- Seraphine or another Ternus contact gets a polite but unmistakable "tell the captain to answer" message.

### What Rourke wants

- To learn whether the crew found something that threatens Ternus policy, legitimacy, or public stability.
- To keep explosive truth from reaching the public before he understands the risk.
- To decide whether the crew should be treated as citizens, assets, embarrassments, or hazards.
- To convert a raw discovery into a manageable political outcome.

### What he should not know yet

- The full contents of the Hand disk.
- The full Terra / Sol implication.
- Whether the crew intends to give the disk to the Flame of Knowledge.

### What he can offer

- War Archive access.
- Signed authorizations and custody overrides.
- Quiet military cover.
- Official legitimacy for the crew's next move.
- A controlled channel for "sensitive discoveries."

### What he wants in return

- First access to the shape of the truth.
- Limits on who else gets told.
- Cooperation framed as citizenship or public duty.
- The right to decide what becomes classified.

### Pressure this creates on the PCs

- If they tell him too much, they risk losing control of the record.
- If they tell him too little, they risk becoming suspicious.
- If they openly defy him, they risk waking the whole Ternus state apparatus.
- If they work with him, they gain leverage fast but become entangled in Ternan politics.

### Session goals for the contact

Use the call to do **three** things:

1. Confirm that the Hand record is now politically alive.
2. Offer something the crew genuinely wants so the choice is not trivial.
3. Force the crew to decide whether Rourke gets truth, a lie, or a curated half-truth.

### Best tone for Rourke

He should feel:

- polished
- intelligent
- candid when it helps him
- unflappable under insult
- fundamentally political even when sounding sincere

He is strongest when he sounds like a man who can admit ugly truths while still trying to manage them.

## Pressure fronts

Use fronts, not mandatory encounters.

## Front 1 - Maena response

### What changed

- Yraina is attached to the crew.
- Saezo now knows Maena sabotaged the freighter.
- The crew recovered Eterra-adjacent truth.

### How Maena can respond

- polite contact through Yraina
- surveillance
- scrub teams cleaning evidence
- market pressure through Saezo's interests
- an offer that is really a leash

### Best version for Session 24

Keep it cool, elegant, and controlled.

Maena should feel like a family that assumes it can still manage the situation.

## Front 2 - Spark scale

### What changed

The party has now seen industrial Spark in a way that reframes it from rumor to civilization-level infrastructure.

### What this front should do

- make Spark harder to treat as just a fuel
- connect the freighter sabotage to logistics, not only mysticism
- tempt Asha into following the supply chain
- force Yraina and Kaelith to articulate what Spark means to them

## Front 3 - Truth custody

### What changed

The group chose not to destroy the disk.

### What this front should do

- make that choice costly or weighty
- produce disagreement without forcing party fracture
- create urgency around where the disk is safest

## Front 4 - Saezo's expectations

### What changed

Saezo invested at a level that effectively promotes the crew into a more important class of actors.

### What this front should do

- make the crew feel newly powerful
- make them feel newly visible
- remind them that patrons create gravity

## Front 5 - Ternus narrative control

### What changed

The crew has moved from "useful survivors" to "people who may have touched one of the reasons Ternus became what it is."

### What this front should do

- bring Governor Rourke back into play without making him omniscient
- make archive secrecy feel current and active
- pressure the crew to define how much of the Hand truth belongs to a state
- let Ternus behave like a nervous government, not a cartoon conspiracy

## Best NPC uses

## Asha

- Use her to frame the ethical core: what knowledge should survive, and who deserves it.
- Her reaction to wealth disparity after giving Joren's wife one million is important. Saezo's scale should keep bothering her.
- She is well positioned to drive the Spark route or the custody debate.

## Kaelith

- Use him for pattern recognition, ancient framing, and refusal to let truth get simplified.
- His staff and Saezo's payment should make him feel the reality of newfound resources.
- He should be one of the first people to care that **SOL** is the missing record, not just a missing object.

## Yraina

- She is the cleanest bridge into Maena pressure.
- Her usefulness should now be rising at exactly the same speed as her danger.
- She should know enough to recognize style, tone, and family methods, but not enough to neutralize them alone.

## Seraphine

- Use her to connect ancient secrecy to modern Ternus policy.
- She is strong in any War Archive or anti-surveillance angle.
- She should distrust both Ternus and naive divine handoff.

## Tamsin

- Use her to stop the session from dissolving into pure lore.
- She asks the best operational question: "What do we do with this now?"
- She is ideal for arguing that one path should become actionable immediately.

## Rho

- He matters more if the session stays grounded.
- Use him as a voice for what all this means to ordinary Terrans who never asked for ancient destiny.
- He can also be the first to ask whether power is about to make the crew stupid.

## Lysa

- Use her to keep the freighter and Joren routes alive.
- She can frame the difference between spirit contamination, memory architecture, and whatever Eterra was.
- She is one of the best people to say, "Do not confuse resonance with understanding."

## Nyrix

- Use her lightly.
- She should not solve Terra.
- She is best as a signal that Kumoshi or Dream-memory architecture overlaps this mystery in uncomfortable ways.
- If she reacts, it should deepen the route rather than explain it.

## Saezo

- He should feel generous, sharp, and never fully transparent.
- He does not need to betray anyone to stay dangerous.
- His greatest threat is that he may honestly like the crew and still use them.

## Governor Rourke

- He is not a hidden mastermind; he is a skilled political survivor sitting on top of an unhealthy system.
- He thinks in terms of morale, leverage, classification, and what the public can survive hearing.
- He is very good at making state interests sound like civic duty.
- He can be honest about ugly tradeoffs without becoming trustworthy.
- He should pressure the crew with usefulness, not with cartoon threats.
- He is an ideal way to make the Hand record a **Ternus problem** before it becomes a galaxy problem.

## Reveals worth holding in reserve

Use one, not all.

### Reveal option 1 - Maena reaches out first

Kyrsa or a Maena intermediary sends a message that is too calm to be reassuring.

Best effect:

- validates Yraina's fears
- proves the crew is now visible
- turns the Maena route into something active

### Reveal option 2 - The SOL trail is already moving

Someone else recently searched, moved, bought, or fenced an old human storage format connected to Sol.

Best effect:

- makes the missing record a race
- creates forward motion without forcing a fight

### Reveal option 3 - Saezo wants prioritization

His support is real, but he wants to know which target the crew is pursuing first so he can "help."

Best effect:

- turns resources into commitment
- forces strategic choice

### Reveal option 4 - Nyrix recognizes a pattern, not an answer

She reacts to one phrase, image, or cadence from the disk in a way that implies Kumoshi fenced this knowledge on purpose.

Best effect:

- keeps the Terra route alive
- preserves mystery

### Reveal option 5 - Rourke wants a secure line

The governor's office reaches out with courtesy so polished it is almost coercive.

Best effect:

- confirms Ternus noticed something
- gives the crew a political decision instead of just a tactical one
- lets the party bargain with the state before the state escalates

## What not to do yet

- Do not fully explain Terra.
- Do not make Soma available for a clean conversation.
- Do not fully define Eterra as only a person, only a protocol, or only a god-adjacent force.
- Do not resolve Maena's role all at once.
- Do not let Saezo become either a perfect patron or an obvious villain.
- Do not let the new upgrades eat the whole session.

## Good end-session buttons

Pick whichever route the players leaned toward and end with a consequence that confirms the choice matters.

### Button A - A Maena contact

An elegant message arrives for Yraina before the crew can feel settled.

The point is not immediate violence.  
The point is: **they know.**

### Button B - A War Archive movement

Kaelith or Seraphine gets notice that the bill-of-sale request has moved, been blocked, or been approved under suspicious terms.

The point is: **Ternus is awake now.**

### Button C - A Spark opportunity

Saezo offers a controlled look at a Spark source, flow, or refinement site.

The point is: **the scale of the problem is now in reach.**

### Button D - The SOL lead surfaces

A fragment, serial match, salvage rumor, or private listing tied to **SMD-156cd-c13c1-SOL.cc2** appears.

The point is: **the next truth can be chased.**

### Button E - The governor calls personally

Rourke does not accuse.
He simply says he believes the crew has found something old enough to make modern people stupid, and he would prefer to speak before that happens.

The point is: **Ternus wants the truth managed, not merely found.**

## Practical recommendation

If you want the cleanest Session 24 shape:

1. Open with **custody + next-step debate** on the Lone Star.
2. Let **Saezo's support** become concrete enough to change the crew's expectations.
3. Drop **one active pressure** from Maena, Ternus, SOL, or Rourke.
4. End with the party choosing the next route and seeing its first consequence immediately.

That keeps the session high-level, player-driven, and full of momentum without forcing a scene-by-scene script.
