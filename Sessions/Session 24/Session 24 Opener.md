## Session 24 — Opening

---

**Narrator:**
Saezo walks back toward his Spark pumps without looking at you again. Business concluded. That's what that was.

The corridor outside is quieter than it should be. The gold light from the industrial glass dies somewhere around your boots. You are already turning back toward the lift. Back toward the Lone Star.

Nobody says much at first.

There is not much to say after major revelations, a cleared debt, and a man who treated 285 million like a dock fee.

---

**Narrator:**
The elevator ride up is short.

Yraina is still watching her slate when the invoice clears. A clean transfer. Kaelith's staff, paid in full.

Then her comm pings again. Soft. Neat. Professional.

She checks it, stops walking, and lifts the slate just enough for the others to see.

> *Commission complete. Item ready for collection.*

---

**Narrator: Yraina**
"It's ready."

---

**Narrator:**
That lands in the space between all of you and just sits there.

The Lone Star is waiting. Bigger problems are waiting. Bigger choices too.

And now Kaelith's staff is ready.

Kaelith, that old tightness is back in your jaw. The one that shows up when power gets close enough to touch. Asha, you know what that staff cost. You also know the man who just erased that cost without blinking.

The crew has a ship to return to.

They also have a new weapon three minutes away.

---

**Narrator:**
The lift doors open to the trade moon promenade. One way leads back to the docks and the Lone Star.

The other leads to the runeworks.
