# Ternus News Streams (as received en route back to New Texas)

> Short bursts, reposts, and official bulletins the crew's feeds pick up while Ternus is still on fire.

- **Mainstream Live Feed (TernusNet News):** The anchor tosses to a field reporter in a pressure-rated jacket, visor reflecting drifting sparks and emergency strobes, standing behind a cordon line on the surviving ring. Her voice keeps the practiced calm of someone reading tragedy for a living: "Behind me is what remains of Bay Seventeen; rescue crews are still cutting access routes through buckled plating, and Orbital Authority confirms the missing list will keep growing as sealed compartments are opened." The feed is full of clean graphics; casualty estimates, lane closures, and a looping "violet event" timeline. The audio catches real details they cannot polish; distant hull groans, shouted med calls, and the constant hiss of fire-suppressant foam.

- **Ternus Orbital Authority: Emergency Bulletin:** Dock Ring segments 12-19 declared lost; emergency traffic lanes active; all civilian traffic rerouted. Expect multi-day berth delays and intermittent comms jamming.

- **Confederacy Dispatch (Public):** "Incident under investigation." No confirmation of hostile foreign fleet action. Requesting all private vessels submit sensor logs and flight telemetry from the last 48 hours.

- **Dockworker Unioncast:** Casualty lists still incomplete. Volunteer crews needed for seal-welding, air scrubber swaps, and triage. Hazard pay approved, but "paperwork is a graveyard."

- **Trade and Shipping Ticker:** Docking fees surge; cargo insurance freezes payouts for "anomalous-force events" pending review. Salvage claims spike as scavengers circle the debris field.

- **Crimewatch / Scanner Reposters:** Multiple eyewitness feeds mention a "violet shear" before the blast. The most specific clips get scrubbed within minutes. Rumors of a quiet blackout order.

- **Conspiracy Stream (Tin-Foil Beacon):** "It wasn't the freighter; it was the station." Claims the station ribs lit up and the docks were "burned open from inside the structure."

- **Inuran Watch (Translated):** Trade Moon arrival delayed "for security review." Merchants deny involvement. Black market chatter predicts a buying frenzy once the Moon lands.

- **Grey-Market Price Boards:** Dream scale valuations jump; stealth-coating kits go invite-only. Spell-armor components sell out across the belt.

- **Veteran Network / War Rumor Channels:** Old war names resurface. Chatter about tainted lots moving again; unverified, but the tone shifts from gossip to fear.

- **Human Interest Feed:** Memorial walls go up in surviving dock sectors; families post last comms and missing-person calls. Donation drives are immediately targeted by scammers.
