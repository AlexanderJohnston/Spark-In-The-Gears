# Lone Star–Class Spell-Battleship (Confederacy surplus refit)

**Role:** Capital ship / fleet anchor / Catalyst-extraction platform  
**Scale:** ~1.2 km long, ~220 m beam (widest point), ~18 decks (varies by section)  
**Crew:** ~180 standard / **45 skeleton crew** (w/ heavy automation)
**Embarked:** 2 shuttles + 6 spellfighters *(or 1 dropship + 4 spellfighters)*; optional marine platoon

## Exterior Description (table-ready)

A brutal, slab-sided capital hull with a **raised dorsal spine** that houses the spell-lance focusing rails, and a ventral “keel trench” packed with heat sinks, tether reels, and external service catwalks. The **bow** is all sensor geometry—layered plates around a forward aperture array—while the **stern** is dominated by clustered drive bells and a ring of spell-coil “throat” structures that glow when the ship throttles up.

---

## General Layout (macro)

Treat the ship as **five functional cities** in a line:

1. **Bow:** Sensors + forward weapons
2. **Command Citadel:** bridge / CIC / command spaces
3. **Habitat Spine:** crew life, medical, admin
4. **Industrial Midships:** hangars, cargo, shops
5. **Engineering Mountain + Stern:** cores, drives, coils, aft weapons

---

## Compartment-by-Compartment (deck grouping)

### 1) Bow / Prow (Decks 1–4)
- **Prow Sensor Cathedral:** multi-aspect sensor stacks, passive arrays, occult resonance baffles.
- **Astrogation Vault:** void-route plotting, encrypted charts, no-nonsense access controls.
- **Forward Battery Rooms:** turret mechanisms, capacitor banks, ammo feeds.
- **External EVA Lock (Bow):** rapid-response airlock for sensor repairs under fire.

### 2) Command Citadel (Decks 5–7)
- **Bridge (Primary Helm):** pilot/helm chair, tactical holo, hardline manual controls.
- **CIC / Tactical Operations:** gunnery coordination, targeting, threat board, firing permissions.
- **Signals + EW:** encryption, spoofing, jamming, quiet-burn comm discipline.
- **Captain’s Ready Room:** private planning, secure calls, crisis briefings.
- **Senior Staff Briefing Theater:** stepped room for maps, arguments, and casualty math.
- **Black Box + Records Safe:** immutable logs, contracts, warrants, “what really happened.”

### 3) Spell Systems Complex (Decks 8–10)
- **Universal Matrix Hall A / B:** two chambers with shock-absorbed dais rings for cooperative casting.
- **Offense Matrix Chamber (“The Rail”):** narrow, reinforced, aligned with the dorsal spine lance.
- **Defense Matrix Chamber (“The Choir”):** circular ward-projector lattice; chapel/server-room vibes.
- **Matrix Control Booths:** non-mage monitoring of stability, heat, backlash thresholds.
- **Reagent & Conductor Vaults:** crystals, inks, etched plates, replacement focusing rings.

### 4) Habitation Spine (Decks 11–14)
- **Crew Berths:** modular bunk bays (hot-bunk capable during war footing).
- **Officer Quarters:** small, private, better noise insulation.
- **Galley + Mess:** industrial kitchen; multiple mess halls.
- **Hydroponics / Fresh Air Garden:** morale-critical; emergency oxygen buffering.
- **Medbay + Trauma Pods:** surgery; burn treatment; anti-magic contamination protocol kits.
- **Brig + Interview Room:** restraints rated for mages; separate evidence locker.
- **Chapel / Quiet Room:** multi-faith space; often repurposed for memorials.

### 5) Marine / Security Zone (Decks 15–16)
- **Armory:** small-arms, spellshields, breaching charges; biometric locks.
- **Marine Berthing + Ready Lockers:** rapid deployment gear staged by team.
- **Boarding Tube / Clamp Access:** hard suits, mag-seals, external boarding procedures.
- **Internal Security Control:** cameras, door control, segmented lockdown systems.

### 6) Industrial Midships (Decks 17–18 + hangar volumes)
- **Main Hangar:** large bay with overhead crane and mag-clamps.
- **Aux Hangar / Boat Bay:** utility shuttles, drones, salvage skiffs.
- **Cargo Holds:** container rails; hazardous cargo isolation cells; refrigerated vault.
- **Machine Shop + Fab:** printing, milling, emergency part manufacture.
- **Damage Control Lockers:** foam, patch plates, sealant, portable ward emitters.

### 7) Engineering Mountain / Stern (heavily segmented)
- **Power Core Vaults:** four isolated pits with blast doors + suppression fields.
- **Drive Control:** throttle management, heat routing, thrust-vector authority.
- **Spelldrive Coil Galleries:** glowing conduit runs; maintenance-heavy spaces.
- **Heat Sink Trenches:** venting, radiators, coolant reservoirs, external fin actuators.
- **Aft Battery + PD Control:** stern-facing turrets and point-defense coordination.
- **Stern EVA Lock + Tow Rig:** salvage operations, towing, ship-to-ship umbilicals.
