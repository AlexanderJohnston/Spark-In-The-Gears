# Open Investigations (Player Cheat Sheet)

> Status: **what we know / what we don’t** as of the end of Session 5.

## 1) The Ternus Dock Blast ("purple light")
- **What happened:** The docks were obliterated during the freighter handover; comms jammed; chaos covered a lot of tracks.
- **Open questions:**
  - Was the blast triggered from **inside the freighter**, or did it come from the **station itself**?
  - Who (if anyone) *planned* it, and why?
  - What exactly did Joren do at the bridge matrices right before he died?

## 2) The Too-Convenient Tow Contract
- **What happened:** Asha was paid unusually well to tow an Inuran stealth freighter that “nobody should’ve known was there.”
- **Open questions:**
  - **Who hired Asha**, and how did they know the freighter’s location?
  - What did they want: **the hull**, **the cargo**, **the dream scale(s)**, or something else?
  - Are they still looking for it now that the contract was effectively blown apart?

## 3) The Inuran Stealth Freighter (and its ghost)
- **What happened:** The freighter was a war-era supply ship loaded with spell gear, munitions, stealth coating, dream scales, etc.
- **Open questions:**
  - Where is the freighter *now* (and what’s left on it)?
  - Why was it carrying a war-cache big enough to reshape the region’s black market?

## 4) The Catalyst Map Dream Scale
- **What happened:** A “dream scale” showed a **map of catalysts** and implied someone knew we’d be involved.
- **Open questions:**
  - **Who made the map**, and how could it show us *before* events happened?
  - Why did it highlight a **Dream** target so aggressively?
  - What else is hidden on the scale besides the map/augury we’ve already seen?

## 5) Aashitak (Dream God-Corpse) and what we learned inside
- **What happened:** We reached the nerve column and gained **Dream catalyzation** + **Essence Sight**.
- **Open questions:**
  - What does “Essence Sight” reveal that we haven’t learned to interpret yet?
  - What, exactly, changed in us (capabilities, side effects, dependencies)?
  - Why did Aashitak’s memory frame us as her “children” (or at least recognized us)?

## 6) The Half-Slumbering (Guardian / Wyrm)
- **What happened:** The Guardian hunts us, claims authority over the domain, and is fixated on Kaelith’s armor.
- **Open questions:**
  - Is it *actually* Aashitak wearing stolen Dream, or something else claiming the name?
  - Why could it threaten us but not simply kill us immediately ("can’t touch you, but they will")?
  - What did it mean by: **“her secret dies with you”**?

## 7) “Nefarious”
- **What we’ve heard:** A name delivered like a warning; described as an ancient, god-level hunger (“Devourer of Systems”).
- **Open questions:**
  - What is **Nefarious**, and what does it have to do with Aashitak’s death?
  - What does it have to do with Kaelith’s armor ("wearing it on your skin")?
  - Where can we find **archives** or reliable intel about it?

## 8) Kaelith’s Inuran Mark V Spell Armor
- **What happened:** The armor has a “magic meter,” drinks power, has void-storage behaviors, and unlocked a god-killing siphon/beam mode.
- **Open questions:**
  - Who built it, and for what war/theater?
  - Why does it behave like it has an **appetite**?
  - What does “fully charged” actually mean long-term (costs, risks, corruption)?
