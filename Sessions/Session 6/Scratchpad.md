In the quiet after escape, the ship rides dark in the Umbral Depths and the adrenaline finally drains out, leaving an awful emptiness where Black Trace's voices should be. Asha rips off her helmet, raw and shaking, trying to process that her ship is gone; Rho stays in captain mode and does the impossible anyway, keys the intercom, calls for status checks, and lets the silence answer, while Lysa keeps a hand on the spell matrix like it's the only solid thing left, murmuring that "Aashitak chose" and tasting the Catalyst in the back of her throat. Kaelith, still rattled by how hungry his Inuran armor felt, cannot stop replaying the Guardian's warning and starts scanning the suit for what it really is. And then, just for a breath, the pressure breaks as talk turns practical, Lone Star as a stop, New Texas shipyards for a replacement, and the five of them alive, bruised, and bonded fall into gallows humor and tentative plans about what kind of ship they'll buy and what it would mean to become a real crew.
