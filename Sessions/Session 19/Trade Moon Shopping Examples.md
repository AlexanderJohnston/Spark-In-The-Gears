# Trade Moon Shopping Examples

GM-facing example stock for what the crew could find on the Trade Moon.

Use the following files in `Rules\` as the final source of truth for standard item stats and base prices:

- `Rules\armor-cheat-sheet.md`
- `Rules\weapons-cheat-sheet.md`
- `Rules\equipment-cheat-sheet.md`
- `Rules\ships-cheat-sheet.md`

This file is for **Trade Moon presentation**: where an item is likely sold, what sort of seller carries it, what looks scarce, what feels illicit, and what bespoke relics or prestige wares might tempt the crew.

## General pricing mood

> **Spark surge price spike (current):** All prices in this document have already been multiplied above their cheat-sheet baselines to reflect the current Trade Moon market. Tier 1-2 stock is roughly **5x** baseline, scaling smoothly up to **10x** for Tier 8 prestige and relic gear. The Spark surge from Ternus, the Inuran consortium's bidding war over rare goods, and several quiet hoarding moves by mid-guild buyers have all collided. Crews who walked the Trade Moon a year ago will not recognize the price tags. Sellers know it, and they are not embarrassed about it.

- **Surface Docks:** Common gear, basic weapons, loaded consumables, and opportunistic resale. Prices range from inflated to shameless.
- **Black Exchange Levels:** Rare, stolen, deniable, cursed, customized, or politically embarrassing goods. Expect surge markups, favors, or strings on top of the standard markup.
- **Orange Market Levels:** Legitimate merchants, guild counters, reputable artificers, and clean paperwork. Surge pricing is openly priced in and openly defended.
- **Saezo / Yraina access:** They do not make things cheap. They make scarce things *available*. In a surge market that is its own kind of currency.

## Invented Trade Moon Stock - Tiered Catalog

The cheat-sheet items above are the published baseline. The Trade Moon also moves a flood of bespoke, prestige, prototype, and relic gear that does not appear in the rules files. The tiers below are a soft 1-8 power band ladder for *Trade Moon presentation only* - they map roughly to existing Marks but extend past the published top end.

Soft mapping reference:
- **Tier 1-2:** Civilian / dock-grade. Comparable to NTM Cavalier Light, Mark III Laspistol.
- **Tier 3-4:** Professional / merc-grade. Comparable to Mark V-VII Laspistol, Cavalier Heavy, Light Spellshield.
- **Tier 5-6:** Elite Inuran / guild standard. Comparable to Mark III-V Inuran spell weapons and spellarmor.
- **Tier 7:** Top published Inuran production. Comparable to Mark VII / Mark IX Standard Spellarmor.
- **Tier 8:** Beyond the cheat sheet. Prototypes, custom relics, godseed work, things that should not exist twice.

GMs should treat all stat numbers below as *rough targets in the published grammar*, not balanced drops. Tune to the table.

### How to use the tier ladder in play

- **Default crew comfort zone:** Tiers 5-6. That matches their current arsenal and current spending power.
- **Aspirational stretch buys:** Tier 7. They can probably afford one or two pieces if they liquidate.
- **Tier 8 should always cost more than money.** Favors, public visibility, political alignment, debt to Saezo, attention from rival guilds, or commitment to a faction.
- **Saezo's tells:** He will quietly steer interested buyers toward Tier 8 items he wants tied to the crew. Yraina will not be subtle when an item *has been chosen for them*.
- **Black Exchange variants:** Anything in Tier 5+ can appear on the Black Exchange with a custom name, a discount, and a complication.

## Fast complications tied to shopping

- A desired item is available, but only if the crew accepts a public fitting, visible bid war, or politically awkward seller.
- A relic is clearly real, but its provenance points back to someone dangerous.
- A standard item from the Rules sheet is available immediately, but only in faction colors or with recognizable guild branding.
- Yraina can get something rare on short notice, which says more about Saezo's reach than the crew may like.
- A Black Exchange seller recognizes the crew from the Ternus dock incident and adjusts the pitch accordingly.
