# Session 23 Scratchpad

## Session thesis

Session 23 should turn the Hand of Eterra from a hidden archive reference into a physical, dangerous place. The crew is leaving the public archive with coordinates to a buried pre-Ternus research observatory, Rho as their only practical guide, Yraina freshly exposed as Kyrsa Maena's estranged daughter, and Ternus under a hard-casting ban.

Keep the session about descent, old truth, and modern pressure. The players should feel like they are going below the current city and below the current version of history at the same time.

Do not answer everything. Give them a concrete Hand of Eterra reveal, a strong Terra/Eterra/Soma thread, and one new problem that points forward to Maena, the freighter, or Nyrix.

## Start state from Session 22

- The active group is **Asha, Kaelith, Seraphine, Yraina, Tamsin, and Rho**.
- They are in a six-seat Ternus vehicle that Rho has reprogrammed beyond normal operating zones.
- Destination: a route down toward a huge cavern in an old buried city.
- Target: the last known location of **SMD-456cd-e99c2-Ëterra_hist.cc2**, an old pre-Ternus storage reference tied to the Hand of Eterra.
- Rho recognized the coordinates because he explored the lower layers as a child.
- Ternus has banned hard casting for two weeks and claims it can detect spell use.
- Kaelith filed an official request for the derelict freighter bill of sale, claiming the purpose was assisting the Ternan military at the governor's behest.
- Asha learned Yraina is the estranged daughter of **Kyrsa Maena** and tentatively accepted Yraina's explanation.
- Asha sent **one million credits** to Joren Arden's surviving wife.
- The freighter leads still open: aux core, Joren's body, remaining ship sweep, bridge hardware, Pell Vask, dock workers.

## Carry-forward truths

- Maena is the modern hand on the knife, but the knife itself is older.
- The old freighter's anti-magic system is tied to Nefarius-blood warships and non-mage operation.
- Talifax brought the Nefarius fleet weapon into the world and died under impossible intergalactic circumstances.
- Soma Arata was born on Terra and served as a captain in the original crews who left Terra.
- Kumoshi taught Ternus memory wiping and may be one of the few living connections to memories of Terra.
- Nyrix may matter to Terra through Kumoshi, but do not overuse her in this session unless the players bring her in.
- Maena cybernetic interface work encodes spells as executable packets fueled by Spark.
- Kemet, emergency interface kits, and Maena wartime contracts remain unresolved but related.
- The cleanest framing for the Eterra file is not "ancient title lore" but **the mercy protocol**: a containment doctrine for teaching a wound not to open.

## Opening beat - the vehicle leaves the clean city

### Read-Aloud (Primary)

Narrator:
The taxi gives up pretending this is a normal fare three minutes after Rho touches the route controls.

The city outside changes by degrees. Gold-lit commercial fronts become concrete service mouths. Concrete becomes old steel. Old steel becomes something older still, ribs of infrastructure laid over ribs of infrastructure until Ternus stops looking like a planet and starts looking like a machine that forgot what it was built to protect.

The vehicle chirps once.

> Destination outside standard civic transit envelope. Cleaning fee may apply.

Rho leans back like that is the funniest part of the problem.

"See? Told you. Deep clean."

Then the road angles down.

### Immediate prompt

Ask what they do during the ride:

1. **Question Rho** about the lower city and how he knows this place.
2. **Question Yraina** while there is still privacy after the Maena reveal.
3. **Plan the descent**: armor thrusters, rope, sensors, comms, no hard casting.
4. **Check for surveillance** on the vehicle, route, or Kaelith's archive request.
5. **Call the ship** for backup, research, or a status check from Lysa.

## Pressure structure

### Ternus hard-casting clock - 0 / 6

Start at **0**.

Increase by 1 when:

- A PC hard casts in a monitored area.
- They use obvious spell effects near public infrastructure.
- They tamper with civic sensors in a way Ternus can trace.
- They discuss highly restricted topics on open comms.
- They damage old-city structures badly enough to trigger municipal alerts.

At **2**, the vehicle or route logs are flagged for review.

At **4**, Ternus security pings Seraphine or Kaelith for explanation.

At **5**, a drone or remote enforcement system begins following them.

At **6**, a Ternus response team locks the return route or demands surrender to a checkpoint.

### Deep-city hazard clock - 0 / 6

Start at **0** when the vehicle leaves normal roads.

Increase by 1 when:

- They take loud shortcuts.
- They ignore Rho's route advice.
- They descend without checking old structures.
- They disturb scavenger territory.
- They activate dormant systems in the observatory.

At **2**, they draw attention from lower-city locals.

At **4**, the descent path becomes physically dangerous: falling debris, bad air, collapsing gantry, or old ward interference.

At **6**, they face a hard obstacle on the way out: ambush, locked lift, failing dome segment, or alert drones.

### Kaelith's request clock - 0 / 4

Start at **0**.

Tick once at a dramatically useful pause, then again whenever the party spends time waiting, making noise, or connecting to official networks.

At **1**, Kaelith gets a receipt update: "Escalated for executive authorization."

At **2**, Seraphine gets an uncomfortable ping asking whether she knows the requester.

At **3**, the governor's office acknowledges the request and asks for a secure call window.

At **4**, Governor Saezo signs it, denies it, or signs it with a joke that creates new trouble.

Recommended outcome: **Saezo signs it because it causes useful political discomfort**, but the record access is delayed until after the observatory scene or requires in-person retrieval at the War Archive.

## Scene 1 - Rho's route through the buried city

### Purpose

Give Rho texture and make Ternus's lower layers feel lived-in, not just dungeon space.

### Read-Aloud

Narrator:
Rho stops joking when the vehicle passes the first old floodgate.

It is big enough to swallow a house. Bigger, maybe. Its surface is layered with city department tags, gang marks, old military serials, and prayers scratched by people who had no temple and no reason to believe anyone upstairs was listening.

Rho watches it pass.

"This is where the city starts lying about itself," he says.

### Rho can reveal

- He grew up in the lower layers, not the polished Ternus people pretend is the whole world.
- The place ahead is not part of normal civic traffic.
- Old-city people know routes that official maps treat as sealed, collapsed, or nonexistent.
- The coordinates point near an enormous dome-cavern he saw as a kid but never entered.
- He is embarrassed by this history but not ashamed of surviving it.
- If pushed for specifics, Rho once reached the dome as a teenager by following someone he should not have followed. He saw it from above through a maintenance grate and never went in. Until now, he assumed it was structural.

### Roll options

- **Rho navigation:** 4d6 or Streetwise DT 3.
- **Asha / Kaelith reading the route:** Investigation, Systems, or Perception DT 3.
- **Tamsin security scan:** Perception or Tactics DT 3.
- **Seraphine civic knowledge:** Bureaucracy or History & Lore DT 3.
- **Yraina social read:** Streetwise or Insight DT 3.

### Successes

- **Success:** safe route to the old dome access without official checkpoint.
- **2+ net:** spot a bypass around a monitored service gate.
- **3+ net:** identify a return path in case the taxi cannot come back.
- **Failure:** arrive safely, but tick Deep-city hazard +1 or Ternus hard-casting +1 from route logging.

### Optional lower-city contact

If the scene needs a face, use **Mara Venn**, an old-route broker who recognizes Rho.

Mara:
"Rho Anselm. Look at that. You got taller and found expensive friends."

Use Mara to offer:

- A warning about recent clean white cases moving below.
- A rumor that Ternus sealed the dome because people who slept near it dreamed of an ocean.
- A quick route for a price or favor.

Do not force this scene if pacing wants the descent now.

## Scene 2 - The top of the cavern

### Purpose

Turn the coordinate into a physical set piece and make the no-casting constraint matter.

### Read-Aloud

Narrator:
The vehicle stops where the road ends.

Not officially. Officially, the road ended six turns ago.

Here, a maintenance platform juts into darkness. The ceiling above is a stack of old city foundations and pipework. The space below is too wide for your eyes to trust at first.

Then a few amber warning lights blink awake along the rim, one after another, like something huge opening tired eyes.

You are standing at the top of a dome.

The bottom is a long way down.

### Player prompt

How do they descend?

- Armor thrusters.
- Rope and clamps.
- Slow platform rigging.
- Search for old lift systems.
- Risk subtle magic.
- Send one scout first.

### Roll options

- **Thruster descent:** Piloting, Acrobatics, Athletics, or Armor systems DT 3.
- **Group descent coordination:** Tactics, Command, or Engineering DT 3.
- **Find an old lift:** Systems or Engineering DT 4.
- **Read structural risk:** Perception or Engineering DT 3.
- **Hard cast descent aid:** Works, but tick Ternus hard-casting +1 or +2 unless they justify concealment.

### Outcomes

**Clean success:** everyone reaches a lower gantry near the observatory approach.

**Mixed success:** choose one:

- One PC lands separated on a neighboring platform.
- Deep-city hazard +1.
- Equipment scrape, armor cosmetic damage, or lost consumable.
- A lower-city scavenger group sees them.

**Failure:** nobody dies from one bad roll; instead, make the descent dramatic:

- Falling platform.
- Thruster turbulence.
- Old anti-flight baffles flicker on.
- Rho has to talk someone through a blind landing.
- Tamsin anchors a line and curses the entire way down.

### Environmental details

- The air tastes like wet concrete, rust, and old coolant.
- The bottom holds a dead neighborhood built around a crater-like hollow.
- Some buildings are sideways because later city layers shifted over them.
- The research observatory sits at the far side like a sealed eye, half-buried in stone and steel.

## Scene 3 - The people below

### Purpose

Give the lower city a living complication. This should be avoidable, negotiable, or brief, not a mandatory combat slog.

### Setup

Rho was right: there are people who will try to kill you for gear, but not every person below is a bandit. Some are scavengers, squatters, route-keepers, or families avoiding the upper city.

Use one of these depending on tone:

| Option | Use when | What they want |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route-keepers | You want social texture | Toll, respect, Rho's name |
| Gear thieves | You want a quick action beat | Armor pieces, weapons, credits |
| Old-city family | You want moral texture | Medicine, safe passage, no trouble |
| White-case watchers | You want Maena pressure | Observe the party, report back |

### Route-keeper read-aloud

Narrator:
The first voice comes from above you, which is annoying because you just spent all that effort getting down.

"Pretty armor," someone calls from a broken apartment balcony. "Bad place to show it off."

Rho does not draw.

That is probably the only reason nobody gets shot in the first three seconds.

### Roll options

- Persuasion, Streetwise, or Intimidation DT 3.
- Asha can spend money to bypass the problem cleanly.
- Rho can grant +1 die if the approach respects lower-city rules.
- Tamsin can identify ambush angles.
- Yraina can quietly point out if anyone is wearing subsidized Maena work.

### Useful answers locals can provide

- The observatory has been avoided for years.
- Sometimes lights come on inside with no power feed.
- A pale medical crew passed through recently, but did not go in the normal way.
- People who sleep near the observatory dream of water, old stars, and someone saying "do not open the wound."
- Ternus official maps mark the place as collapsed.

## Scene 4 - The dead observatory

### Purpose

Deliver the core exploration site: old science, old memory, and the first real Hand of Eterra data.

### Read-Aloud

Narrator:
The observatory was built before the city forgot the sky.

You can tell because it is shaped by hope instead of efficiency.

Its dome is cracked but still intact, a black half-sphere wedged beneath millions of tons of later Ternus. Star-tracking rails run around the base. Old glass panels have gone opaque with mineral bloom. The front doors are sealed by three different generations of locks: one civilian, one military, one that does not look like Ternus at all.

Above the entrance, nearly polished away by time, are four letters:

**S M D**

And under them, scratched by hand in a script the public archive tried very hard not to understand:

**Ëterra**

### Entry routes

- **Restore power:** Engineering or Systems DT 4.
- **Bypass locks:** Systems, Security, or Thievery DT 4.
- **Find collapsed side entry:** Investigation or Perception DT 3.
- **Use Seraphine's military knowledge:** Bureaucracy or History & Lore DT 4; success identifies which lock is later Ternus, not original.
- **Use Kaelith's reference ID:** reduces DT by 1 for data-facing entry.
- **Use hard magic:** effective, but dangerous; tick Ternus hard-casting +1 and Deep-city hazard +1.

### Lock layers

1. **Civilian observatory seal:** early human colonist engineering.
2. **Ternus military quarantine:** later cover-up.
3. **Eterra-pattern null seal:** not a lock against people; a lock against activation.

### Failure costs

- Power flicker wakes old systems out of order.
- A local sensor pings a long-dead Ternus archive address.
- The door opens, but seals the exit behind them until they finish the core sequence.
- Someone hears a voice in their own memory say, "Not that way."

## Scene 5 - Inside the old sky room

### Purpose

Reveal what the observatory was and seed the Hand of Eterra without turning it into a lore lecture.

### Read-Aloud

Narrator:
Inside, the air is old enough to have opinions.

Dust rolls away from your boots in soft gray sheets. The main chamber is a circular theater of dead consoles facing a ceiling that used to open to stars. Now it opens to stone, pipework, and the underside of a city that buried its own beginning.

At the center stands a cracked projection table.

It turns on when you approach.

No welcome message. No corporate logo. No military warning.

Just a starfield.

Then a blue-green point of light.

Then a word:

**Terra**

The system glitches.

The word changes.

**Ëterra**

### Investigation structure

Let them pick two to four consoles before the core reveal. Each console gives a clue.

#### Console A - Departure registry

**Roll:** Systems, History & Lore, or Investigation DT 3.

- Soma Arata appears as a captain in an early human deep-space initiative.
- The registry names Terra directly, then later overwrites use Ternus-standard redaction.
- Several crew records show voluntary memory compartmentalization after arrival.

#### Console B - Eterra protocol

**Roll:** History & Lore, Spellcasting, or Reason DT 4.

- Eterra is not just a name. It is a doctrine or protocol for preventing catastrophic magical hunger from fully opening.
- The old records call it **the mercy protocol**.
- Recovered phrase:

> Eterra did not kill the hunger. She taught the wound how not to open.

- The Hand of Eterra appears to be an authorized bearer, executor, or witness to that protocol.
- Recovered phrase:

> The Hand was never a title. It was an obligation. Whoever wears it inherits what Eterra could not finish.

#### Console C - Zenzuata pairing

**Roll:** Spellcasting, History & Lore, or Essence Sight DT 4.

- Zenzuata appears as closure, seal, or final binding.
- It is paired with Eterra in catastrophic-containment records.
- Recovered phrase:

> Zenzuata closes what Eterra spares.

#### Console D - Kemet Continuity

**Roll:** Investigation, Systems, or Engineering DT 4.

- Kemet emergency interface designs were adapted from older non-mage survival systems.
- Later Maena work corrupted or commercialized these principles.
- The original intent was not "give non-mages weapons." It was "let terrified survivors operate sealed systems when mages were dead, absent, or unsafe."

#### Console E - Memory ethics record

**Roll:** Insight, History & Lore, or Spirit DT 4.

- Early Ternus/Terran memory wiping was first justified as protection of Terra's location.
- Later generations turned it into military and civic infrastructure.
- Kumoshi's teachings are referenced indirectly as "Dream-borne civic veilcraft."
- Essence Sight or flame reading catches old Dream traces in the archive itself. Not fresh. Not hostile. More like a lock designed by someone who knew memory could be a door.

### GM note

If they ask whether Eterra is a person, place, protocol, or title, the best answer is:

> "Yes, but not all at the same time anymore."

Let that be unsettling rather than evasive.

## Scene 6 - The Hand record

### Purpose

Give a satisfying answer to the Session 22 lead while preserving mystery.

### Trigger

The crew accesses the reference file **SMD-456cd-e99c2-Ëterra_hist.cc2** or reconstructs enough of it from damaged consoles.

### Read-Aloud

Narrator:
The old file does not open.

It exhales.

Light spills across the cracked projection table in broken bands. A woman stands there in the static, or the memory of a woman, or a record pretending very hard not to be a ghost.

Human. Tired. Military coat with no modern insignia. One hand resting on the edge of a console as if the universe has been asking too much for too long.

The audio catches on the third try.

"If this record survived, then the seal failed, the wound moved, or my successors became cowards."

She looks toward someone outside the recording.

"Soma, begin the Hand transfer."

### What the record reveals

Pick the amount based on pacing and rolls:

#### Baseline reveal

- **Hand of Eterra** is a title attached to custody of a catastrophic-containment protocol.
- The Hand is not supposed to rule, worship, or command. The Hand carries authorization to prevent a sealed hunger from becoming a weapon again.
- The file frames the Hand less as status and more as inherited duty: **"The Hand was never a title. It was an obligation."**
- Soma Arata was present at, or adjacent to, a Hand transfer.
- The observatory once stored a continuity record because the first settlers feared later Ternus would bury or weaponize the truth.

#### 2+ net reveal

- The Hand title can pass by formal transfer, emergency appointment, or memory inheritance.
- Ternus later redacted the term because it conflicted with state memory policy and Terra secrecy.
- A Hand can identify Eterra-pattern seals, null events, and "wound-opening" technologies.

#### 3+ net reveal

- The old freighter's deeper system was not merely a Nefarius weapon. The Nefarius layer was wrapped around or grafted onto an older Eterra/Zenzuata containment principle.
- Talifax's crime was not only manufacturing anti-magic ships. He turned a containment principle into a hunger engine.
- Someone modern is trying to recreate that bridge using Spark, cybernetics, and non-mage execution architecture.

#### 4+ net reveal

- The record contains a partial signature that resonates with Nyrix/Kumoshi, but not enough to identify Nyrix unless the players bring her into it.
- The signature feels like Dream magic used to protect a memory by making everyone misname it.
- The observatory can produce one physical data shard, but doing so wakes something.

### What the file does not reveal

- It does not give Terra's location.
- It does not prove exactly who in Maena ordered the modern attack.
- It does not explain all of Talifax.
- It does not make Soma trustworthy.
- It does not crown a PC as the Hand unless the players actively chase that.

## Scene 7 - The observatory wakes

### Purpose

Create a consequence for finding the truth. The conflict can be puzzle, chase, social, or combat-lite.

### Trigger options

- Opening the Hand record.
- Removing the physical data shard.
- Failing an entry/power roll.
- Hard casting inside the null seal.
- Mentioning Nyrix, Kumoshi, Talifax, or Maena in a way the old system indexes.

### Choose one response

#### Response A - Null seal stabilization

The observatory thinks a wound-opening event is possible and starts sealing the room.

**Rolls:** Systems, Engineering, Spellcasting, or Command DT 4.

Success lets them shut it down or redirect it. Failure traps them briefly and ticks Ternus hard-casting +1 from the energy pulse.

#### Response B - Memory defense

Everyone hears a different version of the same warning.

**Rolls:** Willpower, Insight, Spirit, or History & Lore DT 3.

On failure, a character loses a few minutes, repeats a phrase, or briefly forgets why they came. Do not remove agency for long.

Warning phrases:

- "Do not open the wound."
- "Do not turn mercy into appetite."
- "The Hand carries; the Hand does not own."
- "If Terra must stay hidden, let it stay hidden from kings first."

#### Response C - Maena watcher

A modern sensor wakes in the old room. It is not Ternus military. It is a small pale-silver device hidden in a maintenance panel.

**Rolls:** Perception DT 4 to spot before it transmits; Systems DT 4 to jam; Engineering DT 4 to preserve it as evidence.

Success gives a hard proof item: modern Maena-linked monitoring hardware in the observatory. Failure means Maena now knows someone reached the Hand record.

Recommended: use Response C if the session needs a forward push toward Maena.

## Scene 8 - Exit choices

### Purpose

End with agency and a clear next path rather than a lore dump.

After the observatory, ask what they do with the new evidence.

1. **Return to the Lone Star** to brief Lysa, Nyrix, and the others.
2. **Go to the War Archive** if Kaelith's request advances.
3. **Return to the freighter** to compare Eterra/Zenzuata data against the aux core and bridge.
4. **Pursue Joren's body** now that they better understand the trigger.
5. **Go after the Maena Trade Moon facility** with stronger evidence.
6. **Contact Soma Arata** and test what he knew.
7. **Contact Saezo / the governor** and decide how much proof to hand over.

### End-session button options

#### Button A - The governor signs

Narrator:
Kaelith's datapad chirps as the vehicle climbs back toward light.

The authorization is one line long.

> Request approved under provisional gubernatorial curiosity.

Seraphine reads it twice.

"That is not a legal category."

Rho looks over her shoulder.

"It is now."

#### Button B - Maena knows

Narrator:
Yraina's face changes before she says anything.

Her tablet is dark. No message. No alert.

Just a pale reflection in the glass.

White. Silver. A bruise of violet.

Then a woman's voice, calm as a clinic:

"Yraina. You should have called your mother."

#### Button C - Nyrix dreams

Narrator:
Back on the Lone Star, Nyrix is asleep when you arrive.

Not peaceful. Not frightened.

Listening.

When her eyes open, they do not quite focus on the room.

"Kumoshi knows that word," she says.

Then, quieter:

"He does not want me to."

#### Button D - The Hand record names Soma

Narrator:
The damaged shard runs one last recovery pass.

Most of the file is gone.

One authorization block survives.

> HAND TRANSFER WITNESS: SOMA ARATA  
> STATUS: LIVING MEMORY CARRIER  
> WARNING: DO NOT TRUST STATE ARCHIVES

OOC: Great place to call it.

## Character-specific beats

### Asha

- Let her disgust at Ternus sharpen into action rather than just commentary.
- She just humanized Joren with the payment to his family; use that if Joren's name comes up.
- If she spends money below, locals should react like she is casually moving impossible weight.
- Her armor remains a possible answer to Nefarius-tainted material, but the observatory should not be devoured unless she chooses a dramatic risk.

### Kaelith

- He earned this lead. Let him be the one who recognizes the importance of the accented **Ë** and the old data format.
- Give him strong History & Lore / Essence Sight value.
- His official record request is now a live political lever.
- If he reads the Eterra/Zenzuata seal magically, show structure and warning, not a full encyclopedia.

### Rho

- This is a Rho spotlight session.
- Show his discomfort with the lower city as lived experience, not shame comedy.
- Let him be competent in route-finding and lower-city rules.
- He can explain which dangers are real and which are upper-city myths.

### Yraina

- She is newly exposed and may overcompensate by being extra useful.
- If Maena devices appear, she recognizes style, etiquette, and what is too polished to be accidental.
- She should not know everything; her estrangement is real.
- She will not volunteer all of this proactively. She needs the crew to ask the right questions, because she is still deciding whether this is a job, revenge, or family war.
- Her fear that Kyrsa already knows she is involved is reasonable.
- If pressed about Drifter Rock, give one sentence now and save the full story for drinks unless players insist.

Yraina:
"Drifter Rock was a job where I thought I was the clever one. I was not. People got hurt, I ran, and Saezo eventually decided I was more useful embarrassed than dead."

### Seraphine

- She is useful with Ternus systems and old military layers.
- She should be uneasy when old human secrecy, Terra, and memory policy come up.
- She can identify which parts of the observatory were sealed by later Ternus military hands.
- She should strongly object to letting Maena or unknown systems examine Nyrix.

### Tamsin

- Use her to keep momentum practical: entry points, ambush angles, exit plan.
- She should distrust any "helpful" Ternus authorization.
- She is the best voice for "we got the clue, now leave before the building decides to become a coffin."

### Lysa

- Not physically present unless the party calls her or changes plans.
- If contacted, she can say old memory defenses feel adjacent to spirit work but not the same.
- She may remind them Joren's body still matters.

Lysa:
"If this place teaches people how to forget, then ask what it was built to remember."

### Nyrix

- Use only if contacted, brought in by comm, or as an end button.
- Her reaction should be intimate and unsettling, not exposition-heavy.
- If she hears "Kumoshi" and "Terra" together, she gets quiet and protective of her own thoughts.
- Kumoshi's Dream fingerprint in the observatory should imply Nyrix may have a key nobody has found yet, but do not make her explain it on command.

## GM must remember

- **The aux core is still in Seraphine's possession and has never been opened.** It likely contains hard Maena evidence: bridge handshake, Kemet continuity, and a Maena access trace.
- **The freighter is not exhausted.** Armory, crew quarters, engineering, hidden medical locker, bridge implant shard, and Joren's body all remain live if the crew pivots.
- **The Hand was never a title; it was an obligation.** Let that phrase reframe the mystery without immediately explaining it.
- **Kyrsa Maena is alive, attentive, and likely keeps tabs on Yraina.** She does not need to act immediately to be dangerous.
- **Kumoshi's Dream magic fingerprint is in or around the observatory archive.** This connects memory protection, Terra secrecy, and Nyrix without requiring a direct reveal.
