# Session 22 Scene Summary

## Scene 1 - Leaving the freighter and choosing the archive team

### What happened

The session opened with a recap of the freighter investigation. The crew had enough evidence to say Maena touched the freighter before it reached Ternus, but not enough to prove what they did or how Joren became the trigger. Seraphine had the auxiliary data core, Asha had identified the **Absorb / Release** retrofit, Kaelith had seen Maena-like blue-and-white Inurans loading the armor and a masked spirit binder controlling the captain, and Yraina had named Maena as the likely family behind the spirit-magic angle.

The group decided they were finished with the freighter for now and returned to the Lone Star. They discussed who should go down to the Ternus public archives. Asha, Kaelith, Seraphine, Yraina, Tamsin, and Rho went. Lysa stayed behind to dig from another angle.

### Major points

- The freighter investigation is paused, not exhausted.
- The active Ternus archive team is **Asha, Kaelith, Seraphine, Yraina, Tamsin, and Rho**.
- Lysa remained on the ship and may have off-screen research or spirit-related follow-up available later.
- The party's immediate research goal was the **Hand of Eterra** and references to the magic used in the freighter/dock incident.

### GM needs to remember

- The auxiliary data core still has not been fully exploited in play.
- Joren's body and the Lysa dead-interview route are still live unresolved leads.
- The crew deliberately skipped the remaining freighter sweep for now.

### Players need to remember

- The freighter still contains unresolved evidence.
- Lysa may be able to help later if they obtain Joren's body, remains, or a strong sympathetic link.
- The archive trip was a pivot, not a conclusion.

## Scene 2 - Ternus descent, customs, and the hard-casting ban

### What happened

The crew took a space elevator down to Ternus. At customs, a spell-armored officer stopped them because they stood out and warned them that **hard casting is banned for the next two weeks**. He made it clear Ternus claims it can detect who casts, where, when, why, and how. Asha behaved, Seraphine barely behaved, and Kaelith gave the guard a look of open disgust.

The group then took a self-driving taxi to the nearest public archive. The building was utilitarian, crowded, and filled with public terminals rather than curated reading rooms. Seraphine suggested splitting up but staying in comm contact.

### Major points

- Ternus is on heightened anti-magic monitoring.
- Armor and weapons were not prohibited, but casting is politically and mechanically dangerous.
- The public archive is accessible, crowded, and impersonal.
- Seraphine believes Ternus likely has Spark detection devices deployed during the ban.

### GM needs to remember

- Any hard casting on Ternus during this window should carry consequences unless carefully shielded, hidden, or done somewhere outside normal sensor coverage.
- The party is visibly unusual but not automatically illegal.
- Ternus security can become a pressure clock without needing to become a full combat encounter.

### Players need to remember

- Hard casting on Ternus is banned for two weeks.
- Thrusters, armor systems, mundane skills, and subtle magic are safer than overt spellcasting.
- The party entered Ternus through official channels and can be traced if authorities care enough.

## Scene 3 - Asha researches Nefarius warships, Talifax, and Soma Arata

### What happened

Asha began by researching the Nefarius technology behind the **Absorb / Release** controls. She found that the ships were sold to Ternus by Inurans and designed for ordinary Terran soldiers. A captain could press **Absorb**, causing black tendrils to tear apart an intended magical target and store the energy. The ship could later **Release** that energy through specialized spell cannons or from the hull itself.

When Asha dug deeper into the origin of the technology, she found a name: **Talifax**. The records imply Talifax or Nefarius had the responsible Inuran merchant under their control. Talifax brought the weapon into the world and was hunted down by Xal Aran and Nara for that crime. His death is recorded in another galaxy thirty light years away, despite mortal intergalactic travel being effectively impossible.

Asha later searched **Soma Arata**, the source of the Hand of Eterra tip. Records say Soma was born on Terra, with dates redacted, and served as a captain in the original crews who left Terra to explore the galaxy.

### Major points

- The Absorb / Release ships were usable by non-mage Terran soldiers.
- Talifax is now tied to the origin of the Nefarius anti-magic fleet.
- Talifax's death location is cosmologically strange.
- Soma Arata has records from Terra and the original human exploration era.
- Asha received **+1 EXP** during the session after the Hand of Eterra search beat.

### GM needs to remember

- Talifax should continue to feel dead but not simple.
- "Thirty light years away in another galaxy" is a major weird flag; do not let it become throwaway trivia.
- Soma Arata is old enough and historically placed enough to matter to the Terra/Eterra mystery.

### Players need to remember

- Talifax brought the Nefarius fleet weapon into the world.
- The old freighter's weapon system was built around non-mage operation.
- Soma Arata is connected to Terra's original crews, not just a random informant.

## Scene 4 - Kaelith finds the Hand of Eterra archive coordinate

### What happened

Kaelith searched for planetary plans and ancient archives, then looked into the title **Hand of Eterra**. His first search returned too much: Ternus is full of ancient archives buried under layers of steel and city growth. Direct searches for Eterra failed because the system kept correcting the term to Terra.

After a History & Lore success, Kaelith found one surviving reference that escaped deletion because of an accented letter: **SMD-456cd-e99c2-Ëterra_hist.cc2**. It appears to be an old pre-Ternus data storage format from the era when humanity first arrived. The reference returned a last known location deep inside Ternus, at what used to be a research observatory.

### Major points

- The Hand of Eterra trail is not on the public internet in useful form.
- Kaelith found a specific reference ID and coordinates.
- The target is deep within Ternus, in an old research observatory.
- The file format predates modern Ternus and ties back to early human arrival.

### GM needs to remember

- The clue exists because of an accented **Ë**, which is a great symbol for partial erasure and human/machine mistranslation.
- The research observatory should feel old, buried, and pre-current-Ternus.
- The data may be fragile, offline, or physically present rather than network-accessible.

### Players need to remember

- Kaelith has coordinates for the next lead.
- The party is looking for an old data object or document, not necessarily a person.
- Public records are unreliable when Eterra is involved.

## Scene 5 - Kaelith researches Maena, Nefarius, and the derelict freighter sale

### What happened

Kaelith cross-referenced Maena with Nefarius. He found that Maena provided crucial support to Ternus at the tail end of the war with Nefarius. The information was partly hidden under records about **Necrotis**, who later attempted to seize control of the galaxy by turning Inura's Grace into **The Maker's Wrath**.

Kaelith found plenty of under-the-table Maena/Ternus dealings involving armor and weapons quotas, but notably no Spark sales. He also searched the derelict freighter's identifier and found it was sold to the Ternus military but listed as undelivered. The bill of sale names the manufacturer only as Inura and the buyer as Ternus; the specific Inuran seller is redacted.

Kaelith requested access to deeper military records about the bill of sale, attaching his own name and listing the reason as assisting the Ternan military at the governor's behest. The request was accepted, but no timeline was given. Seraphine later realized that because of Kaelith's wording, the governor may have to personally sign off on it.

### Major points

- Maena supported Ternus late in the Nefarius war.
- The Maena/Nefarius thread is entangled with later Necrotis history.
- Maena has many under-the-table Ternus dealings, but no obvious Spark sales.
- The freighter sale was to Ternus, listed as undelivered, with the seller redacted.
- Kaelith has an official request pending that may go to the governor personally.

### GM needs to remember

- Kaelith's request is an excellent delayed complication or gift.
- Seraphine thinks the request probably will not hurt them, but politics may follow.
- The governor may sign because it is useful, funny, destabilizing, or all three.

### Players need to remember

- Kaelith has an active bureaucratic thread in motion.
- The derelict freighter's official sale record is thin but suspicious.
- Maena's relationship with Ternus is long, profitable, and dirty.

## Scene 6 - Asha finds Yraina's file and confronts her

### What happened

Asha searched Yraina on a whim and found a classified military file. Seraphine used her military access to open it. The file identifies Yraina as the daughter of **Kyrsa Maena**, matriarch of one of Inura's most profitable manufacturers. Ternus has repeatedly tried to bribe, blackmail, and coerce Yraina into helping them. She is listed as problematic but not a security threat.

Asha confronted Yraina directly. Yraina first thought Asha had found out about an old incident on Drifter Rock, then realized the issue was her family. She explained that she has been estranged from Maena for over half her life and insisted Maena would not use her as a spy. Asha pressed her for not mentioning this earlier. Yraina admitted she had been winging it and had lied by omission because this was the first decent job she had in years and she wanted a chance to see Maena burn.

Asha accepted the explanation enough to soften the moment and suggested drinks later, possibly with the Drifter Rock story included.

### Major points

- Yraina is Kyrsa Maena's estranged daughter.
- Ternus has tried to use Yraina against Maena before.
- Yraina omitted her family connection during earlier Maena discussions.
- Yraina claims she wants Maena hurt and is not their spy.
- Drifter Rock is now an unresolved Yraina backstory hook.

### GM needs to remember

- Yraina should not be treated as cleanly innocent or secretly villainous by default; her useful truth is complicated.
- Asha and Yraina's relationship survived the confrontation, but trust has changed shape.
- Ternus already knows Yraina is leverage-adjacent.

### Players need to remember

- Yraina is tied to Maena by blood, not loyalty.
- Kyrsa Maena is a major name.
- Yraina has a past on Drifter Rock that she expected might cost her the job.

## Scene 7 - Asha researches Kumoshi, Nyrix, and Joren Arden

### What happened

Asha searched Nyrix and found nothing, then searched **Kumoshi**. Records describe Kumoshi as a significant god thousands of years ago, a hero to the Dragonflights of Virkonna, Ternus, and others, and one of the figures who helped turn opinion against Krox after Krox turned the gods against Xal. Kumoshi disappeared and was assumed to be in long slumber like Virkonna.

Further digging revealed Kumoshi was one of Krox's children and turned on Krox after Krox consumed Xal, likely because Kumoshi was close enough to understand Krox's true plan. Asha also found that Kumoshi was a master of Air and Dream magic and taught Ternus how to wipe memories. That technique became foundational to Ternus society and military secrecy, including hiding Terra.

Asha then searched **Joren Arden** and found he was born on Ternus thirty-four years ago, served briefly in the military, became a salvager, and left behind a wife and children. Asha arranged for Joren's wife to receive **one million credits**. The payment processed immediately, and Asha received **+1 EXP**.

### Major points

- Kumoshi is Krox's child and was important in turning against Krox.
- Kumoshi mastered Air and Dream magic.
- Kumoshi taught Ternus memory-wiping techniques.
- Nyrix may be linked to the only living access point for memories of Terra.
- Joren has a family, and Asha sent them one million credits.
- Asha received a second **+1 EXP** during the session for the Joren payment.

### GM needs to remember

- The Kumoshi/Nyrix/Terra connection is now explicit enough to matter.
- Memory magic is not just personal tragedy; it is Ternus infrastructure.
- Joren should remain humanized. He was a person with a family, not only a trigger.

### Players need to remember

- Kumoshi may know, or have touched, memories of Terra.
- Nyrix may become crucial to Terra's location or history.
- Joren's surviving family exists and has been materially helped by Asha.

## Scene 8 - Regrouping and heading toward the buried observatory

### What happened

Last session, Kaelith called the group and announced he had a possible lead on the Hand of Eterra. Everyone regrouped around Asha and Yraina, then left the archive. Kaelith showed the coordinates to the group. Rho recognized them and became visibly unsettled. He said he had seen the place from far away as a kid because he came from "dirt" and had been far below the city.

Rho explained that the coordinates point to a huge cavern deep below the city, in one of the old cities. He knows how to reach the top of the cavern, but not how to reach the bottom safely. The group discussed whether Ternus would detect casting down there and decided to assume yes. Tamsin recommended thrusters only. Rho then realized the party's armor makes the descent much easier.

The group entered a six-seat vehicle. Rho successfully reprogrammed the destination beyond the normal operating zone and joked that Asha would be charged a few hundred credits for a deep clean.

### Major points

- The next location is a buried old-city cavern containing or leading to the research observatory.
- Rho knows how to get to the top of the cavern because of his childhood in Ternus's lower layers.
- The descent problem is likely solved by armor thrusters, not casting.
- There may be dangerous locals or scavengers, but Rho thinks the crew can avoid them or handle them.
- The session ended in the vehicle on the way to the deep route.

### GM needs to remember

- Start Session 23 in the reprogrammed vehicle or as it reaches the forbidden lower route.
- Rho's lower-city past should get texture, not just exposition.
- The party is still under a hard-casting ban unless they get far enough below normal monitoring or accept consequences.
- Yraina is with them unless something changes.

### Players need to remember

- Rho is the guide now.
- The target is below the city, not in a normal public archive.
- Thrusters are the safe plan for the cavern descent.
- Kaelith's military-record request is still pending.
