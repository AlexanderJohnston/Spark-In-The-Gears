# Session 21 Scratchpad

## Session thesis

The crew is still standing on the bridge of the old Inuran freighter, looking at the first wound in the campaign with much better eyes than they had the first time. Session 21 should make the ship feel less like an old crime scene and more like a sealed historical weapon: Maena is the modern hand on the knife, but the blade itself is older, stranger, and tied to Eterra, Zenzuata, Xal, Aashitak, Kemet, and the thing the gods were trying to stop.

Keep the session local-scale and investigative. The players asked for grounded investigation, social texture, and skill use. Let the freighter breathe. Let the crew search compartments, talk through implications, recover the aux core, do a flame read, and choose whether to go to Ternus archives before returning to the Trade Moon / Maena lead.

Do not force a single solution path. The freighter should yield enough proof to continue through several routes:

1. **Magical route:** Essence Sight, flame read, Sargoth, Asha's armor, Nyrix/Kumoshi.
2. **Technical route:** Seraphine's aux core, bridge matrices, ship logs, material residues.
3. **Historical route:** Ternus archives, Kemet records, Eterra / Zenzuata redactions.
4. **Social route:** dock workers, Pell Vask, reconstruction crews, cheap Maena implants.
5. **Trade Moon route:** Yraina's Maena lead, staff invoice restriction, Maena clinic / atelier.

## Start state from Session 20

- The crew returned from the Trade Moon to the Lone Star with Yraina now attached to them as a temporary crew/fixer arrangement.
- Kaelith commissioned Orrel Tain to build a one-of-a-kind Eldimagus staff for **285 million credits**, due in a few days if they can pay and resolve sourcing.
- The staff material list was flagged by **Maena Biologic Interface Group** because of a **Spark-compatible neural lattice** restriction.
- Yraina says Maena is effectively untouchable on Inura and powerful on the Trade Moon, but the current trade frenzy makes it possible to slip through cracks.
- Asha accepted Yraina's proposed new deal: Yraina will find a Maena lead and go with them; Asha pays what Yraina is worth after they get answers.
- The crew went to Saezo's secured Ternus private dock and boarded the old Inuran freighter.
- Kaelith's Essence Sight found old Dream, Void, and Fire traces; the bridge matrix has a blue-white / sterile-silver signature unlike normal magic.
- Asha's Nefarius armor says the ship reeks of Nefarius, but that is only a coating over something worse, "like glass," that it cannot touch.
- Sargoth feels dread and familiarity. Xal and Aashitak are both connected to the ship, perhaps through a great tragedy.
- Kaelith's history read suggests this is not Talifax's style. Eterra and Zenzuata may be involved, but the records are hidden; Ternus archives may hold answers.
- Nyrix/Kumoshi identified Eterra as important and possibly responsible for the impossible magic here. Kumoshi is blocking Nyrix from remembering Eterra.
- Nyrix revealed that Xal was one of the creators/titans of the universe and that dragons, including Nyrix, ate the creators. Virkonna was present as a hatchling when Xal was consumed.
- The old bridge spell is ancient, wrong, engineered, and not written in comprehensible runes, language, or concepts.
- Seraphine found an auxiliary data core behind a panel. This is the immediate actionable object.
- Sargoth suggested Kaelith could use a quick flame read to find or witness the previous occupants.

## Opening beat - resume on the bridge

### Read-aloud

Narrator:
The freighter bridge stays quiet after Seraphine pulls the aux core free.

That is almost worse than a warning siren.

Portable dock lamps throw pale light across dead Inuran consoles, locked matrix rings, purple glass in the fracture line, and the faded evidence outline where Joren's body touched the spellwork. The dense, black core in Seraphine's hand is small enough to hold like a baseball.

For now, the rest of the ship waits.

There are old cargo bays. Dead crew rooms. War compartments. Storage geometry. Whatever survived official inspection, Maena scrubbing, Ternus laziness, and decades of being lost.

This was the first place the story tried to kill you.

It may not have been trying very hard.

### Immediate prompt

Ask the players what they want to do first:

1. Examine Seraphine's aux data core.
2. Use flame read / Essence Sight on the bridge and Joren's evidence mark.
3. Search the bridge physically for fragments.
4. Inspect the cargo bay / armory / crew quarters.
5. Question dock workers.
6. Go straight to Ternus archives with the core.
7. Contact Saezo, Corvin, Bell, Maris, one of the gods (Aran, Nara, Voria), or Seraphine's Ternus channels.

## Important GM frame

Session 20 already revealed that Maena blocked Kaelith's staff component and that the freighter signature is old, artificial, and wrong. Session 21 should connect those ideas without making Maena the only answer.

Recommended truth stack:

1. **Ancient layer:** Eterra and/or Zenzuata used the freighter, Kemet, or similar war systems to contain, redirect, or study a threat tied to Nefarius / world-devouring / titan remains.
2. **War layer:** Kemet emergency programs adapted ancient interface logic into non-mage spell operation during a desperate wartime event.
3. **Modern layer:** Maena rediscovered or inherited pieces of that work and is turning it into Spark-fed cybernetic spell programs.
4. **Incident layer:** Joren triggered a weak, degraded, or deliberately throttled version of a much larger effect through the bridge matrix.
5. **Trade Moon layer:** Maena is now blocking neural lattice materials and recovering evidence because the crew is walking too close to the current program.

The dock blast was "weak" because it was not the intended weapon. It was leakage, a misfire, a test, a broken handshake, or a warning flare from something designed for a much bigger battlefield.

## Main investigation structure

Run the freighter as a hub with clue zones. Let the players choose order. Give meaningful clues even on failed rolls; failures add Heat, partial corruption, attention, or backlash.

### Heat clock - Secured Ternus dock

Start at **0 / 6** unless the players are loud.

Increase Heat by 1 when:

- They force systems or doors.
- They cast loud magic without containment.
- They mention Maena on open comms.
- They trip old Inuran security.
- They route the aux core through public Ternus networks.
- They let Yraina query Trade Moon systems from the dock.
- They antagonize private security.

At **2 Heat**, Pell Vask checks in and asks what they are doing.

At **4 Heat**, Ternus security wants logs, not explanations.

At **5 Heat**, a Maena-linked legal/medical office pings the dock about "biohazard neural evidence."

At **6 Heat**, lockdown, injunction, or a Maena scrub team arrives.

## Scene 1 - Aux data core

### Purpose

Give Seraphine a practical win, reward technical play, and provide a bridge from the freighter to Ternus archives / Maena / Kemet.

### Read-aloud

Narrator:
The aux core looks like nothing.

That is how you know it was meant to survive.

No polished shell. No noble metals. No glowing Inuran vanity-work. Just a dense black block with three contact lines and a tiny maintenance glyph etched so shallowly it looks accidental.

Seraphine turns it once in her hand.

Seraphine:
"Main recorders get found. Main recorders get scrubbed. Aux cores are where engineers hide the truth because nobody respects maintenance until something explodes."

### Roll options

- Systems + Reason DT 4 to read it safely.
- Engineering DT 4 to build a clean interface.
- Investigation DT 4 to identify useful data clusters.
- Seraphine assistance grants +1 die.
- Bell or Corvin remote assistance reduces DT by 1 but increases Heat by 1 if using active comms.

### Results

**Success:** recover three damaged clusters:

1. Last bridge matrix handshake.
2. Pre-disaster crew / cargo ghost logs.
3. A war-route reference tied to Kemet.

**2+ net:** recover a manufacturer or project tag:

> MNA-CYB / KEMET CONTINUITY / SPARK-COMPATIBLE INTERFACE

**3+ net:** find that the same tag was accessed recently from a Trade Moon node.

**Failure:** the core still gives a clue, but one cluster corrupts during extraction. Choose:

- Heat +1 as dock sensors detect unauthorized hardware access.
- False timestamp: the logs appear older than they really are.
- Feedback: Willpower DT 3 or the reader hears a few seconds of dead-crew audio in their own voice.

### Core contents

Use these as fragments, not a clean exposition dump.

#### Fragment A - matrix handshake

> PILOT CONTACT: MANUAL  
> BIO AUTH: MISMATCH  
> SECONDARY INTERPRETER: PRESENT  
> INTERPRETER SOURCE: EXTERNAL / NEURAL  
> FUEL SOURCE: SPARK-COMPATIBLE  
> PACKET TYPE: EXECUTABLE SPELL LATTICE  
> RESULT: DISCHARGE BELOW STRATEGIC THRESHOLD  
> STATUS: FAILURE / FAILURE / FAILURE

This confirms that Joren did not cast the effect. Something used him or something attached to him as a trigger.

#### Fragment B - old war route

> KEMET RELIEF THEATER  
> EMERGENCY INTERFACE KITS  
> NON-MAGE SPELL OPERATION AUTHORIZED  
> COMMAND OVERRIDE: ETR-NULL / ZEN-SEAL  
> CATASTROPHIC EVENT PROTOCOLS ACTIVE

Do not fully explain ETR-NULL or ZEN-SEAL yet. Let them suspect Eterra / Zenzuata.

#### Fragment C - recent access

> RECORD TOUCHED  
> TRADE MOON NODE: ORANGE/GOLD MEDICAL-COMMERCIAL  
> AUTH FAMILY: MAENA  
> ACCESS PURPOSE: BIOHAZARD NEURAL RECOVERY

This gives Yraina enough to start finding a facility later.

## Scene 2 - Flame read on the bridge

### Purpose

Give Kaelith/Sargoth a magical reconstruction that feels visceral and dangerous. Show that the bridge has more history than Joren's death.

### Setup

Sargoth suggested the flame read. If Kaelith takes the prompt, run it near the matrix ring where Joren touched the spell.

### Roll

Spellcasting + Intuition or Reason DT 4.

- Edge if Kaelith uses Sargoth carefully and braces his mind as warned.
- +1 die if Asha anchors him physically or emotionally.
- +1 die if Lysa helps frame the matrix pattern.

### Read-aloud - success

Narrator: Kaelith
The purple glass in the matrix fracture catches your flame.

Not burns. Catches.

For one breath, the bridge is not dead.

Joren is there, hand on the ring, eyes wide with the terrible realization that his body has become part of a sentence he cannot read.

Behind him, not physically but in the working, a second shape unfolds: blue-white, sterile silver, precise as surgery. It is not a mage. It is not a spell. It is an instruction set wearing magic as a borrowed language.

Then the vision falls backward.

The bridge changes. Older. Cleaner. Full of voices speaking Inuran too fast, Kemet command codes, panic held inside military discipline. Someone says Eterra's name like a prayer and Zenzuata's like a lock.

Then farther back still.

Black water under a sky that is not a sky.

A god's bone bright enough to blind.

Something enormous trying to teach the world how to eat itself.

The flame goes out in your hand.

Sargoth:
"_Brace. Do not follow it deeper._"

### Clues from flame read

Give based on roll quality:

- **Success:** Joren was a trigger, not the author.
- **2+ net:** the spell packet was using a neural interpreter, consistent with implant/cybernetic interface.
- **3+ net:** the old system was originally designed for a catastrophic threat, not dock sabotage.
- **4+ net:** Eterra and Zenzuata appear as protocol names or mythic presences: one associated with nulling/mercy/containment, the other with sealing/binding/order.

### Failure

The flame still shows Joren as a trigger, but Kaelith sees too much of the black water and takes a consequence:

- -1 die to next magical perception roll.
- A short intrusive image of Xal being consumed.
- Heat +1 as the matrix emits a dead pulse.
- Sargoth becomes sharply protective and refuses a second flame read here.

## Scene 4 - Search the freighter

### Purpose

Let Kaelith inspect every inch as requested, give other PCs/NPCs roles, and make the ship feel like a place with layered history.

Offer these zones. Each can yield one clue and one mood beat.

### Zone A - Cargo spine

Old Dream residue clings here, tied to the dream scale from early sessions.

**Rolls:** Essence Sight DT 4, Investigation DT 4, History & Lore DT 5.

Clues:

- The dream scale was not random cargo. It was stored in a containment cradle built for something dangerous and sacred.
- The cargo spine has void-fold geometry meant to hide or preserve impossible mass.
- The freighter likely carried relic material or survivors from a catastrophe, not ordinary supplies.

Read-aloud:
The cargo spine is empty in the way a grave is empty after the body is gone.

No crates. No racks. No manifest tags worth trusting.

But Essence Sight catches the places where things once pressed against reality hard enough to bruise it.

Dream along the rails. Void in the seams. Aashitak warmth like old blood under cold metal.

### Zone B - Armory

This recalls where Kaelith found the armor and old war materials.

**Rolls:** Perception DT 3, Engineering DT 4, History & Lore DT 4.

Clues:

- Weapon racks were stripped long before Ternus investigators arrived.
- One rack still carries scorched residue from non-standard spell ammunition.
- The armory was prepared for boarding actions against something that did not fight like normal soldiers.

Tamsin:
"This is not anti-pirate loadout. This is 'we expect the hallway to become theology' loadout."

### Zone C - Crew compartments

Useful for humanizing the dead and setting up flame reads.

**Rolls:** Investigation DT 3, Perception DT 4, Empathy / Persuasion with ghosts only if using magic.

Clues:

- Crew left personal effects as if mobilized suddenly.
- Some bunks have old medical straps and neural-interface burn marks.
- A hidden personal recorder references "Kemet sky turning black" or "the relief route became a coffin."

Rho:
"I hate ships like this. Everyone leaves something behind, and then people like us come along and pretend it is evidence instead of grief."

### Zone D - Engineering

Connects "weak attack" to something larger.

**Rolls:** Engineering DT 4, Systems DT 4, Essence Sight DT 5.

Clues:

- The reactor was not in a death spiral.
- The bridge matrix discharge drew almost no true ship power.
- The ship contains old safety locks for a much larger matrix event, but those locks never opened.
- The dock blast was a degraded packet, failed activation, or low-power leak.

Seraphine:
"That is what bothers me. It did not use the ship. It used a corpse, a bridge ring, and a spark. If the big systems had woken up, we would not be standing here."

### Zone E - Hidden medical locker

Connects Maena and neural interfaces.

**Rolls:** Perception DT 5, Medicine / Cybernetics DT 4, Systems DT 4.

Clues:

- Empty slots for cybernetic interface kits.
- Old labels reference Kemet emergency theater use.
- A modern inventory tag was peeled off recently.

Yraina:
"Maena medical-white cases. That is their old aesthetic too. They do love making help look expensive enough to forgive."

## Scene 6 - Ternus Archives branch

### Purpose

Answer the session's biggest lore questions without dumping everything on the freighter. This is the best place to bring in Eterra, Zenzuata, Kemet, and Maena contracts.

Use if the crew follows Kaelith's hunch or wants official records before confronting Maena.

### Read-aloud

Narrator:
The Ternus War Archive Annex is built under a government block that looks personally offended by windows.

Inside, the air is dry and filtered and faintly metallic. Rows of sealed terminals sit behind glass partitions. Clerks move with the careful exhaustion of people who know history is dangerous only after someone important notices it.

This is where old wars go after everyone powerful agrees to misremember them.

### Entry options

- Bureaucracy / Persuasion DT 4.
- Saezo authorization reduces DT by 1, but his name appears in the access log.
- Seraphine's Ternus authority reduces DT by 1, but creates military notice.
- Asha's reputation grants Edge if framed as survivor / investigator.
- Forgery / Systems DT 5; failure adds Heat +2.

### Research topics

Let them pick 2-3 before pressure arrives.

#### Topic: Kemet

**History & Lore DT 4**

- Success: Kemet's planet and trade moon were destroyed after Nefarius-war events.
- 2+ net: the freighter was outside normal fleet position when the catastrophe occurred.
- 3+ net: Kemet authorized emergency non-mage spell operation programs.

#### Topic: Eterra

**Forbidden Lore / History & Lore DT 5**

- Success: Eterra appears in redacted records as an ancient dragon / creator-adjacent force associated with mercy, nullification, and impossible containment.
- 2+ net: some records imply Eterra acted against world-devouring threats, not mortal enemies.
- 3+ net: phrase recovered:

> Eterra did not kill the hunger. She taught the wound how not to open.

#### Topic: Zenzuata

**Forbidden Lore / History & Lore DT 5**

- Success: Zenzuata appears as a seal, lock, or binding principle in catastrophic-event protocols.
- 2+ net: Zenzuata's name is often paired with Eterra in records that are later overwritten.
- 3+ net: phrase recovered:

> Zenzuata closes what Eterra spares.

#### Topic: Maena war contracts

**Investigation / Bureaucracy DT 4**

- Success: Maena supplied cybernetic interface kits during emergency wartime conditions.
- 2+ net: kits blurred mage/non-mage operational law and were controversial.
- 3+ net: some Maena wartime records were updated recently from a Trade Moon medical-commercial node.

#### Topic: The weak blast

**Systems / Engineering / Military Tactics DT 4**

- Success: the Ternus dock blast does not match the output of the freighter's true strategic systems.
- 2+ net: it resembles a failed handshake or degraded test discharge.
- 3+ net: if the main system had fired, the dock would not have been the target scale. The city, orbital ring, or local reality layer might have been.
