## Session 7 Summary (GM Prep)

---

### What Happened

The crew committed to the Confederacy. Seraphine transferred them to the *Lone Star*, a battleship with three universal spell matrices. Kaelith's Nefarius god-blood armor catalyzed into a sentient Eldimagus, named itself **Sargoth**, and hungers for magic. They deployed to Virkon.

Ship combat went clean. Asha piloted, Kaelith fired disintegration bolts, Lysa warded, Tamsin locked targets, Rho ran the gauss cannon. One Elder Wyrm down. Fleet pushed toward the planet.

Then Virkonna woke.

The Goddess shredded four battleships instantly, breath attack wiped the rest. Seraphine's ship went down. The *Lone Star* survived only because of the stealth coating. Then Xal Aran's dreadnought *The Word of Xal* blinked in, revealed them with a sweep spell, and stopped Virkonna from incinerating the crew by threatening mutually assured destruction. Virkonna smelled Nefarius, almost certainly Sargoth. Aran ordered the crew to dock.

Aran told Asha her mother would be "waiting for her" aboard the dreadnought. How and why: unexplained.

## Read-Aloud (Primary) — Player Recap

**Narrator:**


You joined the war and took down your first dragon. Then a god woke up and ate your fleet.

The Lone Star slipped through Virkonna's wrath only because you were invisible, at least until Xal Aran's sweep lit you up like a signal flare between the two titans. Virkonna smelled something on your ship, something old that she *remembered*. She wanted to burn you for it, and she would have, if the Guardian hadn't aimed a god-killer at her throat.

Now you're instructed to dock with the dreadnought the size of a city. Your mother's fate is uncertain. Your armor whispers in hunger. The two most dangerous beings in the known universe have both seen your face on the front line of it all.

Welcome to the war for your galaxy. Ternus has historically been an underdog until their recent advancements with Spark technology along with many decades of investment in orbital shipyards.
