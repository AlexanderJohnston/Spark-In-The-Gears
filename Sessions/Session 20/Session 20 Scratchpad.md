## Open GM questions

No blocking questions. The only tuning choice is how culpable Veyra Maena should be on first contact:

1. **Plausible deniability:** she knows Maena tech was used but frames it as stolen legacy hardware.
2. **Complicit but careful:** she helped recover/scrub evidence and is protecting a live program.
3. **Direct antagonist:** she ordered or enabled the Spark-interface test and is now managing fallout.

Recommended for Session 20: start with **complicit but careful**. It gives the crew a person to pressure without forcing immediate combat or prematurely resolving the Maena arc.
