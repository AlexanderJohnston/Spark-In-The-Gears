# Session 20 Eldimagus Shop Scene

## Purpose

This scene gives Kaelith a place to search for a spellstaff worthy of becoming an Eldimagus without turning it into a normal shopping counter.

The seller should feel like a master artificer, but not a famous one. He is not a celebrity craftsman with a showroom and a waiting list. He is the person better-known masters quietly hire when they need something strange repaired and do not want anyone asking why it broke.

The goal is not for Kaelith to simply buy a staff. The goal is for him to choose the first bones of a future living weapon.


## Location: The Quiet Bench

The Quiet Bench is buried in an orange service level close enough to the surface that Yraina looks faintly annoyed about the walk.

It is not on the public shopping maps. The visible entrance is a parts reclamation office with an old sign, scratched glass, and a counter drone that has not been updated in years. Behind it is a narrow lift that only opens if Yraina pays a private transit sigil, then waits for the system to pretend it was a maintenance request.

The shop itself is a long workshop tucked between heat exchangers, tram supports, and forgotten cargo shafts. It has no sales floor. No velvet cases. No branded lighting. Just workbenches, mineral drawers, old forge arms, drying racks, spell clamps, half-built weapons, and shelves full of components wrapped in cloth and handwritten tags.

Every object here has been touched by a person who knew what they were doing.

### Read-aloud

Narrator:
Yraina takes you away from the expensive halls and into the places the Trade Moon needs but does not advertise.

The air gets warmer. The walls get uglier. The crowds thin out until the only people moving through the service corridors are workers, runners, and professionals who have learned not to look curious.

At the end of a ribbed maintenance hall, Yraina stops before a reclamation office with scratched glass and a dead little sign that reads **Tain Salvage Calibration**.

She does not knock.

She taps her tablet against the door frame, waits through three soft clicks, then looks back at Kaelith.

Yraina:
"This is not the nicest place I could take you. It may be the correct one."

The door opens into heat, metal dust, old magic, and the steady sound of tools being used carefully.


## Master Artificer: Orrel Tain

**Orrel Tain** is a Ternan-born master artificer in his late fifties or early sixties. He is not well-known because he has avoided being well-known on purpose.

He has worked under Inuran masters, repaired Shayan relic fittings, built private spell matrices for war people, and taken apart more fake Eldimagi than most shops have sold. He is not socially polished. He is not impressed by money. He dislikes famous customers because famous customers bring witnesses.

He is a master because his work survives Catalysts.

He is obscure because he refuses to sell his name.

### Presentation

- Worn work coat.
- Thin hair tied back with wire.
- Artificial left eye made from old brass and pale spellglass.
- Hands stained with polish, rune ink, and tiny burns.
- Speaks plainly.
- Looks at materials before he looks at people.
- Does not flatter.
- Does not haggle much.
- Will refuse a bad commission.

### What he knows

- Yraina is useful and dangerous.
- Saezo has sent him problems before.
- Kaelith is not ordinary, even before he sees the Xal resonance.
- Eldimagi are alive, and customers who forget that should not own one.
- The Trade Moon is full of expensive dead objects being sold as future legends.

### What he wants

- To find out whether Kaelith wants a tool, a trophy, or a companion.
- To avoid making a powerful Eldimagus for someone careless.
- To choose materials that can survive Kaelith's void, life, and dream-adjacent path.
- To make something interesting enough to be worth the risk.

### First impression

Orrel Tain:
"If you came for a famous name, you are in the wrong room."

He looks at Yraina.

"If you came for the work, close the door."


## Scene structure

Run the scene in four beats.

1. Yraina brings them to the Quiet Bench.
2. Orrel tests Kaelith through conversation and material handling.
3. Kaelith chooses a staff concept and material combination.
4. Orrel gives price, time, and the first hook needed to make it real.


## Beat 1: Why this shop

Yraina should frame this as a choice, not a downgrade.

Yraina:
"There are prettier artificers. There are louder ones. There are several who will charge you more because their signatures look lovely on an invoice."

She glances toward the workshop.

"Tain builds things that survive being taken seriously."

If Asha asks why he is not famous:

Yraina:
"Because fame is surveillance with better lighting."

If Kaelith asks why him:

Yraina:
"Because you asked for a staff that can become alive. That is not a retail question."


## Beat 2: Orrel tests Kaelith

Orrel does not start by showing stock. He starts with questions.

Orrel:
"A staff is already a statement. A living staff is a future argument. What do you want it to argue for?"

Possible questions:

1. "Do you want it to obey you, challenge you, or understand you?"
2. "Is this meant to kill, protect, reveal, travel, or remember?"
3. "Which Catalyst do you expect to bring it to first?"
4. "If it wakes and disagrees with you, what happens then?"
5. "Do you want a staff that tempers your void, or one that leans into it?"

### Roll prompt

**Roll: Willpower or Charisma + Persuasion / Artistry / Spellcasting DT 3**

This is not a sales roll. It is Kaelith proving he understands what he is asking for.

- **Success:** Orrel takes him seriously and opens the sealed material drawers.
- **2+ net:** Orrel shows a private component he was not planning to sell.
- **Failure:** Orrel still works with him, but gives a blunt warning and only offers safer builds.

Orrel on failure:
"You are thinking about power. That is normal. Try thinking about the person you are asking me to make."


## Beat 3: Eldimagus rules at the table

Use this as the short rules explanation in play.

An Eldimagus is made by fusing at least two magical materials into a single nascent intelligence. A normal magic item can be powerful, but it will not grow from Catalysts. An Eldimagus can.

### Creation basics

- The Eldimagus has a **rating**, also called potential for the purposes of Catalyst growth.
- The artificer needs at least **rating x 1,000 credits** worth of each magical material.
- At least two magical materials are required.
- Each additional material increases complexity and adds 10 hours of work.
- The workshop must have a rating equal to or higher than the Eldimagus rating.
- The artificer rolls **Reason + Artificing** with threshold equal to the item rating.
- Each success beyond the threshold adds one flourish.
- Some materials already come with flourishes.
- Total flourishes cannot exceed twice the rating.
- Eldimagi have armor equal to rating x 5 and hit points equal to rating x 10.

### Awakening basics

Each time an Eldimagus visits a Catalyst, it gains a point of Charisma, Reason, Will, and Intuition until it reaches its potential and becomes fully realized.

Before that, it has rudimentary awareness.

Once fully realized, it awakens with personality, desires, thoughts, spell pool equal to its rating, and 10 additional hit points. It may lend spell pool to its wielder or use spells and abilities it knows.

Orrel:
"If it can wake, it can want. If it can want, it can refuse. That is not a flaw. That is the entire point."


## Beat 4: Staff concepts

Orrel lays out three serious staff concepts. None are finished. They are possible futures.

Kaelith can choose one, combine ideas, or push for something stranger.


## Option 1: The Umbral Pilgrim

**Theme:** void travel, perception, restraint, hidden paths.

**Base materials:**

- Bone of Xal
- Voidsteel
- Feathersteel

**Suggested rating:** 3 or 4

**Native flourishes:**

- Bone of Xal grants **Soulguard**
- Voidsteel grants **Shadowed**
- Feathersteel grants **Light**

**Possible added flourishes:**

- Anchor for a void pocket or travel working
- Barrier if later tempered with life, water, earth, or spirit
- Elemental Battery only if another elemental material is introduced

**Personality seed:**

Quiet, watchful, old before it is awake. It may become protective in a distant way. It will care about routes, secrets, and promises.

**Orrel's warning:**
"This one will listen too much. To you. To the room. To places that are not rooms. If you are careless with it, it may learn fear before it learns trust."

**Kaelith resonance:**
The Xal sigil in Kaelith's memory may react to this build. Not enough to awaken the staff, but enough to make the workshop lights dim for a moment.

**Roll option: Spellcasting + Intuition DT 4**

- **Success:** Kaelith senses the staff would be a strong match for the new Umbral Blink sigil.
- **2+ net:** he realizes it could one day help stabilize Depths-routed movement.
- **Failure:** the resonance is cold and unpleasant, but not harmful.


## Option 2: The Green Star Staff

**Theme:** life, endurance, beauty, command presence, survival.

**Base materials:**

- Shayawood
- Blood of Shaya
- Air diamond or feathersteel

**Suggested rating:** 3

**Native flourishes:**

- Shayawood grants **Regeneration**
- Blood of Shaya grants **Accurate** and **Regeneration**
- Air diamond grants **Light** and **Elemental Battery**
- Feathersteel grants **Light**

**Note:** Duplicate flourishes do not stack. If using both Shayawood and Blood of Shaya, treat the repeated Regeneration as thematic reinforcement, not an extra flourish.

**Possible added flourishes:**

- Gifted if the table wants the staff to awaken early at its first Catalyst
- Guardian if leaning toward protective life magic
- Barrier if built to protect against hostile magic

**Personality seed:**

Warm, proud, protective, and hard to break. It may care about beauty, survival, and the living cost of violence.

**Orrel's warning:**
"This one will not be subtle. Life does not always mean gentle. Sometimes life just means difficult to kill."

**Shaya resonance:**
If Kaelith still carries Shaya's Beauty openly, the Blood of Shaya runes briefly brighten when he touches the staff blank.

**Roll option: Charisma + Spellcasting DT 3**

- **Success:** the staff responds warmly.
- **2+ net:** it may qualify for **Gifted** if Kaelith wants an earlier personality arc.
- **Failure:** the staff is viable, but it does not feel like Kaelith's first path.


## Option 3: The Ashen Choir

**Theme:** battle casting, accuracy, spell storage, damage, force of presence.

**Base materials:**

- Fire ruby
- Bone of Xal
- Frost sapphire or air diamond

**Suggested rating:** 4

**Native flourishes:**

- Fire ruby grants **Accurate** and **Elemental Battery**
- Bone of Xal grants **Soulguard**
- Frost sapphire grants **Savant** and **Elemental Battery**
- Air diamond grants **Light** and **Elemental Battery**

**Note:** Duplicate Battery flourishes do not stack.

**Possible added flourishes:**

- Guardian if built with air or water emphasis
- Anchor for an elemental aura
- Barrier if a defensive material is added later

**Personality seed:**

Sharp, hungry, clever, and proud. It may enjoy being used well. It may dislike hesitation.

**Orrel's warning:**
"This one will want to win. That sounds attractive until you realize winning is not always the same as choosing well."

**Battle resonance:**
If Kaelith channels purple-black flame near it, the fire ruby burns colder at the center.

**Roll option: Reason + Spellcasting DT 4**

- **Success:** Kaelith understands how to shape the staff toward spell precision rather than raw violence.
- **2+ net:** add a custom accuracy-focused flourish if Orrel succeeds on the final craft roll.
- **Failure:** Orrel refuses to build this version without more stabilizing material.


## Hidden fourth option: The Unwritten Staff

Only offer this if Kaelith resists all three prepared concepts or speaks strongly about the staff needing to call to him.

Orrel closes the drawers and brings out a wrapped bundle from under the central workbench.

Inside is not a staff. It is a long, unfinished core of pale wood, black bone dust, and a metal thread that shifts color under the light.

It has no final shape yet.

**Base materials:**

- Shayawood heart
- Bone of Xal dust
- Unnamed flexible metal thread, possibly a tiny amount of voidsteel and feathersteel worked together

**Suggested rating:** 4

**Current native flourishes:**

- Regeneration
- Soulguard
- Shadowed or Light, but not both until stabilized

**Problem:**

It is not ready. It needs one missing component before Orrel will attempt the fusion.

Choose the missing component:

1. **A dream-aspected material** to help it interpret visions and nightmares.
2. **A water-aspected crystal** to give it Savant potential and a more reflective mind.
3. **A spirit-aspected binding** to keep the future personality from becoming too alien.
4. **A fresh void component** tied to Kaelith's own Xal sigil.

Orrel:
"This is the one I would not have shown you first."

If asked why:

"Because if you choose it, the staff is not the only thing being judged."


## The artificer roll

Do not resolve the full crafting immediately unless the table wants to. This is a good place to set up a future roll after materials are finalized.

If resolving now:

**Orrel Tain**

- Reason: 5
- Artificing: 5
- Mastery applies, successes on 4-6
- Workshop rating: 5
- Edge if Kaelith chooses materials that clearly match his magical path
- Edge if Corvin assists with Shaya materials
- No Edge if rushed

**Craft roll:** Reason + Artificing vs rating threshold

- Rating 3: reliable, but still meaningful.
- Rating 4: serious work.
- Rating 5: possible only with rare material, time, and a real favor.

### Result guidance

- **Meets threshold:** the Eldimagus staff is created.
- **1 net success:** add one flourish.
- **2 net successes:** add two flourishes or one flourish plus a distinctive personality seed.
- **3+ net successes:** add a flourish, personality seed, and a latent Catalyst hook.
- **Failure by 1:** the item can be completed, but loses one intended flourish or requires more time.
- **Failure by 2+:** Orrel refuses to force it. The materials are not ruined, but he needs a missing stabilizer.

Orrel:
"I do not kill unborn things for schedule."


## Pricing

These prices assume Trade Moon surge pricing and the fact that Kaelith is asking for living weapon work.

| Staff rating | Material requirement | Work quote | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Rating 3 | 3,000+ credits per material minimum | 35-70 million | Safe serious option, still rare work |
| Rating 4 | 4,000+ credits per material minimum | 80-140 million | Best fit for a future signature item |
| Rating 5 | 5,000+ credits per material minimum | 180+ million | Requires favor, time, and rare stabilizer |

The material minimum from the rules is tiny compared to the Trade Moon cost. The real price is not raw material weight. It is rarity, purity, legal risk, workshop time, artificer skill, privacy, and the fact that the item is meant to survive Catalysts.

Yraina:
"The credits are the easy part. I apologize for how often that is true here."


## Complications and hooks

Use one if the scene needs a hook into the wider Session 20 investigation.

### 1. Maena has been buying staff cores

One of Orrel's material ledgers shows that Maena Biologic Interface Group tried to purchase neural lattice cores from him six weeks ago.

He refused.

Orrel:
"They did not want a staff. They wanted the part of a staff that learns."

### 2. The Forge flagged Kaelith

The moment Orrel checks Kaelith's desired build against Trade Moon sourcing networks, a restricted ping appears:

> SOURCE CONFLICT  
> MAENA BIOLOGIC INTERFACE GROUP  
> SPARK-COMPATIBLE NEURAL LATTICE  
> NOT FOR DIRECT RELEASE

Yraina sees it first and goes still.

Yraina:
"That is not a normal denial."

### 3. Someone else wants the same component

A polite buyer already has a hold on the missing material. The buyer is not named, but the deposit came through a Maena shell company.

This can lead into the Maena facility scene later.

### 4. Orrel recognizes the freighter residue

If Kaelith describes the blue-white, sterile silver, bruised violet signature from the freighter, Orrel knows enough to be unhappy.

Orrel:
"That is not artificing. That is a machine wearing the skin of it."


## If Asha is present

Orrel respects captains who do not pretend to understand crafts they have not learned.

If Asha tries to buy the best answer:

Orrel:
"You can buy time, privacy, and materials. You cannot buy a better first thought."

If Asha asks whether Kaelith should choose the safest option:

Orrel:
"Safe is for ladders and pressure seals. Living weapons need honesty before safety."

If Asha asks what the staff might become:

Orrel:
"That depends on where he takes it, what he asks of it, and what it learns when nobody is speaking."


## If Lysa is present

Lysa should be fascinated and irritated.

Lysa:
"I hate how much sense this makes. I also hate that every answer creates three worse questions."

She can help identify whether a material combination is magically stable.

**Roll: Spellcasting + Reason DT 4**

- **Success:** identify one weak point in a proposed build.
- **2+ net:** suggest a stabilizing material or flourish.
- **Failure:** she over-focuses on theory and Orrel grunts at her.

Orrel:
"Good thought. Wrong bench."


## If Nyrix is present

Nyrix should treat the future staff as something not quite born.

Nyrix:
"It is quiet because it has not learned how to be afraid yet."

If she touches the Unwritten Staff, it should react with a faint pulse. Not because she is the right wielder, but because old magic recognizes old magic.

Do not let Orrel scan her. He is curious, but not stupid.

Orrel:
"No. Whatever she is, I am not putting tools on her without permission from three different people and one of them better be her."


## If Corvin is involved

Corvin can contribute Shaya materials and practical workshop insight, but Orrel is still the master.

Corvin:
"I know enough to know when I am standing in someone else's temple."

Orrel may respect that.

If Corvin helps with Shayawood or Blood of Shaya:

- Reduce time by 10 hours.
- Grant +1 die to the craft roll.
- Orrel gives Corvin one very specific lesson about material listening.


## Possible scene endings

### Ending A: Kaelith chooses a build

Orrel wraps the selected materials in separate cloths and marks each one with a handwritten tag.

Orrel:
"Do not name it yet. Names stick. Let it earn the first one."

### Ending B: Kaelith chooses the Unwritten Staff

Orrel studies him for a long time, then locks the workshop door.

Orrel:
"Fine. Then we stop shopping."

He clears the central bench.

"Now we find out what kind of future you are willing to carry."

### Ending C: Maena hook interrupts

Yraina's tablet chirps once.

She looks at the sourcing denial.

The shop feels smaller.

Yraina:
"We may have found your staff component."

She looks toward the door.

"Unfortunately, someone else found it first."

### Ending D: Orrel refuses the commission for now

Use this only if Kaelith treats the Eldimagus like a trophy or disposable weapon.

Orrel:
"No."

He starts closing drawers.

"Come back when you are asking for a companion instead of permission to own one."


## Quick GM notes

- Orrel should feel obscure, not weak.
- He does not advertise because serious work attracts dangerous customers.
- He can make an Eldimagus, but he will not rush one.
- The staff should become a future character, not a stat stick.
- The best default build for Kaelith is probably **The Umbral Pilgrim** or **The Unwritten Staff**.
- If you want the scene tied tightly to Session 20's mystery, use the Maena sourcing conflict as the bridge.
