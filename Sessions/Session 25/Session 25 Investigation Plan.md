# Session 25 Investigation Plan

```yaml
objective:
  develop_next_session:
    - preserve continuity from the actual Session 24 endpoint
    - convert unresolved campaign material into player-driven options
    - avoid prematurely resolving major mysteries
    - produce a modular, adaptable session framework

source_handling:
  authoritative:
    - Sessions/Session 23/Session 23 Log.md
    - Sessions/Session 24/Session 24 Log.md
  interpretive:
    - Sessions/Session 23/Session 23 Scratchpad.md
    - Sessions/Session 24/Session 24 Scratchpad.md
  rules:
    - treat logged player choices and revealed facts as canon
    - treat scratchpad material as proposals unless confirmed by the logs
    - distinguish resolved, partially resolved, dormant, and invalidated threads
    - preserve contradictions for later adjudication rather than silently reconciling them

investigation_phases:
  - id: reconstruct_endpoint
    task:
      - identify the exact physical, emotional, political, and informational state at Session 24's end
      - record party location, possessions, allies, obligations, injuries, upgrades, and pending actions
      - identify the immediate next-action implied by the final exchange

  - id: extract_continuity
    task:
      - compile all confirmed revelations from Sessions 23-24
      - compile all player commitments and declared intentions
      - compile all unresolved questions explicitly raised by players
      - identify consequences already triggered but not yet delivered

  - id: classify_threads
    categories:
      - ancient_history
      - Terra_and_Soma
      - Hand_of_Eterra
      - Spark
      - Maena
      - Ternan_politics
      - freighter_investigation
      - divine_and_catalyst_relationships
      - crew_resources_and_power_growth
      - personal_character_arcs
    output:
      - status: active | dormant | completed | ambiguous
      - urgency
      - player_interest
      - faction_interest
      - potential_cost
      - evidence_base

  - id: map_factions
    task:
      - identify every faction now aware of or adjacent to the crew's discoveries
      - determine what each faction knows, suspects, wants, and can do
      - distinguish direct pressure from background pressure
      - avoid making any faction omniscient without evidence

  - id: identify_design_constraints
    task:
      - list mysteries that should remain unresolved
      - list themes that must remain intact
      - identify content that would overexpose Terra, Soma, Spark, Maena, or divine lore
      - identify prior promises that require follow-through
      - identify upgrades or rewards that should create consequences without dominating play

  - id: derive_session_function
    task:
      - determine what the next session must accomplish narratively
      - select a small number of high-value functions
      - prioritize meaningful choice, pressure, consequence, and forward motion
      - avoid repeating the previous session's exploration structure

  - id: generate_modular_options
    task:
      - create several interchangeable pressure modules
      - create several player-selected operational directions
      - define what each option reveals, risks, costs, and advances
      - ensure no single option is mandatory
      - include at least one grounded, character-facing option and one strategic option

  - id: choose_pressure_model
    task:
      - select one primary external pressure
      - select one secondary pressure that can advance offscreen
      - define escalation thresholds without scripting exact encounters
      - ensure pressure reacts to player choices

  - id: design_session_shape
    structure:
      - opening_state_and_reorientation
      - immediate_decision_or_problem
      - information_or_negotiation_phase
      - player_selected_direction
      - consequence_or_reveal
      - end_state_with_new_choice
    rules:
      - use modular beats rather than a fixed scene sequence
      - preserve room for player priorities
      - end with a concrete changed condition

  - id: continuity_qa
    checks:
      - every major element is supported by the logs or clearly labeled as new proposal
      - no scratchpad-only plan is treated as completed history
      - unresolved mysteries remain appropriately unresolved
      - NPC knowledge matches established evidence
      - player agency is preserved
      - the session does not become a lore lecture, shopping sequence, or forced railroad
      - the final state creates at least one actionable next-session lead

deliverables:
  - confirmed_current_state
  - unresolved_thread_matrix
  - faction_and_pressure_matrix
  - thematic_and_reveal_constraints
  - candidate_session_functions
  - modular_session_outline
  - end_state_options
  - continuity_risks
  - questions_requiring_gm_decision

prohibitions:
  - do not write read-aloud text yet
  - do not determine exact scenes, encounters, stat blocks, or dialogue
  - do not resolve the central mysteries prematurely
  - do not assume unused scratchpad ideas occurred
  - do not force every active thread into one session
```
