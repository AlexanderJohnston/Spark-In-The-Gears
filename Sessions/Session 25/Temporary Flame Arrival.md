# Temporary Flame Arrival

Narrator:Nara reaches out, and the Skull of Xal disappears.

For the brief instant between places, Asha and Kaelith remember the Flame as they first saw it from the Lone Star's bridge: one of two artifact-class dreadnoughts hanging beside the colossal Skull, parked opposite the Word of Xal like a continent. It had been too large to understand as a ship. Its ancient hull simply continued beyond the edges of the scry screens, dark and motionless against the violet light pouring from Xal's empty eyes.

Then reality closes around them.

They stand inside one of the Flame of Knowledge's docking bays. The chamber is vast, but almost entirely empty. Long rows of recessed berths and extended mooring arms wait for vessels that never returned. Old fire-aspected guide marks glow dimly along the deck, illuminating blackened metal, sealed pressure doors, and launch apertures large enough to swallow the Lone Star.

Only two ships remain. A pair of emergency corvettes rest in neighboring cradles near the far bulkhead, compact only by the standards of this place. Their scorched hulls are intact, their launch rails still connected, and faint amber readiness lights pulse beneath ten thousand years of dust.

Nothing else moves. No deck crews answer their arrival. No machinery wakes to challenge them.

The silence is so complete that the settling click of Axiom's rings carries across the bay.
