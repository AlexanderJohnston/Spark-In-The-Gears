# Flame Journey to Bridge Plan

## Purpose

Move Asha, Kaelith, and Nara from the Flame of Knowledge's docking bay to the Web of Divinity without turning the journey into a conventional dungeon crawl.

The experiential progression is:

**abandonment -> impossible knowledge -> surveillance -> denied passage -> negotiated audience -> temporal awe**

## Scene constraints

- The Flame is otherwise empty for this visit. Do not introduce arachnidrake groups, Cinder Hatchlings, Cindara, explorers, or other personnel.
- **Kek is present on the bridge as its current Guardian.** He is an arachnidrake whom Jerek helped take up the mantle to contain the Flame's madness. Present him as visibly burdened but initially lucid: Kek maintains containment while the Swarm interfaces with the Web. Do not turn him into an immediate combat encounter or resolve the question of his eventual corruption during this opening.
- The Swarm remains aboard because it is the distributed intelligence tending and quarantining the Web and Archive.
- The two emergency corvettes in the docking bay are the fallback extraction route.
- Nara knows how to move cautiously through the vessel but does not command the Flame or the Swarm.
- The Archive should tempt the characters without becoming a prolonged research or loot sequence.
- Reaching the bridge should require recognition by the Swarm, not victory over it.
- Asha and Kaelith do not connect directly to the Web. The Swarm mediates their question.

## Sequence tracker

### Stage 1 — Empty docking bay

**Status: completed**

- Nara translocates Asha and Kaelith into an immense, otherwise empty docking bay.
- Two intact-looking emergency corvettes remain in neighboring launch cradles.
- Nara identifies them as the fallback exit if she cannot translocate the group away.
- She leads the group between the corvettes to a warped personnel hatch behind the launch-control station.

### Stage 2 — Burned docking spine

**Status: completed**

- The group enters a broad service corridor of blackened bronze, old fire sigils, damaged conduits, and fractured sections held together by pale fire silk.
- The atmosphere is hot and dry, smelling of ash, heated metal, and ozone with a copper-soot taste.
- Ancient machinery wakes briefly and falls silent.
- A faint, irregular patter can be heard within the walls.
- Nara keeps moving rather than stopping to explain the ship.

**Transition condition:** Reach the first intact junction or indexing threshold opening into the outer Archive.

### Stage 3 — Outer Archive

**Status: completed**

- The corridor opens into immense galleries containing knowledge scales rather than books.
- Scales vary greatly in size and carry magical inscriptions or stored impressions.
- Damaged indexing systems still move and reorganize selected scales.
- A few scales react to the party's presence, magic, or unspoken interests.
- Axiom can sense deliberate absences in the collection but cannot identify what was removed.
- Nara prevents the group from becoming distracted by unrestricted investigation.
- The group has reached the first intact indexing threshold and can see the outermost scale galleries beyond it.

**Transition condition:** The party recognizes the Archive's value and continues toward increasing web density.

### Stage 4 — Signs of the Swarm

**Status: completed**

- Individual spiders appear first and behave too deliberately to be animals.
- Their numbers increase as the group approaches the bridge.
- Large groups turn, stop, or retreat with perfect coordination.
- Fire silk changes from structural repair material into surveillance lines and intentional barriers.
- Nara becomes quieter and more tactical as the Swarm's attention concentrates.
- The group is following the command route beyond the outer galleries as coordinated spiders begin appearing among increasingly deliberate silk lines.

**Transition condition:** The Swarm openly acknowledges the intruders or directs them toward the webbed threshold.

### Stage 5 — Webbed threshold *(complete)*

**Status: current**

- The route to the bridge is completely sealed by fire silk and innumerable spiders.
- The Swarm forms a face, symbols, text, or coordinated motion to refuse passage.
- Nara cannot order it aside and must state why the group has come.
- The impossible hole in possibility and the crew's anomalous survival provide the reason for the Swarm to listen.
- Asha and Kaelith decide how much truth to disclose and may participate in earning the audience.
- Violence would close the route rather than solve the obstacle.
- The Swarm has closed every side route and concentrated ahead, bringing the armed group to the fully sealed bridge threshold.

**Transition condition:** The Swarm agrees to mediate one narrowly framed question.

### Stage 6 — Controlled passage *(complete)*

**Status: pending**

- The Swarm opens only a narrow path through its bodies and silk.
- The passage reseals behind the group, containing them nearer the Web.
- Minor temporal inconsistencies begin: repeated footsteps, displaced shadows, or glimpses of mutually exclusive positions.
- Nara requires the group to confirm the shared present aloud.
- The Swarm remains close enough to close the route instantly.

**Transition condition:** The group crosses onto the bridge without directly touching the temporal matrix.

### Stage 7 — The bridge and Web *(current)*

**Status: pending**

- The bridge surrounds a well of white fire connected to ancient temporal machinery.
- The Web hangs above it at contradictory distances, branching into overlapping possibilities.
- Kek waits at the temporal matrix as the bridge's Guardian, using his mantle to contain the madness pressing through the Web while the Swarm performs the comparison.
- The Swarm remains between the visitors and the physical interface.
- The trapped darkness is implied through attention, absence, or shadows without a visible source.
- The Swarm requires one precisely framed counterfactual question.
- Stop the traversal sequence once the question is accepted. The Web revelation is its own scene.
