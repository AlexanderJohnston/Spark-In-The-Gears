Session 17 summary

 - The crew arrived on Shaya, resupplied through Cormac, and Nyrix led Asha and Kaelith to Bord, Voria’s paladin, in the drifter square.
 - Their meeting was interrupted by a sudden rooftop attack from two Shayan true mages using destruction/voidflame. Both Asha and Kaelith were hit once, and Bord threw up a 20-meter life ward to protect the square.
 - Asha used Greater Illusion and then Chameleon to confuse targeting and vanish in the chaos. Kaelith closed range and killed both attackers, one with Implode and the other with a devastating greater bolt.
 - After the fight, Kaelith checked the corpse with Essence Sight and found nothing obviously unusual. Bord insisted they relocate and took Asha, Kaelith, and Nyrix aboard his corvette while the rest of the crew withdrew safely to orbit.
 - On the way up through the branches, Bord revealed he knew their growing reputation on Ternus and correctly guessed they were on Shaya for catalysts.
 - In a private, heavily warded recovery room, Kaelith openly told Bord about Nefarius, the plague-tainted armor, and Xal’s blessing on both suits.
 - Bord did not react like a loyal servant of Voria. Instead he admitted his faith was broken, argued that the gods use people, and chose to help them. After Asha said that if she lived long enough she knew she could kill Nefarius, Bord offered immediate access to the life catalyst.
 - Bord opened a tear directly into Shaya’s inner chamber using life magic and a dragon scale.
 - In the catalyst chamber, Kaelith was seized by Shaya’s light and recognized by both the Tree and his Shayan lineage. He realized life was already threaded through all the aspects within him and sensed some deeper boundary between Dream and Life.
 - Asha catalyzed more gradually. She felt life settle alongside dream, void, and air, sensed the same current of life magic tied to Seraphine’s resurrection, and came away with a much sharper sense that life and death are inseparable.
 - Nyrix drank first, froze in vision, then transformed in golden light into a larger, almost-human draconic form. She revealed that Shaya and Kumoshi are linked, that Kumoshi is not gone, and asked to help end the war.
 - Nyrix then vowed her life to Kaelith. Kaelith refused the hierarchy of that oath and instead named her his sister and equal.
 - The session ends inside Shaya’s catalyst chamber with Bord stunned, Asha overwhelmed, Kaelith freshly changed, and Nyrix newly awakened and self-aware.

Carry forward: Asha and Kaelith both catalyzed Life, gained 3 Life resistance and 3 Life spells, may receive a later Shaya boon, and both earned 5 EXP.

Loose thread: it’s still unclear whether the assassins were after Bord, the party, or both.

Narrator-style intro

Narrator: The words hang in the chamber after Kaelith speaks.

The pool of Shaya still glows in its basin at the center of the room, too small to look important until its light touches something living. Then it is everywhere. Gold in the grain of the walls. Gold in Asha’s tears. Gold along Kaelith’s hands where the last of revelation still trembles. Gold on Nyrix’s new shape, pale and strange and unmistakably dragon despite the almost-human face. Gold caught hard in Bord’s stare.

The room smells of warm wood, clean linen, herbs, and life magic packed so thick it feels like breath against your skin. For one long beat, nobody says a damn thing.

Nyrix stands changed, awake, carrying memory she did not have an hour ago. Asha is still breathing like she only just remembered how. Kaelith has only barely come back from the edge of something vast enough to know his name. And Bord — paladin, cynic, survivor — looks like a man watching doctrine split open under his boots.

Then the room starts again.

Bord: “Right,” he says, low and rough. “So we’ve gone from rooftop murder to divine revelation to a dragon swearing an oath in under an hour. That feels excessive, even for you lot.”

His eyes move from Nyrix, to Kaelith, to the golden pool.

Bord: “Somebody tell me what just happened. Properly.”
