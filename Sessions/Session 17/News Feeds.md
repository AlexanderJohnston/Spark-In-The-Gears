News Feeds:

Ternus
- The docks have been fully repaired from the recent terrorist attack and the incident blamed on Virkon.
- Spark fuel is rapidly proliferating as Ternus ramps up production. The government's hope is to get ahead of the trade moon and liquidate as much spark fuel from reserve as possible, converting it into credits they can trade once the moon arrives.
- The Trade Moon is now officially expected within a week. Economic forecasts claim it will bring in staggering revenue, but only if Ternus can meet the aggressive trade quotas over the coming decade.
- Rumors are spreading of a corrupted catalyst site, though it's not clear at all what is meant by this. It's being labeled as "spiritual corruption" and "unstable", and relic hunters are warned from visiting unknown catalysts.

New Texas
- Governor Rourke's office is pushing the Lone Star as a symbol of resilience after the Virkon disaster, with state media quietly shaping Captain Morrow and her crew into wartime folk heroes.
- Dockworker chatter says military procurement never really slowed after Virkon. Fresh anti-wyrm refits, armor orders, and tank deployments are still being pushed through the yards under priority clearance.
- Market prices are already surging ahead of the Trade Moon. Ship coatings, spell-armor components, catalyst gear, and high-end magitech have all risen sharply as merchants prepare for a buying frenzy.

Shaya
- Reports filter back from Shaya describing a daylight magical attack in a crowded port square at the base of the Great Tree against high-profile paladins, renewing fears that the war is spreading beyond open battlefields.
- The First Spellship has recently re-appeared in orbit around Shaya. Some speculate that Voria has learned information which she refuses to share with the rest of the Shayan Confederacy.

Virkon
- Conspiracy feeds insist the official Virkon narrative around the dock attack is incomplete, citing scrubbed footage, missing telemetry, and how quietly the state moved from mourning to reconstruction.

Inura
- Grey-market boards are buzzing that whoever controls spark fuel and direct access to the Trade Moon will also control the next phase of the war.
- Confederacy officials continue to frame recent instability as proof that Ternus must secure its trade position before hostile powers can isolate it again.
- Analysts warn that the Trade Moon will be more of a political event than an economic event, with every major power expected to test Ternus influence the moment it arrives. High-profile individuals are urged to relocate to New Texas during this time period and to use proxies for any trade negotiation.
