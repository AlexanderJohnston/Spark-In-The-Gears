# Session 30 — Take Exchange Nine

**Run this file.** [[Session 30 - Continuity]] contains source notes and corrections; [[Session 30 Log]] is ready for play notes.

**Session aim:** fight Vesk's people, disrupt Maena's operation, and settle Exchange Nine's fate. Allow roughly three hours.

Everything beyond the logged opening is GM preparation, including the encounter profiles and timings.

## Read this before starting

- **Start:** Asha and Kaelith have just walked into sample custody at Survey Exchange Nine. Each has a research tablet. Their memories are restored and their assessor cover still works.
- **Outside:** Daal and Senn are arguing over custody records in receiving. Vesk has ordered them to provide samples and stop outgoing shipments. He is coming here.
- **Already learned:** Maena wants to animate a manufactured god and use it to reach the power associated with Sol/Terra. Kaelith identified the Hand's Void contamination in the lab and privately told Asha.
- **Give them a short opportunity:** inspect samples, preserve evidence, contact support, prepare equipment, or arrange an ambush. Then bring Vesk into the situation they created.
- **Vesk's move:** requisition the assessors for compulsory continued research, seize their tablets, and deny their departure. His guards move to enforce it. This is the intended trigger for combat.
- **The fight has two useful targets:** seize Vesk's packet and stop Senn's outbound report. Afterward, disable the freight-loading machinery to interrupt this station's research shipments.
- **Resolve the station:** surviving opposition, evidence, machinery, and the party's exit. Reaching the mines can be the next session.

**Three-hour budget:** opening and preparation 25 minutes; Vesk confrontation 20; combat 45–60; disruption and departure 35–45; closing decisions 15. If short on time, put the important records in Vesk's packet and skip a separate archive search.

## 1. A few minutes alone

**Purpose:** let the players turn their earned access into an advantage. **Cast:** Asha, Kaelith, the receiving attendant; Yraina nearby. **Setting:** a comparison bench beside sealed sample racks, with receiving visible through the doorway.

> Narrator: The attendant sets two sealed orange samples on the bench beside an empty transfer vessel. Through the doorway, Daal and Senn are still arguing over a shipment entry. Your tablets carry the laboratory's full trial history. A small indicator above the racks changes to **OUTBOUND DISPATCH HELD**.
>
> Attendant: “The comparison stock you asked for. Vesk will want your findings when he arrives.”

Let the players act. The attendant fetches supplies. **Default:** Yraina joins them with the restoration case; she can keep watch or call support while Daal and Senn remain occupied.

### Give useful answers quickly

| If they…                                  | Give them…                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Examine the empty vessel                  | No physical defect or residue explaining the failure. Asha can establish that with ordinary competent inspection.                                                                                                                      |
| Examine retained stock with essence sight | The same Void contamination Kaelith saw in the lab. These are comparison portions of the already affected reserve, held separately from the experimental apparatus.                                                                    |
| Compare custody entries                   | The sampled material was affected before entering Daal's apparatus. The chain includes the Trade Moon reserve; these are **not untouched samples taken directly at a mine face**. They do not establish where the contamination began. |
| Ask for original surveys                  | The attendant points to Survey Records. The shipment index is open; the protected survey bundle requires a staff login or Vesk's packet.                                                                                               |
| Preserve what they already have           | Let them save the displayed specifications, trials, and Sol brief locally on the tablets. No roll; record who holds them. A separate transmitted backup still needs a working connection.                                              |
| Prepare weapons                           | Asha's hammer is in her pocket space. **Axiom is aboard the Lone Star**, available through Kaelith's established Void Rift retrieval if its requirements are met. No retrieval occurred in Session 29.                                 |

Keep the comparison brief. It gives them grounds to demand source access.

**Arrival:** Vesk arrives eight in-world minutes after the opening. At five minutes, the communicator announces his approach to the headquarters transfer chamber. Advance time for substantial actions. If the PCs interrupt his route, honor that.

**Nyrix:** offer the overdue check-in. Prepared default: a message can pass through the open headquarters gateway. Give her their location, return route, and next deadline. She remains with the Lone Star near headquarters; an independent private link across the station's unknown distance is unconfirmed.

## 2. Vesk takes ownership

**Purpose:** turn the institution's coercion into an immediate conflict. **Cast:** Vesk, two executive escorts, Senn, Daal, the trio. **Setting:** receiving, with the gateway, dispatch booth, and dock corridor all visible.

Locate the guards and exits before the demand. Vesk arrives through the headquarters passage with **two executive guards**, relocated from the director's suite.

> Vesk Aruun: “Daal says you've found something her team missed. Show me.”

Let the PCs answer or spring their plan. Vesk knows only what has been reported to him.

After hearing them, he signs a protected reassignment to retain these useful specialists:

> Vesk Aruun: “Your household placement is suspended. You will continue this work under my authority. Hand over the tablets. My people will take you back.”
>
> Meret Daal: “I asked for their assessment. I did not ask you to take them.”
>
> Vesk Aruun: “You asked for a solution. Get back to your laboratory.”

Senn signals for restraints. Yraina disputes the order; Vesk refuses to release them. Their assessor cover has made them valuable enough to requisition.

**Start initiative when a guard advances to restrain someone, a PC attacks, or someone attempts a contested seizure.** Nobody is automatically disarmed or restrained. Vesk authorizes force against resistance; Senn wants to make an example of them.

A decisive bargain, intimidation, or ambush can change the encounter. If they submit, play the attempted escort and their next action; don't force an attack or skip to completed capture.

### Voices and priorities

| Person | Play them this way | When control breaks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vesk** | Quiet, proprietary, expects obedience. “Your usefulness is the reason you're staying.” | Protects his packet, directs guards, then retreats toward the gateway. Can surrender for his life; no guaranteed escape. |
| **Senn** | Contempt for servants and scientists. “All that cleverness. Let's see it get you out of restraints.” | Runs to dispatch, sends a report, then bargains or flees when protection collapses. |
| **Daal** | Proud of the research; resents losing the assessors and being overruled. | Moves the attendant clear and shuts sample shields. Can identify machinery or comply with demands; she does not become a combat ally automatically. |
| **Yraina** | Controlled fury at Vesk's assumption of ownership. | Protects the crew. Ask the players whether they want her on the booth, an exit, or civilians; use her established abilities. |
| **Attendant** | Wants to survive and avoid blame. | Takes cover, opens ordinary doors if safely asked, and can identify the freight controls. |

## 3. Fight for receiving

**Purpose:** win control of the station's working junction and prevent Vesk from taking people and evidence away.

### Put these five places on the table

Distances are prepared defaults. Receiving is about 20 metres across; there is no hidden anti-magic field.

| Place | Position and use |
| --- | --- |
| **Sample custody** | PCs' starting side room; one doorway into receiving. Bench and sealed racks provide cover. Nobody begins locked in. |
| **Freight lanes** | Across the centre; waist-high crates provide cover. A powered loading cradle and its motor cabinet handle the heavy research consignments. |
| **Dispatch booth** | About 10 m from custody; glass frontage, ordinary door, terminal, and an accessible cable trunk. Senn operates from here. |
| **Headquarters passage** | About 12 m from custody; Vesk and his escorts arrive here. It is the established return route. The navigator remains at the headquarters end. |
| **Dock corridor** | About 12 m from custody; two dock guards are stationed here. The berthed cargo vessel is another 8 m beyond. Survey Records opens off this corridor. |

Introduce the cradle, sample shields, booth wiring, and gateway before combat. Cover adds +2 difficulty to ranged shots. Booth glass is not ballistic protection. Sample shields can stay closed throughout.

### One finite roster

| Opponent | Start and first priority |
| --- | --- |
| **2 receiving guards** | Freight lanes; attempt the restraint, then hold the custody doorway. |
| **2 dock guards** | Dock corridor; one moves toward the booth, the other covers the corridor. They must move through the space to join. |
| **2 executive escorts** | With Vesk at the gateway; protect him and the packet. |
| **Vesk** | Gateway side of receiving; commands, uses cover and his sidearm, retreats if beaten. |
| **Senn** | Receiving desk, moving to the booth; prioritizes the report over shooting. |

Group initiative by guard pair. When Vesk falls or half the guards are disabled, survivors seek cover and terms. No unannounced reinforcements.

### Ready-to-use combat profiles

**Prepared NPC defaults; existing campaign profiles take precedence.** Attack pools include Accuracy.

| Profile | HP / Fortitude | Brace / Dodge / Skepticism | Armor / magic resistance | Initiative | Attack |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Receiving or dock guard ×4 | 25 / 3 | 2 / 3 / 2 | 3 / 0 | 6 + d6 | 8 dice, success on 5–6; rifle, 6 physical, range 30 |
| Executive escort ×2 | 30 / 4 | 3 / 3 / 3 | 4 / 0 | 7 + d6 | 9 dice, success on 4–6; rifle, 6 physical, range 30 |
| Vesk | 25 / 3 | 2 / 3 / 3 | 3 / 0 | 6 + d6 | 10 dice, success on 5–6; pistol, 5 physical, range 12 |
| Senn | 20 / 2 | 2 / 2 / 2 | 2 / 0 | 4 + d6 | 7 dice, success on 5–6; pistol, 4 physical, range 10 |

Rifles use Mark VII physical mode; pistols are Mark IX (Vesk) and Mark V (Senn). All target Dodge; no added armor penetration. Add extra successes to damage, then subtract armor. Use normal combat rules and two actions per turn. Relevant NPC skills: 6 dice; Vesk's social pressure: 8. Resolve restraints as contested actions.

Let powerful PCs win decisively. The objectives supply pressure; defeated guards stay defeated.

### The one combat countdown: Senn's report

At the booth, Senn spends an action starting a report. Announce the lit terminal and: **“Protected research compromised. Sending identities and receiving footage.”**

- Start at **0 of 3**. Advance once at the end of each round after activation. At **3**, the report transmits.
- **Stop it:** interrupt power or the visible cable, cancel with Senn's logged-in controls (one action, no roll if unopposed), or disable the terminal. A remote technical intrusion under fire is DT 2 and takes an action in reach of an appropriate interface.
- Taking out Senn alone leaves the transmission running. An interrupted attempt stays stopped unless someone visibly restarts it.
- If sent, the protected channel receives the available identities and footage. Record the exposure. No immediate reinforcements or self-destruct follow.

Vesk's briefing cartridge is in his carried hard case. Show any retreat toward the gateway and resolve interception. His death leaves the case intact unless the actual attack destroys it.

**Gateway:** stays open by default. If the headquarters navigator hears danger and attempts closure, they warn everyone to clear the threshold. Give a full round to respond. No surprise lethal closure or hidden boss fight.

## 4. Make the victory count

**Purpose:** settle Exchange Nine before choosing another target. **Cast:** survivors and the trio. **Setting:** the controlled receiving floor, booth, and records office.

End initiative once resistance ends. Present the following opportunities.

### Take the evidence

Vesk's packet contains the first three items below. Survey Records has the survey and route duplicates; the booth holds the local dispatch history. No single surviving NPC is required.

| Evidence | Tell the players | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Protected instructions** | Research reports go to **the Custodian**. Vesk is to keep the animation program outside ordinary management review. | Identifies the authority directing this work and its authenticated reporting address. |
| **Order reversal** | Kyrsa first endorsed a suspension, then reversed it after a private consultation arranged through that channel. | Concrete evidence of outside influence. Yraina can react; the document does not diagnose her mother's binding. |
| **Source survey and approach** | Reports record extraction of dead-soul material and divine remains, corroborating Kek's account. A current cargo itinerary gives the approach, docking instructions, and call sign for the mine's receiving berth. | A usable next destination. It is a route to a mining outpost, not Terra or the Custodian's guaranteed location. |
| **Dispatch history** | Connects the lot codes on the PCs' tablets to this station's shipments and Vesk's deliveries. | Evidence tying the laboratory, relay, and protected research channel together. |

Give readable findings without a roll. They provide neither Sol's full explanation nor a contamination cure.

**Vesk alive:** confirms the Custodian's authority and mine itinerary; bargains with access and protection. He does not know Kyrsa's binding mechanism.

### Break a real part of the operation

Show the distinction between **freight machinery** and **the passage controls** before the players choose a target.

| Choice | Immediate result and lasting limit |
| --- | --- |
| **Disable the freight-loading drive** | The motor/control cabinet is beside the heavy cradle. Two minutes of competent isolation and removal, or actual destructive force, takes it out. Research consignments cannot be bulk-loaded through this bay. **Replacement and recommissioning take at least 48 hours.** Small hand-carried loads remain possible; a reroute needs actual transport and staff. |
| **Take the queued research packet and consignments** | Vesk loses those actual records and goods. Secure heavy cargo aboard the berthed vessel if taking it; do not treat its mass as pocket inventory. Copies elsewhere do not recreate stolen physical stock. |
| **Seize the station and its controls** | The PCs can keep the dispatch hold, secure prisoners, and control local traffic. Continuing occupation needs a crew or an agreed handover; the empty station will not guard itself after they leave. |
| **Destroy the transit apparatus** | Stops this established connection. Warn that it also removes their easiest way back and can endanger its operator. First establish another exit and where the navigator is. It does not destroy the mines or the headquarters laboratory. |

**Simplest complete win:** take the packet, disable the loading drive, and get out. A transmitted report doesn't erase those gains. The headquarters laboratory survives unless separately targeted; its two-week recalibration setback requires damage to its actual calibrated assembly.

### Leave, hold, or continue

- **Return to the Lone Star:** gateway → headquarters service/loading route → nearby pad. Daal's escort, Vesk's token, or negotiated release can help. Alerted headquarters guards may challenge them; resolve against the surviving roster, with negotiation, magic, or force available.
- **Take the cargo vessel:** secure cooperation from its survival-minded crew or take control using actual party capabilities. Its prepared archive contains the return approach and mine itinerary. It offers an exit if the gateway is lost.
- **Go to the mines:** settle the station first, then use the itinerary. End on departure or at the mine's receiving berth; save the assault for next session.
- **Hold Exchange Nine:** notify Nyrix, assign controls and prisoner watch, and arrange relief with available personnel.

Let the PCs decide how to treat survivors and allow willing staff to leave. Daal's cooperation does not erase her involvement.

## Close the session with five answers

Record these in [[Session 30 Log]]:

1. **Who controls Exchange Nine, and what opposition remains?**
2. **What was actually disabled or taken?** Record when the 48-hour repair interval begins if the loading drive was lost.
3. **Who holds the evidence, and did Senn's report transmit?**
4. **Where are the PCs, Yraina, prisoners, and any evacuees?** Record the gateway and navigator's status.
5. **What is the next destination and the next agreed contact with Nyrix?**

End on the result they earned: a seized relay, a damaged operation and escape, or a contested withdrawal with evidence. The next session should start from that result.
