# Maena Tech Mage Escorts

They are the same two people who arrived with rifles, not additional reinforcements. Code names are optional GM labels.

Attack pools use **Agility + Ranged + Accuracy** or **armored Strength + Melee + Accuracy**, following the local skill reference. Meet the relevant defense to hit; each extra success adds damage. TN means the minimum die face that counts as a success, not the target's defense.

Use the applicable protection: Armor against ordinary physical weapon damage; Magic Resistance or the relevant aspect resistance against magic, according to the spell. **Do not add natural Fire Resistance to Magic Resistance.** The suits provide the higher Fire protection while functioning.

**Current encounter:** the party has prepared an ambush using Kaelith's Flame Reading. Resolve the established held actions and surprise normally. The escorts' armor and innate defenses apply, but their gear grants no automatic detection, extra turns, or retroactive defensive casting.

## First turns and morale

**On arrival:** both have rifles in hand, their sidearms and blades stowed, and no held actions carried into the encounter. Show the exceptional white-and-blue armor, sealed helmets, and injector housings before the party's opening attack resolves.

- **Bastion:** on the first available turn, move into a position protecting Vesk and attack the immediate threat with the held rifle. If already engaged and a blade is preferable, spend the required action to draw it, then attack. Armor and equipment do not automatically intercept attacks aimed at Vesk.
- **Kestrel:** on the first available turn, move toward usable cover with an angle on Kaelith, then fire if an action remains. If already well positioned, attack and hold an action for an eligible counterspell or to cover Vesk's movement.
- **Injury:** use healing when it will restore a meaningful amount and the escort can legally act. A potion consumes an actual dose and the required opportunity to use it.
- **Withdrawal:** if one escort falls, the survivor prioritizes getting Vesk through the passage while it remains usable. If Vesk is down or captured and escape is cut off, survival or surrender becomes reasonable. These are elite professionals, not compulsory last-stand opponents.
- **Information:** their comms require a deliberate report. Senn's canceled transmission remains canceled. These suits neither reveal hidden characters automatically nor transmit the private conversations they missed.

## Adaptation and calculation notes

- **Armor:** the pasted biography says Mark XI, but its equipment list says Mark V. These sheets explicitly use the Mark IX Heavy and Standard loadouts chosen for this encounter, with their listed general Magic Resistance. No undefined Mark XI or unique Vrykar powers are imported.
- **Race:** Human is retained from Archimedes. Maena is their employer; these sheets do not add an unprovided Inuran racial package. Each Human's attribute adjustment is already included in their final attributes.
- **Advancement:** attributes, skill ranks, and purchased defenses are advanced NPC choices, not a claim to match a starting character's point budget. Both retain one Fire catalyzation.
- **Perks and flaws:** all listed attribute effects are already applied. For Kind of a Dick, the user's supplied -1 Charisma version takes precedence over differently summarized local versions.
- **HP:** Tech Mage base 15 + Fortitude × 5 = **45 / 40**.
- **Spell Pool:** Tech Mage base 2 + Will × 3 + Fire Magic 3 = **14 / 17**. This replaces the pasted template's unexplained pool of 6.
- **Initiative:** Tech Mage bonus 2 + Agility = **6 / 7**, then roll d6 once for combat. This replaces the template's listed 3.
- **Strength:** personal 6 / 4 + armor 4 = **10 / 8**. Heavy melee: 10 + 5 + 0 = **15 dice**. Support melee: 8 + 3 + 1 = **12 dice**. This uses the local skill summary's Melee (Strength); if the live table instead uses Agility for ordinary melee attacks, those pools become **9 / 9**. Spell Riposte's separate Agility rule is not granted to these Tech Mages.
- **Ranged:** Agility + Ranged + Accuracy 2 = **10 / 12 dice**. Do not add Accuracy again.
