There's an older drifter there with a face like boot leather. One boot is up on a crate while he sits on a folding stool behind the stall, cleaning the lens of an old set of field goggles with the kind of care some people reserve for relics. At first glance the stall looks forgettable.

Upon closer inspection though, there's a lot of interesting stuff here. Field kits. Rifle mags. Repair paste. Sealant. Coils of cable. Spare comm beads. Stacked canisters of ration-gel beside bottles of drifter beers with little wax tags tied around the necks marking potion effects in quick shorthand. This is not tourist junk. 

Seraphine slows to a stop in front of him, and for the first time since landing there is something openly familiar in her posture.

The drifter glances up, squints once, then twice. His whole face breaks open. "Well I'll be damned sideways. Sera?" He pushes himself up off the stool with a grunt and spreads his hands. "Fifteen years and ye've still got that same look like ye're about t'complain about my paperwork."

Seraphine actually laughs. Not much, but enough to prove she remembers how. "Cormac."

Cormac barks a laugh and rounds the stall to clasp her forearm, then gives her a once-over like he is checking for missing pieces. "Thought ye'd either made admiral or got yerself killed by now. Glad t'see ye picked the less inconvenient option." His eyes flick past the rest of the crew in quick practiced beats. He stares at Kaelith for a few seconds longer than everyone else. Then he lands on Asha and gives the woman a veteran's nod. "And would ye look at that. Ye brought me customers."
