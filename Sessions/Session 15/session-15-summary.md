## Read-Aloud (Primary)

Narrator:At the Skull of Xal, you did not waste the stop. Asha put her new armor to the test against Sargoth, Kaelith's old spell armor still wet with Nefarius. She gave the order and her armor answered. Black-violet tendrils struck fast and tore the corruption out by the roots. When it was over, the Nefarius taint was gone. Golden crystals were left behind. Sargoth remained alive with dream and Xal's void woven through it.

Then Seraphine saw the crystals and went cold. She said they looked too much like Spark. Ternus runs on that stuff. Your old homes were fueled by it. The docks. Ships. Forges. Scry-killers. Nobody knows where it comes from, but the Inurans are tied to it somehow. Aran took that seriously right away.

You stayed ten days and trained hard. Kaelith studied under Nara and opened the way to divination. Asha trained illusion and sharpened her casting with Voria, then learned Dracon style from Aran the hard way. Corvin was pulled off by Kazon for artificer work. Lysa trained with Nara and came back with that pleased look of someone who got exactly what they wanted.

Your marines spent their leave like happy idiots with good luck. Fifty-three got drunk, ate flesh of Xal, and returned stronger, but undeniably demons now. Four other demons asked to join the crew and were accepted. Later on the bridge, the talk turned to catalyzation and what happens if one of you goes too far. Asha said it plainly; if she starts becoming the next Nefarius, you kill her. Rho agreed.

Now the next problem is already in motion. The trade moon reaches Ternus in two weeks but you are not waiting for it. Tamsin nearly died and one life mage is not enough, so you set a course for Shaya first. Kaelith's homeworld. The place that exiled him. The home of the Consortium, the birthplace of the Confederacy.

## GM Notes

### Major Plot Developments

- **Asha's armor can purge Nefarius corruption.** This was demonstrated live on Sargoth. The void-catalyzed Mk. V armor, enhanced by Xal's latent spell, stripped Nefarius's blood from Kaelith's old armor and converted it into golden crystals of unknown composition. The armor experienced ecstatic satisfaction doing this — Asha felt an addictive rush she had to consciously pull back from. The purged Sargoth is now dream/void with no Nefarius taint.

- **Spark = crystallized magic?** Seraphine identified the golden crystal residue as visually identical to Spark, Ternus's classified fuel source. Spark powers military and civilian infrastructure without mages. The Inurans are involved in its production. Aran took this very seriously and flagged it as something they need to investigate. This is a major thread connecting Ternus, the Inurans, and potentially Nefarius.

- **The crew made a mutual kill pact.** If any of them begin turning into something like Nefarius, the others are obligated to put them down. Asha initiated; Rho seconded immediately.

- **Next destination: Shaya** for a life catalyst. Kaelith is exiled from his homeworld. Terrans are unwelcome. The crew is banking on Voria's name carrying weight. This is going to be politically volatile.

### Items & Lore
- **Golden crystal residue** from Nefarius purge — sealed in Kazon's glass jar; needs proper containment and analysis by an artificer
- **Sargoth** is now purified (dream + void), iridescent, alive but still subdued; something is "critically missing" — void wants to become something, dream wants to emerge, neither can cross the gap
- **Spark revelation**: Ternus's classified fuel resembles extracted magic crystals; Seraphine wants to investigate old contacts in New Texas
- **Catalyzation lore**: Rarely exceeds single digits; many ascend via guardian bonding or god-binding (e.g., Nebiat → Frit → Krox chain)

### Threads to Track
1. **Spark investigation** — What are the Inurans doing? Seraphine's contacts in New Texas are the lead.
2. **Shaya homecoming** — Kaelith is exiled; the crew includes Terrans. Political minefield.
3. **Sargoth's incomplete transformation** — The armor needs something to bridge void and dream. Worth exploring.
4. **Trade moon at Ternus** — 2-week window; ship outfitting, gear upgrades, Asha wants to catalyze the battleship itself.
5. **Asha's addiction risk** — The armor purge felt *too good*. She pulled back this time. Keep an eye on this.
6. **Spirit magic tension** — Lysa and Kaelith want it; Seraphine drew a hard ethical line on soul-binding. This debate isn't resolved.

### Session Tone
Reflective and character-driven. No combat. Heavy on training montage, crew bonding, ethical debates, and forward planning. Players made strong RP choices — Asha's kill-pact declaration, the Spark connection, and the Seraphine vs. Lysa/Kaelith spirit magic argument were standout moments. 5 XP awarded.
