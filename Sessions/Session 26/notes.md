Narrator:The Lone Star hangs beside the Skull of Xal, its rebuilt systems humming quietly beneath the bridge. Violet light spills across the windows and the clean edges of consoles that still look too expensive to belong to you. The crew remains gathered around them. Seraphine is leaning forward in the captain's chair, Tamsin has stopped working on her rifle, and Nyrix is watching the conversation with obvious interest.

There is finally enough work to go around. Seraphine and Tamsin have Ternus to investigate. Rho has proposed taking Smoke and a platoon into Saezo's operation as hired protection. Lysa is waiting to learn whether Nara will let her anywhere near Necrotis' remains. Nobody has actually left yet, but the bridge is already beginning to feel like a place people are preparing to leave.

Yraina, meanwhile, is still staring at Asha. She offered to steal a memory-imprinting device, wipe your memories, and walk you into Maena as slaves. Asha agreed, which seems to have surprised her more than an argument would have. There is still a device to steal and a great deal resting on the promise that she can put you back afterward.

Asha's last question is still waiting for an answer: who would be going with her?
