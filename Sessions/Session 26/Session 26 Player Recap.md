Saezo left you beneath the Trade Moon with his pumps drinking deep and questions multiplying behind him. Kaelith collected Axiom, the Eldimagus staff built to grow from focus to apprentice to equal, while the Lone Star emerged from its refit rebuilt in Inuran metamaterial and ready for a single catalyzation of its own. Governor Rourke wanted the ancient disk back; Asha offered him amnesty instead. Then Xal Aran carried you to the Skull, took the disk into the Flame's keeping, and left Asha and Kaelith to confront Nara about the catalyst map that began all of this.

Nara admitted the map had been planted for you. The Web of Divinity showed almost every other path ending with your deaths on the Trade Moon, followed by every human soul on Ternus being dragged into the moon to birth something that devoured all future possibility. Yet this path broke away: the freighter reached the dock, exploded, and bought the universe time. After Axiom received Ikadra's blessing and took Xal as his first Catalyst, you followed Nara into the Flame of Knowledge—past its silent berths, impossible archives, and the gathering Swarm—to ask the Web what had changed.

Now you stand on the Flame's bridge above a well of white fire, beneath a Web that shows every choice still waiting to be made. Kek, its exhausted guardian, has given the shape of the answer. Maena arranged the freighter and seeks the birth of a god. The Hand of Eterra planted a weapon meant to corrupt the Spark supply before it reached the Trade Moon. Your interference made Saezo hesitate long enough for that weapon to find its target, but Maena remains tied to Spark's production and to something being mined beyond the Web, deep in the Umbral. Stopping Maena or stopping Spark may close the hole—but Kek's last warning still hangs over the bridge: Saezo must be kept calm. Even with the Hand's weapon in place, Ternus can still be lost if he goes through with his plan.
