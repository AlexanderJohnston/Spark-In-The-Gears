# Session 26 Scratchpad

**The crew has chosen its campaign. The next work is giving that choice something tangible to bite into.**

Session 26 moved Asha and Kaelith out of the Flame, established several crew assignments, and ended with Asha accepting Yraina's memory-wipe infiltration proposal in principle. The next session should develop that commitment into local action.

Forward development is in [[Session 27 Design]]. The ready-to-read [[Sessions/Session 26/notes|Narrator opening]] resumes the bridge conversation.

## Exact position at the cutoff

Asha and Kaelith are back on the **Lone Star's bridge in the Xal system**, with their crew. They have left both the Flame and the Skull's gathering. Nobody has departed on the proposed assignments in the recorded play.

Yraina has proposed:

- Steal a memory-imprinting device from her old Matriarch.
- Make imprints before wiping the infiltrators.
- Present the genuinely wiped people to Maena as slaves.
- Restore their memories once inside.

Asha's last substantive decision is: **“I'm good with it. Let's do a soft-death. Who would be going with?”** Yraina is surprised she agreed. That question is still unanswered.

**No device has been acquired, no memories have been imprinted or wiped, and no destination or infiltration roster has been finalized.** Kaelith has investigated possible alternatives but has not individually agreed to undergo a wipe.

The log's last few lines are OOC greetings and a website question. They add no new fictional events.

## Resources that now matter

- **The Lone Star:** refitted in Inuran metamaterial, with improved drives, armor, command systems, sensors, and communications. Its single catalyzation remains unused.
- **Axiom:** one completed catalyzation, Xal/Void; deliberate but pre-verbal responses and a demonstrated sense for hidden space, absence, and displacement. Aashitak/Dream is next. The eight-part construction does not make every proposed diagnostic power active.
- **Marine equipment:** Saezo's Mark IX armor, weapons, and substantial battle supplies have arrived. Detailed distribution remains unrecorded. Their quality should make the delegated protection offer credible.
- **Revival brew:** Voria had a barrel delivered to the ship and taken into the sergeant's keeping. She estimates perhaps a couple hundred brews if handled carefully. It works on someone who has just died and is mostly intact. That is an approximate supply with stated conditions, not an established memory-repair or soul-retrieval effect.
- **Drifter goodwill:** the barrel is a practical expression of the fanclub earned earlier. The crew's past actions are already helping them.
- **The Terra/Hand disk:** remains with Aran for protected storage associated with the Flame.
- **Saezo access:** a paid, completed job and a relationship with a generous patron who knows they dislike Maena. New service still needs to be arranged.
- **Rourke leverage:** the withheld freighter file and his interest in the disk remain live. His Session 24 call ended without a deal.

## What the players are giving us

### Asha: trust becomes an action

Her command performance is exuberant, specific, and affectionate. She wants capable people doing useful things and is willing to stop keeping everyone physically close to feel safe. Seraphine's delighted response makes this growth immediately reciprocal: her mother is proud to receive meaningful orders from her.

The memory proposal goes further. After objecting to Nara deciding her fate, Asha chooses to make her future depend on someone else. The distinction is that this time she is participating in the decision.

Her “soft-death” description is **her interpretation**, not a metaphysical ruling that she will die. Let the player decide whether the experience changes her. The design can give her reasons to trust, conditions to set, and people whose conduct matters without prescribing an identity crisis.

### Kaelith: preparation and participation

Kaelith's concern about confronting an ancient family is a request to prepare, not a refusal to act. He also chose to stay with the main effort rather than derail Axiom's order.

Give him something worth understanding in the immediate problem: whether memories are inaccessible or absent, what the imprint preserves, how Maena routes magical captives, or what a concealed system actually connects to. His magical competence should create options.

His own family rejected his identity; he is now helping Yraina approach hers. He is also raising a potential person while his enemies treat persons as equipment. Both are available resonances, not required conversations or conclusions.

### Yraina: competence with a personal price

Session 22 is essential supporting context. A Ternan file identifies her mother as **Kyrsa Maena**, matriarch of a major manufacturer. Yraina says she has been estranged for over half her life and spent fifteen years on the Trade Moon's surface waiting for a decent opportunity. She admits she was willing to lie if helping the crew would let her see Maena burn. Asha accepted the renewed honesty.

Session 26 pays that relationship forward: Yraina offers a route based on methods the crew finds disturbing, and is startled when Asha accepts.

The productive uncertainty is **what returning costs Yraina and what habits she brings with her**. There is ample tension without deciding she is secretly betraying the crew. Her estrangement also gives her knowledge a natural limit: she knows people, customs, and systems she once encountered; current access still has to be established.

The Drifter Rock incident is unresolved personal background. Its proximity to Voria's gift does not establish a connection.

### Nara: an ally who wants room to be a person

Asha's invitation to be “just Nara” visibly relaxes her. Their shared catalyst-hunting identity matters. She now has a reason to care about this crew beyond preserving a favorable branch of the Web.

Her reference profile also connects memory loss to her own history. If she learns the infiltration plan, that can inform a concise, personal response. No such briefing has happened yet. She need not replace Yraina as the expert or take over the operation.

Kek's request for secrecy was met with affectionate evasion rather than further interrogation. Preserve that relationship and the exit the players chose.

The operator's identity, current form, precise access, and contingencies are not fixed. Neither are the dump's timing, interruptible components, or Maena's missing research dependency. [[Session 27 Design]] supplies options where the local adventure can expose part of this.

## Continuity decisions worth keeping visible

- **Which Matriarch?** Session 22 establishes Kyrsa as Yraina's mother and a manufacturing matriarch. Session 24 also mentions the Inuran Matriarch. Session 26 says “my old Matriarch.” Using Kyrsa as the device owner is a strong working inference, not proof that all three titles denote the ruler of Inura.
- **Memory claims:** Yraina says memories become inaccessible and that a real wipe is behaviorally recognizable. She only speculates that the device was made for torture. The device's actual purpose, safeguards, and limits have not been demonstrated.
- **Recovery claims:** Seraphine's assessment of short versus extended wipes is an NPC assessment. Neither guaranteed restoration nor a mandatory traumatic outcome is established.
- **Necrotis access:** the Maker's Wrath is secured in Xal space. Lysa's request remains unanswered. Studying its contents and catalyzing Axiom there are separate matters.
- **Politics:** Nara warns that Shaya and Krox could join Virkon against Ternus and Inura. That is a feared alignment, not a declared war.
- **Naming:** use **Hand of Eterra** in new notes. “Errata” occurs in dialogue and recap text.
- **Recap age:** the existing “Session 26 Player Recap” recaps the lead-in from Sessions 24–25. It is not an account of completed Session 26.
- **Older proposals:** the wrong-activator explanation in Session 25 prep, Axiom's broad dormant ability list, and the Flame guide's default location do not override the later logs.

## Direction carried into the next session

**A local operation against the machinery of ownership, backed by a crew learning to act beyond its captain's immediate reach.**

The memory device is valuable because it could make the chosen infiltration possible. It also offers leverage over an institution that controls who gets to remember. That gives the players several uses for the same material: theft, bargaining, study, liberation, concealment, or a different entry plan.

Keep a specific Maena objective behind the infiltration. “Get inside” becomes useful when the crew knows what access can buy: a labor destination, an authorization chain, a hidden dependency, or evidence of who can overrule the Matriarch.

The desired gain is something the crew can use. Its scale can be modest: possession of a working device, an insider relationship, an authenticated destination, or the first evidence of divided authority. The session need not contain both the theft and the infiltration.
