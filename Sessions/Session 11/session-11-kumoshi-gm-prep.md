## 2) Table-ready clocks
Use these as hard pacing tools; announce them openly.

### Clock: **Gale Awakens (6)**
Fills when they make noise/bright magic/violent harvest.
- At **3**: hunters begin active pursuit.
- At **6**: Breathkeeper manifests fully (scene escalates).

**Common +ticks**
- Explosives/breaching charges **inside** coil: +2
- Harvesting core-adjacent tissue near node: +2
- Killing “maintenance/herd” primals: +1
- Repeated high-signature casting in confined spaces: +1
- Calder’s drone filming threshold chamber: +1 (treated as theft)

### Clock: **Air Budget (8)**
Abstracts time/air/heat.
- Each major interior scene: tick 1–2.
- At **≤2**: +1 DT to physical actions from cold stress/hypoxia.
- At **0**: forced evac or DT+2 to all actions until stabilized.

### Clock: **Hull/Seal Compromise (4)**
Ticks on decompression events / suit failures.
- At **2**: frost-lock begins (doors stiff, seals brittle).
- At **4**: someone gets spaced / gear freezes / catastrophic breach (hard consequence).

### Clock: **Footage Leak Risk (4)**
Ticks when Calder records restricted visuals, transmits, or third parties intercept.
- At **4**: immediate reaction scene triggers (Voss/Rourke/Confed media response).

## 3) Mechanical baselines (keep consistent)
- DT ladder: **DT1 simple / DT2 moderate / DT3 hard / DT4 extreme / DT5 absurd**.
- Edge: **+2 automatic successes (non-stacking)**.
- Space scan ladder: DT1 ID/basic; DT2 velocity/possible trajectory; DT3 likely destinations; DT4+ technical details.
- Combat: **2 actions/round**; hit if successes ≥ defense; extra successes add damage; Skepticism is the defense for spell/magical hazards.

## 4) What Kumoshi is (table picture)
- A **frozen, drifting rock**: cratered, slow tumble, starless-black ice.
- Wrapped in **ancient air-wyrm remains** like a spiral skeleton reef: rib-coils, cartilage tunnels, lung-sacs flash-frozen into translucent ice.
- “Air in vacuum” vibe: pressure gradients and storm-front behavior **without** atmosphere—wind becomes *directed force* (magic + physics).

### What “10/100” means (concrete)
- God-fragment infrastructure: it can **test**, **punish**, and **permit**.
- Expect **1–2 serious fights max** + **one major threshold negotiation/test** that can become a fight.
- It won’t auto-delete them like a 25; it will **escalate if they act like butchers**.

## 5) Approach & navigation (space-side set pieces)
### Sensor deception (the ‘almost right’ problem)
- Safe pockets appear on sensors, then shear apart.
- Trajectories drift 0.2–0.5% over minutes—enough to scrape coil ice.

### Actions & DTs (use these as prompts)

   - DT2: safe approach vector
   - DT3: identify active storm lanes + dormant vents
   - Fail: pick wrong lane; +1 Gale Awakens *or* +1 Hull/Seal Compromise (your choice)

2) **Tight threading** (close fast)
   - Piloting DT3
   - Fail: +1 Gale Awakens and +1 Hull/Seal Compromise

3) **Anchor the ship** (safe standoff)
   - Engineering DT2
   - Success: grant **Edge** to the first EVA-related roll (non-stacking)

4) **Silent running** (reduce signature)
   - Engineering DT2
   - Success: next time you would tick Gale Awakens from ship signature, ignore it once

## 6) Insertion routes (present as meaningful tradeoffs)
### Route 1: Surface carve-in
- Pros: predictable geometry, fewer decompression traps.
- Cons: loud (tools/heat), slow, easy to track.
- Entry challenge: Engineering/Artifice DT2 to cut without microfracture (fail: +1 Gale Awakens).

### Route 2: Lung-vent breach
- Pros: fastest to interior.
- Cons: pressure traps + decompression.
- Entry challenge: Athletics DT2 reaction if vent “coughs” them (fail: +1 Hull/Seal Compromise).

### Route 3: Rib-gap drift (EVA threading)
- Pros: stealthy if executed well.
- Cons: wind shear corridors; one mistake = pinball.
- Entry challenge: Athletics DT2 each zone; fail: prone + slide into hazard.

## 7) Interior hazards (run as zones; each zone = 1–2 clock ticks)
### Hazard A: Cold burn + ice accretion
- Each set piece: tick **Air Budget**.
- At Air Budget ≤2: +1 DT physical actions.

### Hazard B: Pressure traps (lung-sacs)

- Fail: tick **Hull/Seal Compromise**.

# BOXED TEXT: Exiting the Umbral Depths into Kumoshi’s shadow
(Use immediately after the current bridge dialogue; then ask for scan/approach decisions.)

Narrator:
The Umbral Depths let go like a bad dream breaking. No warning. No ramp. One second the stars are wrong and muted like they're under water, the next the real sky snaps back in sharp enough to cut.

The spell-drive steps down and the whole battleship gives you that low metallic shudder like it just shook something off its back. Frost flashes across the viewport seals in thin veins. Not because space is cold. Because the pressure outside is lying.

The bridge lights brighten. Then dim again. The ship decides it would rather not be seen.

Outside is **shadow**.

Not the clean absence of light you expect in deep space. This is a bruise wrapped around a world. **Kumoshi** turns slow and cratered ahead of you, and its nightside eats starlight in a way your eyes keep trying to interpret as motion.

And around it—wrapped around the whole sphere like a spiral crown—lies the **coil**.

It's a skeleton reef with rib arcs the size of cities. Cartilage tunnels knotted tight. Lung-sacs flash-frozen into translucent ice that catches distant light and throws it back as thin, sick aurora. Between those ribs, the void behaves like weather. Frost-dust and glittering debris stream sideways as if something is pushing it.

Your sensors start speaking in contradictions. Safe pockets blink into shear. Calm lanes rotate half a degree while you watch. Trajectory math comes out correct and still feels wrong.

Somewhere in that spiral, something exhales.

Not air—force. A directed pressure ripple that runs across ice and bone. The coil doesn't make sound in vacuum, but you feel it through the hull like a hand testing a door seal.

Rho, you feel it in your teeth.
Tamsin, your skin prickles like you just walked into a room where an argument ended five seconds ago.
Lysa, your warding tightens of its own accord.
Kaelith, the rings feel *eager*—like they’re waiting to drink a new kind of storm.

Asha—Captain—Kumoshi is right there, and for the first time since Virkon, the ship feels small.
