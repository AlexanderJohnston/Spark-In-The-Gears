# Maena Raid Continuation

## Start here

**Open in the household consultation room. Asha and Kaelith are still wiped. Yraina has their imprints and the permitted restoration kit. They have been left alone, with household review expected in fifteen minutes.**

This session turns their successful entry into answers: **what is Maena building, and why does it need Spark?** Restore their opportunity to act, let them choose an internal destination, reveal the manufactured-god project and Maena's current interest in Terra/Sol, then resolve extraction with whatever they choose to preserve.

**Shortest useful route:** restoration → Reserve Compatibility laboratory → extraction. Household review can instead lead to the director and the same central revelations. Allocation is a useful connecting scene, not a compulsory stop. Research sabotage, freeing workers, and obstructing the takeover of Saezo's infrastructure are substantial additional gains.

## First five minutes at the table

Recap the room, the wafers, and the expected review. Do not replay the approach or intake.

**Optional opening:**

> The consultation room is quiet. Three chairs face the wash station, and the door-status panel glows beside the household call button. Yraina sets down the case and opens it. The two wafers are still inside.
>
> “We made it. These are yours. Same device, right imprint, and you get yourselves back.”

Let each player accept restoration, question Yraina, or refuse. She explains plainly; she does not dictate their emotions or claim ownership of them.

1. **Restore willing PCs.** Retain the earlier guide's working procedure: correct wafer, powered kit and handset, and one uninterrupted minute per person. Routine correct use needs no failure roll. Interruption pauses the process without randomly corrupting a mind.
2. **Allow a brief reaction.** Retained preparation ruling: pre-wipe memories return alongside memories of the interval. Let the players decide how that feels. Neither procedure installs obedience.
3. **Return initiative.** Yraina reminds them: “Reserve Compatibility, or the director's records. We need to know what they're building.” She knows the directory and household procedure, not the hidden answers.
4. **Recover equipment.** After restoration, Kaelith can recover Axiom from its Void pocket. Resolve other retrieval using established reach, capacity, and the actual gear aboard. The room has no general anti-Void ward. Object retrieval does not automatically permit whole-party teleportation.
5. **Offer the check-in.** Yraina can tell Nyrix they are restored and agree on the next report or extraction signal. Record whether contact succeeds.

If a PC refuses, keep that player involved through conversation, observation, or a chosen withdrawal. The other can restore independently. If play makes the kit inaccessible, Nyrix's duplicate imprints and the shipboard recovery arrangement remain alternatives; reaching the subject is the practical problem.

**Setting:** private room, logged door, household corridor, visible service signage.

Neris wants a responsible decision about Yraina's personal-servant claim. She knows procedure and can refer matters to Delven; she does not know the god-making project.

## Scene 2 — Allocation: a useful shortcut

**Goal:** make the laboratory, protected authority, and Terra/Sol interest concrete.  
**Cast:** Teral Iss, two administrative workers, and only guards actually assigned here.  
**Setting:** schedules, locked imprint drawers, return appointments, and a glazed laboratory service passage.

Teral is repeatedly restored for technical assignments. He wants his retained work imprint and cancellation of his placement. Ordinary schedules require no bargain; active assistance against his employer asks more.

**Available records:**

- Specialists manufacture neural-lattice sections for a single larger assembly. The manifests name no living implant recipient.
- Delven suspended transfers; Kyrsa's approval reinstated them and protected Vesk's demands from ordinary review.
- A current request reads **SOL / TERRA — HUMAN ORIGIN POWER STUDY** and requests a lattice-capacity model for the liaison.
- The current comparison results are due to Vesk; his courier booking names **Survey Exchange Nine**.

Teral knows staffing, destinations, and service routes. He does not know the complete divine objective, Sol's power source, or Kyrsa's binding.

If the crew credibly helps him retain his imprint and leave, he can issue a technical escort and open laboratory service access. Neris or Delven can release his record; the party can also obtain it directly. His record is not the complete research archive. One coworker prefers to stay; another wants copies of their own records.

If Teral refuses or is bypassed, credentials, the controller, force, and the visible directory remain usable. His gratitude or survival is not the key to continuing.

## Scene 3 — Reserve Compatibility: the main discovery

**Goal:** establish the manufactured-god objective and Spark's purpose, then offer a real intervention.  
**Cast:** Meret Daal, three technicians, and two research guards unless relocated.  
**Setting:** cooled chamber, inert lattice sections, shielded Spark sample cups, comparison assembly, recording cartridge, shutdown controls, wheeled cradle, and service exit.

Introduce shutdown controls and sample shields before violence makes them important. Meret first tries to stop an unsafe test and protect her staff. She is proud of the work and knows its intended end use.

### Give the answer plainly

Readable plans, the animation protocol, and Meret independently establish:

1. **Maena manufactures a neural lattice as the substrate of a new being.** No mortal is waiting to receive it.
2. **Spark is intended to bring the hardware to life.** Interference in the accumulated reserve prevents the desired animation.
3. **The intended result is an original magitech god.** Existing gods began as living mortal mages; this would be an unprecedented origin.
4. **Success and control are unproven.** The team has not animated a god, solved the taint, or demonstrated obedience.

The samples still support ordinary industrial uses but fail this process. Records do not name the Hand; let the PCs make that connection.

Meret does not know the predicted consumption of Ternus or Kyrsa's binding. Evidence of wider harm can change her cooperation. She can explain safe shutdown while still defending her work.

### Put Terra and Sol in view

A pane reads **SOL / TERRA — ORIGINAL HUMAN HOMEWORLD**. Its current planning note identifies extraordinary power associated with humanity's origin and asks how much a manufactured substrate could sustain. Vesk supplied the reference; executive records hold its source assessment.

Ordinary examination reveals it. This is current Maena research, not a forgotten ancient label. It supplies no route through the seal, full explanation of Sol's apparent ending, or identification and cosmic ranking of the source.

### Make the intervention tangible

| Action | Working time | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read or photograph exposed plans | As the method permits | Learn and preserve the central answer without sabotage. |
| Copy full current results | Two minutes at the powered console | Gain data; Maena retains its local copy. |
| Shut down, then remove the cartridge | One minute, then one minute | Take the current full record, divine objective, and Sol reference; deny those current measurements to the operator. |
| Detach calibrated assembly | Five minutes with Meret or suitable technical work | Stop the experimental series; use the cradle and cargo route for removal. |
| Ruin exposed physical calibration | Resolve actual action | Stop the series. Merely breaking the outer housing causes repairable damage; explain the distinction before commitment. |
| Evacuate willing staff | Depends on their choices and route | Preserve people and expertise without compulsory recruitment. |

**Fixed payoff:** losing the physical calibration requires a replacement specialist team and at least two weeks of uninterrupted rebuilding and recalibration. Vesk's earlier remote summaries lack the current complete measurements and calibration. No perfect backup appears after success.

Ruptured samples can cause local hazards under ordinary rules. Shutdown closes their shutters. This is neither Saezo's reservoir nor the moon's deep engine; damaging the room does not automatically destroy the moon.

If warned, Vesk attempts to collect the cartridge while Oren's people protect staff and access. Track their approach. If Vesk secures the record, the assembly remains a target and information already learned remains known.

**Successful stopping point:** the PCs understand the project and retain convincing evidence. They can extract now.

## Scene 4 — Executive records: ambition, takeover, and a lead

**Goal:** provide an independent route to the main answer and actionable evidence of the wider operation.  
**Cast:** Director Delven Sarr, Vesk Aruun unless moved, two executive guards; Oren only if actually present.  
**Setting:** executive desk, correspondence terminal, laboratory/loading status display, and service exit.

**Delven** wants his office intact and the family scandal contained. He knows the promised god-making outcome and benefits from coerced labor. He resents signing for losses caused by Vesk's protected orders. Under credible pressure he offers safe passage and a narrow equipment loss recorded as an internal dispute in return for silence. The PCs can reject, improve, or exploit the offer.

**Vesk** wants current results delivered and the protected channel concealed. He invokes Kyrsa, demands that Delven secure the laboratory, and attempts to leave with his packet or cartridge if threatened. He is a trained agent with a sidearm and credentials, not a surprise boss mage. He must physically reach an exit.

Maena expects influence through manufacture, resources, and upbringing; animation and obedience remain undemonstrated. | Secure the main answer even without visiting the laboratory. |

The full basis is absent. | Preserve the assessment and follow its distribution address. No route to Terra is supplied. |

| **Protected correspondence and courier packet** | The Custodian directs work outside Delven's chain. Kyrsa reversed an endorsed suspension after private consultation through this channel. | Follow an offsite lead without diagnosing a binding from paperwork. |

**Takeover intervention:** seize the original packet and local execution seal, require Delven to register withdrawal, disable the dedicated execution channel, or send authenticated contradictions to pending signatories and pump-house management. The seal is a corporate device, not the soul repository.

Taking the seal blocks the current prepared ratification pending replacement authority and resubmission; copying the agreement alone does not. Outside copies still exist. A physical takeover would still require operators and access.

### The usable next destination

**Survey Exchange Nine** is an offsite relay and transfer station reached through a filed Umbral transit approach. **Dock Warden Senn** receives sealed research transfers. The packet provides approach instructions and the next scheduled delivery. Set the exact delivery time when needed and record it consistently.

The laboratory shipping console holds the same booking and approach information. Delven's cache duplicates Vesk's correspondence; Teral supplies the relay's name. One dead or uncooperative NPC does not erase the lead.

**GM only:** the Custodian is the ancient Necrotis follower. Exchange Nine is its relay, not a guaranteed location for its body, Kyrsa, or the mines. Vesk knows its practical authority, not the binding formula. Kyrsa's reversal supports investigating coercion, not proving its mechanism.

## Scene 5 — Extraction and a breath afterward

**Goal:** resolve what leaves and let the gains endure.  
**Cast:** the trio, chosen evacuees, actual remaining opposition, and communicated ship support.  
**Setting:** intake or loading court, then the nearby Lone Star.

- **Intake:** household release, negotiation, deception, or force can reopen the route. Retain the logged one-group vault cycle when relevant.
- **Loading court:** a utility carrier accommodates the cradle and passengers. Keys and dispatch control are at the loading desk; negotiation, coercion, technical access, or force can secure them.
- **Void:** use established reach and carrying capacity. A valid magical exit works; object retrieval alone does not establish mass evacuation.
- **Nyrix:** follows a communicated plan with the crew actually aboard. Bell cares for the Educator woman. Detached personnel do not reappear as reinforcements.

Oren prioritizes surviving staff and recoverable assets. He can end pursuit when the site is lost or Delven orders disengagement. Vesk has no guaranteed escape. Outside responders require the process below.

Aboard, allow the PCs to react to the wipe, the research, or their treatment of the Educator woman. Bell's care has not restored her identity. Do not prescribe remorse, forgiveness, or resolution.

Offer one relevant divided-crew report if elapsed time supports it. Rho and Smoke can use takeover evidence if their access is established; Seraphine and Tamsin can investigate procurement; Lysa's supervised work can help compare Spirit evidence. No completed investigation, accepted Saezo assignment, or binding cure is assumed.

## Adjudication and pacing

Use [[Rules/02-basic-mechanics-and-concepts|Basic Mechanics]]. Correct routine kit use and reading accessible documents need no roll. DT 2 suits a moderately protected controller or hurried copying; DT 3 suits contested authorization, technical removal under threat, or persuading an official to take substantial personal risk. Combat uses normal defenses and initiative.

Failures change something specific: time, a witnessed discrepancy, an alerted guard, or damaged equipment. Avoid withholding every route to the central answer after one failure. Use existing NPC profiles; the roster specifies roles and numbers rather than new combat statistics.

**For roughly three hours:** allow 15–25 minutes for restoration and decisions, 20–35 for internal access, 45–60 for the primary discovery/intervention, and 25–40 for extraction and reactions. Executive records can replace the laboratory or extend play. If time is short, compress allocation and deliver essential findings at whichever main target the players reach.

### Keep these truths separate

- Kyrsa is Yraina's mother, secretly bound, and absent. The Custodian is also offsite.
- Yraina's failed soul-linked authority remains unexplained. Household referral does not repair or explain it.
- Memory machinery, soul authorization, the Hand's taint, and Kyrsa's bespoke binding are different mechanisms.
- Terra/Sol's extraordinary source is the universe's most powerful Catalyst in GM truth; this raid does not reveal that full answer.
- Local sabotage does not trigger Saezo's engine, cure Kyrsa, or defeat the entire program.
- Success permanently changes the assets actually taken or destroyed.

A successful session can end with convincing answers and a usable lead. Additional sabotage has the stated impact; a partial extraction preserves whatever the party actually earned.
