# Session 22 Scratchpad

## Session thesis

Session 22 should turn suspicion into leverage. The crew now has enough to say Maena touched the freighter before it ever reached Ternus, but not enough to prove what they did, why they did it, or how Joren became the trigger. Keep this session investigative, grounded, and choice-driven: evidence custody, dead testimony, ship forensics, and political pressure should all matter.

Do not collapse the answer into "Maena did it" and call it done. Maena is the modern hand on the knife. The knife itself is older, stranger, and tied to Kemet, Eterra, Zenzuata, and the catastrophic system sleeping inside the freighter.

## Start state from Session 21

- The crew is in the officers' section of the old Inuran freighter after regrouping off the bridge.
- Seraphine already recovered the auxiliary data core from the bridge and still has it.
- Asha found a captain's-console retrofit with two controls labeled **Absorb** and **Release**.
- Seraphine identified that retrofit as the same kind of Nefarius-blood anti-magic war weapon Ternus once used in desperation.
- Asha's armor confirmed the hull still carries Nefarius's blood and could likely devour it later if Asha chooses.
- Kaelith's Essence Sight in the cargo bays found layered magical residue, evidence of careful handling, and signs that important cargo once sat there and was removed in a hurry.
- Kaelith's flame read of the cargo bay showed blue-and-white Inurans bringing the party's armor aboard before spirits slaughtered the crew and the ship was put on autopilot.
- Asha received **+1 EXP** during the session after re-evaluating her own magical understanding.
- Kaelith's flame read of the captain's quarters showed a masked spirit binder controlling the captain before the freighter ever left Inuran port.
- Yraina identified the described uniforms and spirit-magic angle as strongly suggestive of **Maena**, an Inuran family that embraces spirit magic and has produced spell armor before.
- Lysa reluctantly flame read the trace, confirmed the binder masked himself well, and proposed a more aggressive next step: if the crew can get **Joren's body**, and if Joren and the Inuran captain have not fully moved on, she may be able to get them to speak.
- Immediate unresolved leads: the aux core, bridge fragment search, the rest of the ship, dock workers, Pell Vask, Ternus archives, and Joren's body in Ternus custody.

## Opening beat - officers' corridor after Lysa's offer

### Read-aloud

Narrator:
The corridor goes still after Lysa says it plainly.

Get me the body.

Dust hangs in the old ship-light. Dead terminals sit in open doorways. The stale air of the officer's block carries just enough metal and old cooking wards to remind you that people once lived here before this ship became evidence.

Somewhere behind you, the bridge still holds Joren's outline.
Somewhere ahead of you, the rest of the freighter is still waiting to be read.
And now the dead have become a practical lead.

### Immediate prompt

Ask what they do first:

1. **Go after Joren's body** through Pell, Seraphine's channels, Saezo, or a less legal route.
2. **Examine the aux core** before leaving the dock.
3. **Finish searching the freighter**: bridge fragment, armory, crew quarters, engineering, hidden medical locker.
4. **Question Pell Vask or dock workers** about cleanup crews, evidence custody, and Maena.
5. **Contact Saezo, Bell, Corvin, or Seraphine's Ternus contacts** for help.
6. **Go to Ternus archives** before evidence vanishes behind paperwork.

## Important GM frame

- Reward caution and evidence handling. The players are no longer just exploring; they are deciding who gets to own the truth.
- Spirit testimony should be powerful but incomplete. Let the dead answer emotional and procedural questions, not the entire setting mystery.
- Seraphine, Yraina, Saezo, and Ternus should each feel useful and compromising in different ways.
- Keep the ancient layer separate from the Maena layer. One explains motive and method in the present; the other explains why the weapon exists at all.
- Failures should still advance the investigation. The cost should be Heat, bureaucracy, magical backlash, attention, or lost cleanliness, not dead ends.

## Pressure structure

### Heat clock - Secured Ternus dock

Start at **0 / 6**.

Increase Heat by 1 when:

- They cast loud magic without containment.
- They mention Maena on open comms.
- They force old doors, clamps, or sealed evidence storage.
- They route the aux core through public systems.
- They push Pell too hard.
- They ask too openly about Joren's corpse or chain of custody.

At **2 Heat**, Pell checks in and starts shadowing them more closely.

At **4 Heat**, Ternus security wants logs, names, and a formal explanation.

At **5 Heat**, a Maena-linked legal-medical office flags the investigation as "biohazard neural evidence."

At **6 Heat**, lockdown, injunction, or a scrub team arrives.

### Custody clock - Joren evidence trail

Start at **0 / 4** once they actively start hunting the body.

At **1**, they learn where Ternus logged the remains.

At **2**, they hit chain-of-custody rules, medical holds, or military paperwork.

At **3**, someone notices outside interest and starts tightening access.

At **4**, the body is moved, heavily restricted, or partially redacted by someone with more pull than Pell.

## Main investigation structure

Run Session 22 as a hub. Any two or three successful branches should be enough to carry them into the next major confrontation.

## Scene 1 - Secure the bridge evidence

### Purpose

Give the crew something physical they can hide, show, or lose. Reinforce that the bridge was a trigger site, not the origin of the whole system.

### Read-aloud

Narrator:
The bridge looks worse now that you know what to look for.

The captain's console is all old Inuran practicality except for the ugly little truth bolted onto it later. **Absorb. Release.** The labels are blunt in the way wartime language gets blunt when people no longer have the energy to lie to themselves.

Under the restraint clamps and ring housing, the deck still remembers the moment Joren died.

### Roll options

- Systems or Engineering DT 4 to interpret the captain's retrofit.
- Investigation DT 3 to search the matrix housing and clamp bases carefully.
- Perception DT 4 to find hidden physical residue or fragments.
- Medicine, Cybernetics, or Engineering DT 4 to identify non-ship hardware once found.

### Results

**Retrofit interpretation**

- **Success:** the bridge system was built to draw in ambient or targeted magic, then redirect or discharge it through the ship.
- **2+ net:** the retrofit is older than Joren's death but saw later maintenance or adaptation.
- **3+ net:** the **Release** side expects a secondary interpreter, consistent with a neural or implant-assisted trigger.

**Physical evidence**

- **Success:** recover a pale ceramic-and-silver implant shard fused into purple-black glass under the matrix housing.
- **2+ net:** it acted as a casting bridge for a non-mage, low-skill operator, or forced biological conduit.
- **3+ net:** the material quality is high-end Inuran medical manufacture, not dockside black market work.
- **4+ net:** the cadence matches the Maena-linked restriction Yraina saw on Kaelith's staff sourcing.

### GM note

This shard is one of the cleanest hard proofs in the session. If they get it, everyone important should want it for different reasons.

## Scene 2 - Finish the freighter sweep

### Purpose

Let the players complete the ship search they clearly wanted. Make the freighter feel like a layered historical weapon, not a one-room clue machine.

Offer unresolved zones:

### Zone A - Armory

**Rolls:** Perception DT 3, Engineering DT 4, History & Lore DT 4.

Clues:

- Weapon racks were stripped long before Ternus investigators arrived.
- One rack still carries residue from non-standard spell ammunition or anti-spirit loadout.
- The armory was prepared for boarding actions against something magical, not ordinary soldiers.

Tamsin:
"This is not anti-pirate kit. This is 'we expect the hallway to become a religious problem' kit."

### Zone B - Crew quarters

**Rolls:** Investigation DT 3, Perception DT 4, Empathy or Spirit work if they push beyond mundane evidence.

Clues:

- Crew left personal effects as if mobilized suddenly, not as if abandoning ship cleanly.
- Some bunks show old medical straps, restraint points, or neural-interface burn traces.
- A hidden recorder or note references the relief route turning into a coffin once the "special cargo" arrived.

Rho:
"I hate this kind of place. Too much grief left in drawers."

### Zone C - Engineering

**Rolls:** Engineering DT 4, Systems DT 4, Essence Sight DT 5.

Clues:

- The reactor was not what powered the dock blast.
- The bridge discharge drew almost no true ship power.
- Larger safety locks exist for a much bigger matrix event, but those locks never opened.
- The Ternus blast was a degraded handshake, leak, warning, or failed activation - not the real weapon.

Seraphine:
"That is the part I hate. It barely used the ship. If it had actually woken up, we would not be standing here discussing it."

### Zone D - Hidden medical locker

**Rolls:** Perception DT 5, Medicine or Cybernetics DT 4, Systems DT 4.

Clues:

- Empty slots once held interface kits or surgical modules.
- Old labels reference **Kemet emergency theater** use.
- A more recent inventory tag was peeled off.
- The kit format is consistent with cybernetic spell interfaces rather than normal care.

Yraina:
"That aesthetic is Maena's old sin: make invasive things look elegant enough that people call them miracles."

## Scene 3 - Aux core extraction

### Purpose

Reward Seraphine, anchor the technical route, and give them a usable bridge from old freighter evidence to present-day Maena activity.

### Roll options

- Systems + Reason DT 4 to read the core safely.
- Engineering DT 4 to build a clean interface.
- Investigation DT 4 to identify useful data clusters.
- Seraphine assistance grants +1 die.
- Bell or Corvin remote help reduces DT by 1 but adds Heat +1 if active comms are obvious.

### Results

**Success:** recover three damaged but usable clusters.

1. **Bridge handshake**
   - Manual contact at matrix.
   - Bio-auth mismatch.
   - Secondary interpreter present.
   - Interpreter source external / neural.
   - Fuel source Spark-compatible.
   - Result discharge below strategic threshold.

2. **War-route continuity**
   - Kemet relief theater.
   - Emergency interface kits.
   - Non-mage spell operation authorized.
   - Command override references like **ETR-NULL** and **ZEN-SEAL**.

3. **Recent access**
   - Record touched from a Trade Moon medical-commercial node.
   - Auth family Maena.
   - Purpose biohazard neural recovery.

**2+ net:** recover a manufacturer or project tag:

> MNA-CYB / KEMET CONTINUITY / SPARK-COMPATIBLE INTERFACE

**Failure:** they still get one cluster, but choose a cost:

- Heat +1 from visible unauthorized hardware access.
- False timestamp contamination.
- Dead-crew audio playback in the reader's own voice. Willpower DT 3 to shake off.

## Scene 4 - Joren's body / Ternus custody branch

### Purpose

Make evidence custody into gameplay. The question is not just "where is the body?" but "who is allowed to ask?"

### Possible routes

- **Pell Vask:** knows the chain of transfer and who signed.
- **Seraphine's Ternus channels:** fastest legal route, but creates official notice.
- **Saezo:** can open doors, but now he knows exactly how valuable the body is.
- **Stealth / forgery route:** risky, but keeps the request off some of the books.

### Roll options

- Persuasion, Authority, or Bureaucracy DT 4 for legal access.
- Seraphine support reduces DT by 1 but creates military notice.
- Saezo authorization reduces DT by 1 but gives him leverage over the result.
- Stealth, Forgery, or Systems DT 5 for an off-books approach.

### What they find

- Joren's body was logged as a high-sensitivity dock fatality, not normal wreckage.
- Ternus allowed outside specialists to remove "biohazard neural material" before final evidence lock.
- The autopsy trail is incomplete in exactly the way that means somebody important touched it.
- There is enough left for Lysa to attempt contact if they can physically reach the remains or an intimate token.

### GM note

Do not let this become a single yes/no permission gate. Every route should work if the players are willing to pay its price.

## Scene 5 - Lysa's spirit interview

### Purpose

Give Lysa a decisive spotlight and let the dead confirm method, guilt, and fear without solving the entire campaign.

### Setup

Lysa wants Joren's body. If the crew only gets partial access, let her work with the remains plus the location and the captain's lingering imprint from the ship.

### Roll

- Lysa: Spirit or Spellcasting DT 4.
- +1 die if Kaelith frames the magical residue first.
- +1 die if Asha anchors the ritual or keeps the scene emotionally steady.
- +1 die if they have the implant shard or aux-core handshake data as focus material.

### Joren - what he can say

Use fragments, not a perfect deposition:

- He did not understand the system he touched.
- Something in or on him acted first; touching the ring only completed the sentence.
- He remembers pain, cold instruction, and the sense that the ship already knew what to do.
- He may recall a surgical sensation, old scar, or wrongness that matters in hindsight.

### The captain - what he can say

If Lysa reaches the captain's echo through proximity:

- He was already compromised before departure.
- The special cargo came under emergency authority and spirit-hidden supervision.
- The ship's route and purpose were no longer fully his.
- Kemet continuity or emergency-interface language may surface.
- He may know the uniforms or house marks, but not the full ancient layer.

### Failure or cost

- The dead still confirm Joren was a trigger, not the author.
- Lysa takes a splitting headache and refuses another try until she rests.
- Heat +1 if the ritual disturbs dock sensors or nearby wards.
- Something in the ancient layer notices the ritual and answers only with image, not speech.

## Scene 6 - Pell Vask / dockside social branch

### Purpose

Keep the session grounded and textured. Not every answer should come from magic.

### Pell Vask

Private security supervisor. Exhausted, suspicious, and trying not to drown in paperwork.

Pell:
"Ekron authorization gets you through the gate. It does not make me enjoy hearing the phrase 'we need the corpse' in my dock."

### Roll options

- Persuasion or Authority DT 3.
- Intimidation DT 3 for quicker answers, but Heat +1.
- Yraina can smooth one conversation.
- Asha's reputation grants Edge if she is direct.

### Useful answers

Pell or dock workers can reveal:

1. A private cleanup crew arrived before official investigators.
2. They used Inuran trade or medical authority cover.
3. Workers remember pale cases, blue-white dress, and Maena-adjacent style.
4. They removed "biohazard neural material" from the bridge and related remains.
5. Some injured workers later received subsidized Maena implants.
6. The body trail leads to an evidence facility or hospital wing with unusual restrictions.

## Scene 7 - Ternus archives branch

### Purpose

Answer the biggest lore questions only if the crew chooses to chase them officially before returning to the Trade Moon.

### Entry options

- Bureaucracy or Persuasion DT 4.
- Saezo authorization reduces DT by 1, but his name lands in the record.
- Seraphine's authority reduces DT by 1, but Ternus military notice follows.
- Forgery or Systems DT 5; failure adds Heat +2.

### Research topics

Let them pick two or three:

#### Topic: Kemet

- Kemet's world and trade moon were destroyed after Nefarius-war events.
- Emergency non-mage spell-operation programs existed.
- The freighter was off standard route when catastrophe hit.

#### Topic: Eterra

- Appears in redacted records as nullification, mercy, or impossible containment.
- Associated with stopping world-devouring threats, not normal enemies.
- Recovered phrase:

> Eterra did not kill the hunger. She taught the wound how not to open.

#### Topic: Zenzuata

- Appears as seal, closure, or binding principle in catastrophic-event records.
- Often paired with Eterra before later overwrites.
- Recovered phrase:

> Zenzuata closes what Eterra spares.

#### Topic: Maena war contracts

- Maena supplied cybernetic interface kits under wartime emergency provisions.
- Those kits blurred mage and non-mage operational law.
- Some records were updated recently from a Trade Moon node.

#### Topic: The weak blast

- The Ternus dock event does not match the true output of the freighter's full system.
- It looks like a failed handshake, degraded test, or partial discharge.
- If the real system had fired, the target scale would have been much larger than a dock.

## Evidence tracker

### Soft evidence

- Blue-white / sterile-silver signature.
- Maena-like uniforms and spirit-magic method.
- Absorb / Release retrofit.
- Nefarius blood in the hull.
- Cargo-bay evidence of sacred or dangerous special cargo.

### Hard evidence

- Implant shard from the bridge.
- Aux-core handshake logs.
- Custody or autopsy paperwork showing removed neural material.
- Dead testimony from Joren and/or the captain.
- Archive proof of Maena interface-kit war contracts.

### Proof threshold

- **1 hard proof:** enough to justify deeper suspicion.
- **3 hard proofs:** enough to confront Saezo with confidence or take the lead to a serious ally.
- **5 hard proofs:** enough to challenge Maena's narrative directly, if the crew is ready for consequences.

## Possible end states

### End A - The dead speak

Lysa gets Joren and perhaps the captain to confirm that Joren was only a trigger and that a hidden interpreter or earlier manipulation mattered more than his intent.

### End B - Maena becomes undeniable

The aux core, body trail, or archive record makes Maena impossible to dismiss as a coincidence.

### End C - Ternus notices the body hunt

The crew gets what they need, but now security, Saezo, or Maena knows exactly how far they are digging.

### End D - Return to the Trade Moon with a live thread

They leave Ternus with enough proof to push the Maena clinic or Trade Moon node in Session 23.

## Open GM questions

- Does the crew try to keep Joren's body hunt quiet from Saezo, or decide he is too useful to exclude?
- Do they prioritize the dead, the aux core, or the archives first?
- Does Asha let the armor feed on Nefarius residue before they leave the freighter, and what does that attract?
- How much of the captain's testimony is still intact after decades and spiritual masking?
